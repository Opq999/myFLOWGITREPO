## What you'll get
You will get a custom Chrome new tab page that displays a feed of articles from DEV.to, a popular community for software developers. This extension also offers optional AI-generated summaries for articles, helping you quickly decide if an article is worth reading. This approach helps you discover new content without actively visiting the DEV.to website, integrating discovery into your daily browsing.

## Tools you need
*   **Google Chrome** (free): The web browser where you will install and use the extension.
*   **DEV New Tab** (free): A Chrome extension that replaces your new tab page with a DEV.to feed and adds AI summaries.
*   **DEV.to** (freemium): A community platform for software developers where articles are published.

## Steps

1.  **Install the DEV New Tab extension**: Open Google Chrome and go to the Chrome Web Store page for the "DEV New Tab" extension. Click the "Add to Chrome" button.
    You should see a confirmation pop-up asking if you want to add the extension. Click "Add extension." After installation, a new tab should automatically open, showing the DEV.to feed.
2.  **Browse DEV.to articles**: Open a new tab in Chrome.
    You should now see a feed of articles from DEV.to instead of your usual new tab page. You can scroll through different feeds like "For you," "Latest," or "Top."
3.  **View AI-generated overviews (optional)**: Click on any article card in the feed to open a preview.
    If your browser and device support Chrome's local AI features (which means the AI model runs directly on your device), you should see a short, AI-generated overview (summary) of the article at the top of the preview. This summary helps you quickly understand what the article is about. If local AI is not available, you will still see the article preview, but without the summary.
4.  **Open the full article**: In the article preview, click the link that says "Read on DEV.to" or similar.
    This will open the full article on the DEV.to website, where you can read it in detail, see comments, and view the author's profile.

## Original source
This workflow is based on a blog post by axrisi on DEV.to, where they describe building an unofficial Chrome extension called "DEV New Tab." The extension integrates DEV.to articles into Chrome's new tab page and uses Chrome's local Prompt API for AI-generated article summaries.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier viability**: The DEV New Tab extension is free to use. The AI summaries run locally on your device using Chrome's built-in AI capabilities, so they don't require any paid API keys or external services. This makes the entire workflow completely free.
*   **Common pitfall**: The AI summary feature relies on your browser, hardware, and language support for Chrome's local Prompt API. If you don't see summaries, it might be because your setup doesn't fully support this feature yet. You can still browse and read articles without the summaries.
*   **Tip for better results**: Sign in to DEV.to in your Chrome browser. The extension will use your existing DEV.to session to show personalized feeds (like "For you" and "Following") and your DEV.to reading list, making the new tab experience more relevant to your interests.
