## What you'll get
You'll get a concise, one-page discovery sheet for any small local business (like a tire shop, salon, or restaurant). This sheet helps you understand their current customer situation, identify online gaps, and suggest initial high-impact fixes before your first client meeting. It works by using an AI assistant to process key business details and generate actionable insights, saving you research time.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chat assistant used to generate the discovery sheet based on your input.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Paste the prompt**: Copy the full prompt below and paste it into the chat box. Press Enter or click the send button.

    ```
    Act as a small-business consultant preparing my first walk-in visit to a local shop. Ask me for (or let me paste) everything I know about the shop: what it sells, how long it has been open, how customers find it today (word of mouth, Google, referrals), whether it has a Google Business Profile with current hours, photos and reviews, whether the owner has been burned by ad agencies before, what monthly ad budget they would tolerate, the slowest season, and whether many customers speak Spanish. Then build a one-page discovery sheet, under 350 words, plain enough to hand the owner on paper, covering: how they get customers today and what it costs them, their online gaps ranked by what a customer actually sees, the two cheapest high-impact fixes, three icebreaker questions that don't sound like a pitch, two red flags to walk away, and a 60-second elevator line under 40 words.
    ```

    ChatGPT will likely respond by asking you to provide the details about the shop, or it might immediately generate the sheet if you paste the details along with the prompt.

3.  **Provide business details**: Based on the prompt's request, provide the information you know about the small business. For example, you might type:

    ```
    The shop sells car tires and repair services. It's been open for 15 years. Customers find them mostly through word of mouth and some Google searches. They have a Google Business Profile, but the hours might be outdated, and there aren't many recent photos or reviews. The owner has mentioned bad experiences with past ad agencies. They might tolerate a monthly ad budget of NGN 10,000-20,000. The slowest season is during the rainy months. Most customers speak English, but a few speak Yoruba.
    ```

    You should then receive a one-page discovery sheet from ChatGPT, summarizing the information and providing helpful insights.

4.  **Review and refine the sheet**: Read through the generated discovery sheet. Check if it covers all the points mentioned in the prompt (customer acquisition, online gaps, fixes, icebreakers, red flags, elevator line).

    You should have a concise, actionable document ready to help you prepare for your client visit.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can also use other freemium AI chat assistants like **Gemini** (gemini.google.com) or **Claude** (claude.ai) to run this prompt. Their free tiers offer similar capabilities for text generation.
*   **Common mistake**: Not providing enough detail about the business. The more specific information you give ChatGPT (e.g., exact years open, specific customer types), the more tailored and useful your discovery sheet will be.
*   **Tip for better results**: After getting the initial sheet, you can ask ChatGPT to refine specific sections. For example, "Make the icebreaker questions more specific to a tire shop" or "Expand on the two cheapest high-impact fixes for a restaurant." You can also ask it to adjust the tone or length if needed.
