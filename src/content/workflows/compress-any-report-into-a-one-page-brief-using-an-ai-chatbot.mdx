## What you'll get
You'll get a concise, one-page brief from any long report, designed for busy readers who need to quickly grasp the key takeaways and next steps. This approach works by forcing the AI to prioritize the "bottom line" and highlight uncertainties, building trust and cutting out irrelevant details.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chatbot** (freemium): A web-based tool like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini that can understand and generate text based on your instructions.

## Steps

1.  **Open your AI Chatbot**: Go to your preferred AI chatbot (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) in your web browser or mobile app. You'll see a text box where you can type your instructions.

2.  **Paste the main prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into the chatbot. Replace `[paste your report here]` with the full text of the report you want to summarize. Then, send the message.

    ```
    Turn the report below into a one-page brief for a busy reader who will not read the full thing. Structure, in this order:
    1. Bottom line (2 sentences): what's true now and what it means.
    2. Three things that matter, each one line, ranked by consequence.
    3. What's uncertain or missing (be honest, don't smooth it over).
    4. The decision or action this brief is asking for.

    Constraints:
    - Plain sentences, no adjectives that don't carry information.
    - Every claim must trace back to the report. If it isn't in there, don't add it.
    - If the report doesn't support a clear recommendation, say that instead of inventing one.

    Report: [paste your report here]
    ```

    You should receive a structured brief, usually within a few seconds, following the exact format requested.

3.  **Review the brief**: Read through the generated brief. Check if it accurately captures the main points, highlights uncertainties, and suggests a clear action or decision based *only* on the report's content. Ensure it avoids fluff and sticks to plain language.

4.  **Refine if the AI hedges (optional)**: If the AI's initial brief seems too cautious or avoids making a clear statement, you can follow up with this prompt:

    ```
    You're allowed to be wrong, give me your best single read.
    ```

    This encourages the AI to provide a more direct interpretation, even if it's not 100% certain.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Most major AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini offer robust free tiers that can handle this task. You can try them all to see which one gives you the best results.
*   **Common mistake**: Simply asking an AI to "summarize this report" often results in a longer, less focused version of the original, rather than a true brief. This prompt's specific structure and constraints are key to getting an actionable outcome.
*   **Tip for better results**: For very long reports, some free-tier chatbots might have character limits. If your report is too long, try breaking it into sections and summarizing each section separately, then combining those summaries for a final brief. Always double-check the AI's output against the original report for accuracy.
