## What you'll get
You will get a revised CV that follows specific career guidance, focusing on achievements, quantifiable results, and conciseness. This approach works because it directly addresses common recruiter preferences and helps you stand out by showcasing impact rather than just responsibilities.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chat Assistant** (freemium): To help you rephrase your CV points and ensure it meets the specified criteria.

## Steps
1.  **Understand the CV Rules**: Read through the provided CV guidance carefully. The key rules are: no personal details (DOB, nationality, photo), no salary expectations, focus on achievements with numbers (start with a verb, state what changed), write without pronouns (no 'I' or 'my'), include numbers (team size, budget, percentages), remove 'References available upon request', keep it to one or two pages, add a short summary at the top, and list promotions as separate roles.
2.  **Prepare Your Current CV**: Open your current CV document. You will need to copy and paste sections of it into the AI chat assistant.
3.  **Ask the AI for Help**: Open your chosen AI chat assistant (like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini). Start a new chat and explain that you need help revising your CV based on specific rules. You can start with a prompt like this:

```
I need to revise my CV based on the following rules provided by a career team:

- No personal details (date of birth, marital status, nationality, photo).
- Do not include salary information.
- Focus on achievements, not just duties. Start with a verb and state what changed. For example, instead of 'Responsible for month end close', use 'Cut month end close from 8 days to 5'.
- Write without pronouns like 'I' or 'my'.
- Include numbers in bullet points whenever possible (e.g., team size, budget, percentages).
- Remove 'References available upon request'.
- Keep the CV to one or two pages.
- If promoted within the same company, list each promotion as a separate role to show growth.
- Include a short summary (about 3 lines) at the top: who you are, your scale of work, and what you want next.
- Place your LinkedIn profile link near the top.

Here is a section of my current CV. Please help me rewrite it to follow these rules, focusing on achievements and using numbers where possible. Please also remove any pronouns like 'I' or 'my'.

[Paste a section of your CV here]
```
4.  **Review and Refine**: The AI will provide a rewritten version of your CV section. Read it carefully. Check if it accurately reflects your experience and follows all the rules. You may need to ask follow-up questions or provide more context to the AI to get the best results. For example, you could say: "Can you help me rephrase this achievement to include a specific number?" or "Please ensure all bullet points start with an action verb."
5.  **Update Your CV**: Copy the revised text from the AI back into your CV document. Repeat steps 3 and 4 for each section of your CV until it is fully updated according to the guidance.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free Tier Alternative**: If you prefer not to use an AI chat assistant, you can manually go through your CV and apply each rule yourself. This will take more time but requires no external tools.
*   **Common Pitfall**: Be careful not to invent numbers or achievements. The advice is to use numbers *if you know them*. If you don't know the exact figure, it's better to describe the impact qualitatively or estimate reasonably rather than fabricating data.
*   **Tip for Better Results**: For the summary section, be very specific about your career goals. Instead of saying 'looking for a new challenge', state the type of role or industry you are targeting. This helps recruiters quickly understand your aspirations.
