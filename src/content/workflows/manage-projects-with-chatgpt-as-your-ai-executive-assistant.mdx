## What you'll get
You'll set up ChatGPT to act as a dedicated executive assistant and project portfolio manager. This AI will help you track your commitments, evaluate new tasks, and prioritize your work, ensuring you focus on measurable outcomes rather than just activity. This approach works by giving the AI a clear, persistent role, allowing it to maintain context and challenge your decisions effectively.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that you will instruct to act as your executive assistant.

## Steps

1.  **Start a new chat and set up your AI executive assistant**: Open ChatGPT in your browser or app. Start a new conversation and paste the following prompt. This will define the AI's role and responsibilities.

    ```
    Act as my executive assistant and portfolio manager. Your responsibility is not simply to answer questions. Your responsibility is to help me achieve measurable outcomes across multiple competing commitments. Maintain awareness of my active projects, priorities, blockers, dependencies, deadlines, and available capacity. Before recommending new work: Determine what I'm already committed to. Identify whether the proposed work advances an existing objective. Evaluate its urgency, impact, and opportunity cost. Identify what existing commitment would lose attention. Recommend whether to execute, delegate, defer, park, or reject it. Do not confuse activity with progress. Challenge unnecessary work and identify when I'm optimizing something that doesn't meaningfully advance my goals. Your success is measured by ou
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT acknowledge its new role and confirm it understands its responsibilities. (Note: The original author's prompt was cut off at "ou", so this is the full prompt as provided.)

2.  **Share your current projects and commitments**: Once the AI assistant is set up, tell it about your existing projects, tasks, deadlines, and any personal development goals or recurring commitments you have. Be as specific as possible.

    ```
    Here are my current active projects:
    1. Project A: [Brief description], deadline [Date], current status [Status].
    2. Project B: [Brief description], deadline [Date], current status [Status].
    My main priority for this week is [Goal].
    ```

    The AI should process this information and confirm it has updated its understanding of your portfolio.

3.  **Ask for guidance on new tasks or priorities**: When new work comes up, or you need to prioritize existing tasks, ask your AI executive assistant for its recommendation based on the role you defined.

    ```
    I'm considering taking on a new task: [Describe the new task]. How does this fit with my current commitments and priorities? Should I execute, delegate, defer, park, or reject it?
    ```

    You should receive a recommendation that considers your existing workload, objectives, and the potential impact of the new task, as outlined in the initial setup prompt.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier viability**: The free tier of ChatGPT (GPT-3.5) is generally sufficient for this workflow, though paid tiers (GPT-4) may offer better reasoning and context retention.
*   **Common mistake**: Forgetting that ChatGPT has a limited memory within a single conversation. If you start a new chat, you'll need to re-paste the initial setup prompt and re-introduce your projects. To maintain continuity, always use the same chat thread for your executive assistant.
*   **Tip for better results**: Regularly update your AI assistant on your progress, completed tasks, and any changes to your priorities or capacity. The more context it has, the better its recommendations will be. You can also ask it to summarize your current portfolio periodically to ensure it's on track.
