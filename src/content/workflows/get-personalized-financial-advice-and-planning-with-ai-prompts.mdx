## What you'll get
You'll get personalized financial advice and planning insights by using specific prompts with an AI chat model. Instead of generic advice, this approach helps you analyze your own income and spending to make real-world decisions about trips, job changes, or emergency funds. It works because you provide your actual numbers, making the AI's suggestions highly relevant to your situation.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chat model that can understand your requests and generate text responses.

## Steps
The author emphasizes that these prompts work best when you first provide your income and monthly spending by category. This gives the AI the necessary context.

1.  **Prepare your financial data**: Before using any prompt, gather your monthly income and a breakdown of your monthly spending by category (e.g., rent, food, transport, entertainment). You don't need to share sensitive account numbers, just the amounts.
    You should have a clear list of your financial inflows and outflows ready.

2.  **Open your AI chat tool**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface, ready for your input.

3.  **Provide your financial context**: Start by telling the AI your basic financial situation. This sets the stage for all subsequent prompts.

    ```
    Here is my monthly income and spending by category:

    Income: [Your monthly income, e.g., NGN 250,000]

    Spending Categories:
    Rent: [Amount, e.g., NGN 80,000]
    Food: [Amount, e.g., NGN 50,000]
    Transport: [Amount, e.g., NGN 20,000]
    Utilities: [Amount, e.g., NGN 15,000]
    Entertainment: [Amount, e.g., NGN 10,000]
    Savings: [Amount, e.g., NGN 30,000]
    Other: [Amount, e.g., NGN 20,000]

    Total Spending: [Sum of your spending, e.g., NGN 225,000]
    ```

    The AI will acknowledge your input, often with a simple "Okay, I have your financial data."

4.  **Prompt 1: Get a "roast" of your spending**: Ask the AI to critically review your spending and suggest practical changes.

    ```
    Roast my spending. Don't be nice. Then give me 3 changes that don't require going to extremes.
    ```

    You should get direct feedback on your spending habits and three actionable, non-extreme suggestions for saving money, similar to the author's example of "ready meals three days a week instead of five."

5.  **Prompt 2: Plan a trip that isn't in the budget**: Figure out how to afford a specific trip without changing your savings rate.

    ```
    I want a 2-week trip to Dublin in October. It's not in the budget. Subtract what I won't spend while I'm away, price the trip, and tell me what to cut so my savings rate doesn't change.
    ```

    The AI will estimate potential savings from not spending on usual expenses (like groceries or commute) while away, estimate trip costs, and suggest specific cuts to cover the difference, helping you maintain your savings goals.

6.  **Prompt 3: Evaluate a lower-paying job offer**: Understand the financial impact of taking a job with less pay.

    ```
    I'm offered a job I want at 20% less. Show me two versions: keep my lifestyle and retire later (how much later?), or keep my retirement date and live on less (what does a month look like?).
    ```

    The AI will present two scenarios: one showing how much longer you'd need to work to maintain your current lifestyle with less income, and another detailing what your monthly budget would look like if you cut expenses to retire on time.

7.  **Prompt 4: Calculate your layoff runway**: Determine how long your funds would last if you lost your job.

    ```
    If I lost my job tomorrow, how many months could I search? Split it by money tier: cash, savings, brokerage, retirement accounts with penalties. Tell me which months are free and which ones cost me retirement.
    ```

    You will receive a breakdown of how many months each type of fund (cash, savings, investments) would cover, clearly indicating which funds are easily accessible and which might incur penalties if used early.

8.  **Prompt 5: Compare lifestyle choices (e.g., car vs. Uber)**: Analyze the financial implications of different major lifestyle decisions.

    ```
    Compare one year of: keeping my car, buying a newer one, selling it and using Uber. Include all relevant costs.
    ```

    The AI will provide a financial comparison for each scenario (keeping your car, buying a new one, or using ride-sharing services), detailing costs like maintenance, insurance, fuel, depreciation, or Uber fares, helping you make an informed decision.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can use other freemium AI chat models like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) for these prompts. They offer similar capabilities in their free tiers.
*   **Common mistake**: Do not share highly sensitive personal information like bank account numbers, full names, or addresses with the AI. Stick to financial figures and general scenarios.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific as possible with your numbers and situation. The more detail you provide about your income, spending, and goals, the more tailored and useful the AI's advice will be. You can also ask follow-up questions to dig deeper into any of the AI's suggestions.
