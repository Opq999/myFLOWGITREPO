## What you'll get
You will get an interactive, adaptive AI testing environment within ChatGPT. This environment will quiz you on any subject, identify what you misunderstand, correct your deficiencies, and help you develop a deep understanding and long-term retention of the material. This method is ideal for preparing for exams or mastering complex topics by focusing on genuine comprehension rather than simple memorization.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot used to create your adaptive study environment.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer. You should see the chat interface.

2.  **Start a new chat**: Click the "New chat" button (or similar icon, usually a square with a plus sign) to begin a fresh conversation. You should see an empty chat window ready for your input.

3.  **Paste the Master Prompt**: Copy the "ORDER 66" prompt below and paste it into the message box. This prompt defines the rules for your adaptive testing environment.

    ```

Use the following rules throughout the session. 1. One Question at a Time Present exactly one question at a time . Wait for my response before continuing. Do not reveal the correct answer prematurely. Avoid lengthy introductions or unnecessary commentary. Advance automatically after grading unless I request clarification. 2. Default Question Format Use two-answer multiple-choice questions (A/B) by default. Both answers must be plausible. Whenever possible, construct questions using closely related concepts that are easily confused. Avoid obviously incorrect distractors. The objective is to test genuine understanding rather than recognition of an obviously correct answer. 3. Immediate Grading After each answer: If correct: Confirm briefly. Explain only when clarification ad…
    ```

    After sending the prompt, ChatGPT will acknowledge it and wait for your study material.

4.  **Provide your study material**: In the next message, tell ChatGPT the subject you want to study and paste any relevant notes, study guides, or textbook excerpts. The more detail you provide, the better the AI can tailor its questions.

    ```
    My subject is [Your Subject Here]. Here are my study notes:
    [Paste your study notes or guide here]
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT acknowledge your material and then present its first question based on the rules defined in the master prompt.

5.  **Answer questions and learn**: Respond to each question with your answer (A or B). ChatGPT will grade your answer immediately, provide brief confirmation if correct, or explain the correct answer and reasoning if incorrect. It will then move to the next question automatically, unless you ask for more clarification.

## Notes & variations
*   **Incomplete Prompt**: The original source provided a truncated version of the "ORDER 66" prompt. For the full experience and complete rules, you may want to search online for the complete prompt, as it likely contains more detailed instructions for the AI.
*   **Other AI Tools**: You can adapt this workflow to other freemium AI chatbots like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Anthropic's Claude (claude.ai) by pasting the same master prompt and study material.
*   **Common Pitfall**: Not providing enough detailed study material. If your notes are too brief or vague, the AI may struggle to generate effective and challenging questions. Provide as much context as possible.
*   **Tip for Better Results**: If you have access to ChatGPT's "Custom Instructions" feature (available in some free and all paid tiers), you can save the master prompt there. This way, you won't need to paste it at the start of every new study session, and it will apply to all your chats.
