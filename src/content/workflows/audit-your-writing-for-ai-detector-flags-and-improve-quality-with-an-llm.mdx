## What you'll get
You will get specific feedback on your writing, highlighting sentences that sound generic, lack detail, or have a repetitive rhythm. This helps you identify areas that might trigger AI detectors and, more importantly, improve your writing to be clearer and more engaging. This approach works by having the AI act as an editor, pointing out common pitfalls in hurried writing that also happen to be the patterns AI detectors look for.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A chat AI that can understand and respond to complex instructions, used here to analyze your text.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI chat tool**: Go to `https://chatgpt.com` on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for you to type a message.

2.  **Prepare your writing**: Copy the text you want to audit from your document or notes.
    Make sure you have the full text ready to paste.

3.  **Paste the audit prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into the chat box. Then, replace `[PASTE YOUR TEXT HERE]` with your copied writing.

    ```
    Analyze the following text I wrote myself. I want to know where it reads as generic, flat, or templated, because that's where it's weakest and also where automated detectors false-flag. Do NOT rewrite it. Instead: 1. Point to specific sentences that are vague, hedged, or could have been written about any lab / any topic, and say why. 2. Flag places where I state something without a concrete number, observation, or detail that only I would have. 3. Note any long stretches of same-length, same-rhythm sentences. 4. For each, ask me a question that would pull a specific detail out of my head to replace the generic version. Give me the list. I'll do the rewriting myself.
    [PASTE YOUR TEXT HERE]
    ```

    You should see the AI start processing your request.

4.  **Review the feedback**: Read the AI's analysis. It will list specific sentences or sections and ask questions to help you add more detail or vary your sentence structure.
    You should get a structured list of feedback points, each with a question.

5.  **Improve your writing**: Use the AI's questions to guide you in adding specific details, varying sentence length, and making your writing more unique. Do the rewriting yourself in your original document.
    Your writing will become stronger, clearer, and less likely to be flagged by AI detectors.

## Original source
This workflow was shared by Reddit user /u/InsuranceNeither903, a mechanical engineering junior, who developed this prompt after their own lab report was incorrectly flagged by an AI detector. They posted it on Reddit as a way to improve writing quality and avoid generic patterns.

## Notes & variations
*   You can use other freemium AI chat tools like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) with the same prompt. The results may vary slightly depending on the model.
*   A common mistake is asking the AI to rewrite the text for you. The goal here is to improve your own writing voice, not to replace it with an AI-generated version. Always do the rewriting yourself based on the AI's feedback.
*   For best results, provide a significant chunk of text (a few paragraphs to a page) rather than just a few sentences. This gives the AI enough context to identify patterns in your writing style.
