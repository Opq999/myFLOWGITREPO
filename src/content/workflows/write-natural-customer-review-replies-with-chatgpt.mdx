## What you'll get
You'll get personalized, natural-sounding replies to your customer reviews that genuinely reflect your small business's voice. This approach works by telling the AI model (a computer program that can understand and generate human-like text) what *not* to do, which helps it avoid generic, corporate-sounding language.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chat tool you'll use to generate the review replies.

## Steps

1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website in your browser. If you don't have an account, you'll need to sign up for a free one. Once logged in, you'll see a chat interface.

2.  **Paste your prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into the chat box. Replace `[paste review]` with the actual customer review you want to reply to. This prompt guides the AI to sound like a small business owner and avoid common corporate phrases.

    ```
    You're replying to a customer review for a small local business. Here's the review: [paste review]. Write a reply of 2 to 3 sentences. Mention one specific thing they said. Don't open with "Thank you for your feedback", use one exclamation mark at most, and don't promise anything that hasn't been agreed. If the review is negative, acknowledge the problem plainly and offer one next step, like a quick call. Sound like the owner, not a marketing team.
    ```

    You should get a reply that is concise, specific, and avoids generic language, like the example: "Glad the Sunday call-out helped, burst pipes don't wait for Monday. Thanks for taking the time to write this."

3.  **Review and refine**: Read the generated reply. Check if it sounds like you and addresses the customer's points naturally. You can ask ChatGPT to make small changes if needed, for example, "Make it sound a bit more friendly" or "Can you shorten it to two sentences?"

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can also use other freemium AI chat tools like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) with a similar prompt to get good results.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is asking an AI to "write a reply" without giving it specific instructions on tone or what to avoid. This often leads to generic, impersonal responses that don't sound like a real person.
*   **Tip for better results**: Customize the "don't" list in the prompt to match your own pet peeves or phrases you want to avoid. For example, if you dislike the phrase "We appreciate your business," add "Don't say 'We appreciate your business'" to the prompt.
