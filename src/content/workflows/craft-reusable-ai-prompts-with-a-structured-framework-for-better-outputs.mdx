## What you'll get
You will learn to write clear, structured AI prompts that consistently produce better, more relevant results. This method helps you avoid vague instructions and ensures the AI has all the necessary information without being overloaded, making your prompts reusable for different tasks.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A popular AI chatbot that can understand and generate human-like text based on your prompts.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI chat tool**: Go to ChatGPT (or your preferred AI chat tool like Claude or Gemini) in your web browser or app. You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Understand the prompt framework**: The author suggests a structured approach to writing prompts. Here are the key parts and what they mean:
    *   **Role & expertise**: Tell the AI what persona or job it should act as (e.g., "Act as a marketing expert").
    *   **Objective**: Clearly state the main goal you want to achieve (e.g., "Help me brainstorm blog post ideas").
    *   **Context**: Provide only the essential background information the AI needs (e.g., "Our target audience is small business owners in Nigeria").
    *   **Task instructions**: Break down the specific actions the AI needs to perform (e.g., "Generate 5 unique blog post titles").
    *   **Constraints**: Define limits, requirements, and things to avoid (e.g., "Do not use jargon," "Keep titles under 60 characters"). This also includes quality criteria and how to handle missing information.
    *   **Output format**: Specify exactly how you want the AI's response structured (e.g., "Return a numbered list of titles").
    *   The author also mentions "Reasoning guidance" and "Reusable variables" as part of the framework, but the full template for these sections is not provided in the original excerpt.

3.  **Adapt the template to your task**: Copy the template below and replace the bracketed placeholders `[ ]` with details specific to your task. The author's full template was truncated in the source, so we present it as provided.

    ```
    ROLE Act as a [ROLE/EXPERTISE].
    OBJECTIVE Help me achieve: [DESIRED OUTCOME]
    CONTEXT Relevant information: [CONTEXT]
    TASK [EXACT TASK]
    REQUIREMENTS
    - [REQUIREMENT 1]
    - [REQUIREMENT 2]
    - [REQUIREMENT 3]
    CONSTRAINTS
    - Do not [UNWANTED BEHAVIOR].
    - If information is missing, [ASK / STATE ASSUMPTION].
    - Prioritize accuracy, relevance, clarity, and usefulness.
    OUTPUT Return the result in this format: [OUTPUT FORMAT]
    QUALIT...
    ```

    **Example**: If you want to write a social media post for a new product, your filled-in prompt might look like this:

    ```
    ROLE Act as a social media manager.
    OBJECTIVE Help me create an engaging social media post for a new product launch.
    CONTEXT Our new product is "EcoClean Laundry Pods," which are plant-based, biodegradable, and effective in cold water. Target audience: environmentally conscious individuals and busy parents.
    TASK Write a short social media post (for Instagram) announcing EcoClean Laundry Pods.
    REQUIREMENTS
    - Highlight the plant-based and cold-water effectiveness.
    - Include a call to action to visit our website.
    - Use relevant emojis.
    CONSTRAINTS
    - Do not exceed 2200 characters (Instagram limit).
    - If information is missing, state what assumption you made.
    - Prioritize accuracy, relevance, clarity, and usefulness.
    OUTPUT Return the result as a single paragraph with hashtags at the end.
    ```

4.  **Paste and generate**: Paste your completed prompt into the chat AI and press Enter or click the send button. You should receive a response that follows your instructions and format. If the output isn't perfect, refine the parts of your prompt that were unclear or missing information, then try again.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can use other freemium AI chat tools like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) to apply this prompt framework.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is not being specific enough when filling in the bracketed `[ ]` sections, or trying to cram too many different ideas into one section. Each part should be clear and focused.
*   **Tip for better results**: Start with a simple version of your prompt and gradually add more detail to the `REQUIREMENTS` and `CONSTRAINTS` sections. Test your prompt with different inputs or slight variations to see how robust it is and how consistently it performs.
