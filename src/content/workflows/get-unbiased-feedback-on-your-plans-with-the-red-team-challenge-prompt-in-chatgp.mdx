## What you'll get
You will get a detailed, critical analysis of your strategic plans, product roadmaps, or marketing ideas from an AI. Instead of polite agreement, the AI will actively try to find flaws and weaknesses in your proposal. This approach works by forcing the AI into a "red team" persona, overriding its natural tendency to be overly positive (sycophancy bias) and ensuring you receive genuinely unbiased feedback.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot to get critical feedback on your ideas.
*   **Claude** (freemium): Another AI chatbot that can also provide critical feedback.
*   **Gemini** (freemium): A third AI chatbot option for this task.

## Steps
1.  **Open your chosen AI chat tool**: Go to ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini in your web browser or mobile app.

    You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Set the Red Team persona**: Paste the following prompt to instruct the AI to act as a critical reviewer. The author doesn't share their exact prompt in this excerpt; a starting point based on their description of the "Red Team Perspective Challenge" is: Remember to replace `[Your Plan/Idea Here]` with your actual plan.

    ```
    You are a ruthless skeptic and a "Red Team" expert. Your only goal is to find structural flaws, weaknesses, and potential failures in the plan I provide. Do not offer praise, flattery, or superficial nitpicking. Focus solely on identifying critical vulnerabilities and logical gaps. Your success is measured by how thoroughly you can break my logic.

    My plan is:
    [Your Plan/Idea Here]
    ```

    The AI will acknowledge the persona or immediately start analyzing your plan with a critical tone.

3.  **Review the critical feedback**: Read through the AI's response. It should highlight potential problems, risks, and areas for improvement in your plan.

    You should get a list of specific criticisms and potential issues, rather than general positive feedback.

## Original source
This workflow is based on the "Red Team Perspective Challenge" prompt shared by Reddit user /u/blobxiaoyao on the platform Reddit. The author introduced this adversarial prompting framework to combat AI's sycophancy bias (the tendency for AI to be overly agreeable) when seeking critical feedback on strategic ideas.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: Not being specific enough in your plan. The more detail you provide about your idea, the more targeted and useful the AI's criticism will be. Avoid vague descriptions; instead, outline specific steps, goals, and assumptions.
*   **Tip for better results**: After receiving initial feedback, refine your plan based on the AI's criticisms. Then, you can re-run the prompt with your updated plan or ask follow-up questions to dig deeper into specific issues the AI raised. For example, you could ask, "How would you address the risk of [specific risk identified by AI]?"
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: All the listed tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) offer robust free tiers that are perfectly capable of handling this workflow.
