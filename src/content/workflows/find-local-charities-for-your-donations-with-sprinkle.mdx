## What you'll get
You will find local charities that can accept the items you want to donate. Sprinkle uses AI to understand what you have and then searches for organizations nearby that need those specific items. This approach works because it autom ates the research that would normally take a lot of time, matching your donations to actual needs.

## Tools you need
*   **Sprinkle** (freemium): A web application that helps you find charities for your donations.
*   **Gemini API** (paid): The AI service that powers Sprinkle's ability to understand your donation items and find matching charities. You do not directly interact with this, but it's what makes the tool work.

## Steps
1.  **Go to the Sprinkle website**: Open your web browser and go to sprinkle.pinkpixel.dev.
    You should see the Sprinkle homepage with fields to enter a location and what you want to donate.
2.  **Enter your location**: In the location field, type your ZIP code or city name.
    For example, you could type `Lagos` or `101001`.
3.  **Describe your donation**: In the text box for donation items, describe what you want to give away in plain language. Be specific.
    For example, type: `Two clean winter coats, unopened shampoo, canned soup, and a working laptop.`
4.  **Start the search**: Click the button to begin the search. This might be labeled 'Search' or similar.
    Sprinkle will show you that it is working, parsing your items, searching for organizations, and researching their donation needs.
5.  **Review the results**: Once the search is complete, Sprinkle will show you a list of nearby organizations that can use your items. Each result will include information about what they accept and links to their sources so you can verify the information.
    You will see a list of charities, their addresses or contact information, and details about why they are a good match for your donation.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free tier tip**: While Sprinkle itself is free to use, the underlying AI (Gemini) has costs associated with it. The developer is covering these costs for now, but be aware that heavy usage could potentially impact the free availability in the future.
*   **Common mistake**: Don't be too vague in your donation description. Instead of 'clothes', try 'children's winter jackets' or 'men's business shirts'. This helps the AI find more accurate matches.
*   **Get better results**: If you don't find a good match, try searching with a broader category of items or a different, nearby ZIP code or city. Sometimes organizations have very specific needs that change frequently.
