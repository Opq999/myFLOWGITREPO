## What you'll get
You will learn how to identify if AI tools like ChatGPT are recommending your product to users, leading to organic sign-ups and sales. This approach helps you understand which AI platforms might be acting as valuable, unexpected referral sources for your business.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A powerful AI chatbot that can generate text, answer questions, and provide recommendations. Used here to potentially recommend your product.
*   **PostHog** (freemium): An open-source product analytics platform that helps you understand user behavior. Used here to track where your users are coming from.

## Steps
1.  **Set up PostHog for your app**: Ensure PostHog is integrated into your website or application to track user activity and traffic sources. The author does not specify the exact setup steps, but generally, this involves adding a JavaScript snippet to your website or using their SDK.
    You should see user activity being recorded in your PostHog dashboard.
2.  **Monitor PostHog for unexpected referrals**: Regularly check your PostHog analytics, specifically looking at the 'Referrers' or 'Traffic Sources' sections. Look for any mentions or traffic originating from AI tools, particularly ChatGPT, that you haven't actively promoted.
    You should be able to see a list of websites or sources driving traffic to your app.
3.  **Analyze ChatGPT's role**: If you find traffic or sign-ups attributed to ChatGPT, investigate further within PostHog. Look at user journeys and conversion events for these specific users to understand how they found your product.
    You should be able to see if users referred by ChatGPT are signing up or making purchases.
4.  **Formulate a prompt for ChatGPT (optional, for testing)**: While the author discovered this organically, you can proactively test if ChatGPT recommends your product by asking it directly. The author does not share their exact prompt; a starting point:
    ```
    I am building a [describe your app's function, e.g., 'tool that helps freelancers manage invoices']. Can you recommend any similar tools or services that users might find helpful?
    ```
    You should get a response from ChatGPT, which may or may not include your product.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternative**: If PostHog's free tier becomes too limiting, consider other analytics tools with generous free plans like Google Analytics, though PostHog offers more specific product analytics features.
*   **Common pitfall**: Relying solely on referral links can be misleading. AI recommendations might not always pass clear referral data, making direct user surveys or in-app feedback crucial for understanding discovery.
*   **Tip for better results**: Actively engage with AI communities or platforms where your target audience might be seeking solutions. If you find your product is being recommended, consider how you can ethically and effectively support or collaborate with those AI platforms.
