## What you'll get
You will get a complete, playable web-based game generated by an AI, designed to be fun and engaging. This approach works because advanced AI models can understand complex requests and translate them into functional code, allowing non-technical users to create interactive experiences without coding.

## Tools you need
*   **Claude AI** (freemium): An AI chat assistant that can generate code and creative content based on your prompts.

## Steps

1.  **Open the Claude AI app**: Launch the Claude AI app on your iPhone. Ensure you are in the standard chat mode, not a developer-focused mode like Claude Code.
    You should see the main chat interface, ready for you to type a message.

2.  **Enter your game idea**: Paste the following prompt into the chat box. This tells Claude to create a game using `three.js` (a JavaScript library for 3D graphics on the web) and specifies the core idea and desired qualities.

    ```
    Build a three js game called "Tree appreciator" - it shows you two algorithmically generated trees and the game mechanics is about appreciating the differences between them. Think hard about how to make sure it is both fun and beautiful.
    ```

    After sending the prompt, Claude will begin processing your request. The author noted that Claude took over three hours to generate the game, so be prepared to wait for the AI's response.

3.  **Review the generated game**: Once Claude finishes, it will provide the game's code or a link to play it. The author received a "cosy little spot-the-difference game with music and animation and achievements that's quite compelling to play."
    You should see a fully functional game that matches your description, ready to be played or further refined.

## Original source
This workflow was inspired by Simon Willison's blog post, "Tree Appreciator," where he experimented with using the Claude iPhone app to generate a game. He shared his prompt and the impressive results from Claude Opus 5.5 Max.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Other freemium AI chat apps like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) might also be able to generate simple games, though the exact prompt and results may vary.
*   **Common mistake**: Don't expect instant results for complex requests. Generating a full game, even a simple one, can take the AI a significant amount of time, as seen in the author's experience (over 3 hours). Be patient.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be specific in your prompt. The author found that adding phrases like "make sure it is fun" or "beautiful" can significantly improve the AI's output quality and creativity. You can also specify desired features like "music," "animations," or "achievements."
