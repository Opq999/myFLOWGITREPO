## What you'll get
You will get a rewritten version of your resume's first page, using language and keywords directly from a specific job description. This approach helps your resume get past automated screening systems and catch the eye of human reviewers by showing you've tailored your application.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A popular AI chatbot that can understand and generate human-like text, useful here for rewriting your resume content.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website and log in or sign up for a free account.
2.  **Copy your resume's first page**: Select and copy the text from the first page of your resume.
3.  **Copy the job description**: Find the job description you want to apply for and copy all of its text.
4.  **Craft your prompt**: In ChatGPT, paste the following prompt, replacing the bracketed text with your own resume content and the job description. The author's goal was to change the *verbiage* without changing the *experience*.

```
I am applying for a job and want to tailor my resume to the job description. Please rewrite the first page of my resume below to incorporate keywords and terminology from the job description. Do not change my actual experience or accomplishments, only the way they are described to better match the job posting.

Here is the first page of my resume:
[Paste the first page of your resume here]

Here is the job description:
[Paste the job description here]
```

5.  **Review the output**: ChatGPT will provide a rewritten version of your resume's first page. Read through it carefully to ensure it accurately reflects your experience and uses the desired terminology. You may need to make minor edits yourself.

## Original source
This workflow is based on a Reddit post by user /u/Hofsizzle, who shared their strategy for using AI during a job search. The author aimed to improve their resume's relevance to specific job postings.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: Do not let the AI invent new responsibilities or change the core meaning of your experience. Always review the output to ensure accuracy.
*   **Tip for better results**: If the AI's output is too generic, try being more specific in your prompt. For example, you could ask it to focus on a particular section of your resume or to highlight specific skills mentioned in the job description.
