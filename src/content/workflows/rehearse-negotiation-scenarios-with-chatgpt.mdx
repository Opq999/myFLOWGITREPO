## What you'll get
You will get a realistic practice negotiation session with an AI chatbot that acts as a tough but fair opponent. After the session, the AI will give you specific feedback on your performance, highlighting your best arguments and areas for improvement.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot for text conversations.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    *   You should see the chat interface ready for you to type.
2.  **Set up the negotiation scenario**: Copy and paste the following prompt into ChatGPT. Replace the bracketed placeholders `{{their_role}}`, `{{situation}}`, `{{my_goal}}`, and `{{walk_away}}` with your specific details.

    ```
    I want to rehearse a negotiation. You'll play {{their_role}}. The situation: {{situation}} What I want: {{my_goal}} My walk-away point: {{walk_away}}. Act as if you don't know this. Play them realistically. Argue from their actual interests, push back the way a reasonable but tough person would, and don't give in just because I sound confident or polite. Concede only when I give you a real reason to. One short reply at a time, like a real conversation. After 8 exchanges, or when I type "stop", break character and tell me: the moment I gave away the most, the argument of mine that landed best, and the one sentence I should have said instead.
    ```

    *   You should see ChatGPT acknowledge your request and then start playing the role you assigned, making its first move in the negotiation.
3.  **Start your negotiation**: Respond to ChatGPT as if you are in the real negotiation, making your opening statement or counter-offer.
    *   You should see ChatGPT reply, pushing back or making a counter-offer, acting like a tough but realistic person.
4.  **Continue the conversation**: Keep exchanging messages with ChatGPT, trying to achieve your goal and respond to its arguments.
    *   You should see the conversation unfold naturally, with ChatGPT only giving ground when you provide strong, logical arguments.
5.  **End the rehearsal and get feedback**: After 8 exchanges, or when you feel ready, type `stop` to end the role-play.
    *   You should see ChatGPT break character and provide feedback: the moment you gave away the most, your best argument, and a suggested sentence you could have used instead.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: The free tier of ChatGPT (GPT-3.5) is usually sufficient for this task. Other freemium chatbots like Claude.ai or Gemini.google.com could also work, but you might need to adjust the prompt slightly to get similar results, as their default behaviors can differ.
*   **Common mistake**: Don't forget to replace the `{{placeholders}}` in the prompt with your actual negotiation details. If you leave them in, ChatGPT will ask you for them or generate a generic scenario, which won't be as helpful for your specific practice.
*   **Tip for better results**: Before starting, take a moment to clearly define your `{{their_role}}`, `{{situation}}`, `{{my_goal}}`, and especially your `{{walk_away}}` point. The more specific you are, the more realistic and helpful the AI's role-play will be.
