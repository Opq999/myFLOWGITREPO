## What you'll get
You will learn how to prompt ChatGPT, an AI chatbot, to understand and reproduce the *style* of your writing examples, rather than just copying their exact format, length, or specific phrases. This approach works by helping the AI distinguish between core style elements and incidental features of a single example, leading to more flexible and useful AI-generated content.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot for generating text, used here to understand and apply writing styles.

## Steps
1.  **Understand the AI's default behavior**: When you give ChatGPT only one example, it tends to copy *everything* from that example, including its length, structure, opening phrases, and tone. This happens because it can't tell which parts you liked and which were just how that one example happened to be.

2.  **Provide multiple, varied examples**: To teach ChatGPT your desired style more effectively, gather at least three examples of the writing style you want. Make sure these examples have different lengths and opening phrases. Then, paste them into ChatGPT with clear instructions:

    ```
    Here are 3 examples of what I want:
    [Paste Example 1 here]
    [Paste Example 2 here]
    [Paste Example 3 here]

    Before writing anything, tell me in one line what they all have in common, and in one line what's different between them. Keep what's common. Vary what's different.
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT first summarize the common and differing elements. Then, it will generate new content that maintains the shared style while varying the non-shared aspects.

3.  **Guide the AI with a single example for tone only**: If you only have one example, or if you want ChatGPT to focus purely on the *tone* of your writing without copying other elements, use this specific prompt:

    ```
    Here's an example of the tone I want:
    [Paste your example here]

    Copy the tone only. Don't reuse its length, its structure, its opening, or any of its phrases.
    ```

    ChatGPT should generate new content that matches the *tone* of your example, but with different length, structure, and opening phrases.

4.  **Verify what the AI borrowed**: After ChatGPT generates content, it's helpful to ask it to review its own work against your original example. This step helps you understand how the AI interpreted your request and what elements it might have still copied.

    ```
    Compare what you wrote to my example. List every phrase, structure, or length choice you took from it.
    ```

    ChatGPT will list specific elements it might have copied, which can help you refine your instructions for future interactions.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: While this workflow uses ChatGPT's free tier, similar principles can be applied to other freemium AI chatbots like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai).
*   **Common mistake**: The biggest pitfall is providing only one example without explicit instructions. The AI will likely copy too much, including elements you didn't intend to replicate.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be very specific in your prompts about what you want copied (e.g., "the professional tone") and what you want varied (e.g., "different sentence structures"). Experiment with the number and variety of examples you provide to find what works best for your specific style.
