## What you'll get
You'll get a personalized plan to prevent quitting new habits, a clear strategy for recovering after a missed day, and a daily audit to keep you on track. This approach helps you anticipate challenges and decide on recovery actions *before* you're tired or demotivated, making it easier to stick to your goals.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chatbot** (freemium): To generate personalized strategies and daily actions based on your input (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini).
*   **Notion** (freemium): To store and organize your custom prompts and the AI's responses for easy access.

## Steps

1.  **Prepare for setbacks (Day-4 Insurance)**: Open your AI chatbot and paste the first prompt. Fill in `[HABIT]`, `[X]`, and `[REASON]` with your specific details.

    ```
    I'm starting this habit: [HABIT]. I usually quit around day [X] because [REASON]. Build me a minimum version that takes under 5 minutes, a rule for what I do the day after a miss, and 3 situations where I'm most likely to skip, with a pre-decided response for each. No motivation talk. Under 150 words.
    ```

    You should get a short, actionable plan that includes a 'minimum version' of your habit, a rule for what to do after a miss, and specific responses for common skipping situations. Save this response in Notion or a similar note-taking app.

2.  **Reset after a miss (Miss-Day Reset)**: The day after you skip your habit, open your AI chatbot and paste the second prompt. Replace `[HABIT]` and `[REASON]` with what you missed and why.

    ```
    I skipped [HABIT] yesterday. Honest reason: [REASON]. Don't comfort me. Tell me what actually caused the miss, the smallest version I can do in the next hour, and one change to tomorrow so it doesn't repeat. 3 bullets max.
    ```

    The AI will give you a direct explanation of why you missed, a very small action you can do right away, and a suggestion for tomorrow. This helps you get back on track without dwelling on the miss.

3.  **Audit your progress daily (Plan vs. Execution Audit)**: Every night, open your AI chatbot and paste the third prompt. Fill in `[PLAN]` with what you intended to do and `[ACTUAL]` with what you actually did.

    ```
    Today's plan was: [PLAN]. What I actually did: [ACTUAL]. Score me 1-10 on execution. Name the exact moment I drifted. Give me tomorrow's first 3 actions, each under 15 minutes, in order.
    ```

    You'll receive a score for your day's execution, an identification of when you went off track, and three clear, short actions for the next day. This helps you reflect and plan effectively.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Most major AI chatbots like Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, or Perplexity AI offer free tiers that can handle these prompts effectively. For organizing, any free note-taking app like Google Keep or Simplenote can replace Notion.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is not being honest with the AI about why you missed a habit or what your actual plan was. The prompts are designed for direct feedback, so vague or dishonest input will lead to less useful advice.
*   **Tip for better results**: To get better results, be as specific as possible when filling in the bracketed information in the prompts. For example, instead of `[HABIT]: exercise`, try `[HABIT]: do 10 push-ups and 10 squats`. The more detail you provide, the more tailored the AI's response will be.
