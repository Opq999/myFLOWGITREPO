## What you'll get
You will learn a simple, two-question technique to make your AI assistant predict the outcome of a task before it performs it. This method helps catch errors early by creating a gap between your expectation and the AI's actual result, preventing you from building decisions on incorrect information.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Assistant** (freemium): Any AI chatbot or assistant you use for tasks like coding, spreadsheets, research, or writing.

## Steps
1.  **Ask the AI to predict the result**: Before asking your AI assistant to perform a task, prompt it to state what it expects the outcome to be and why.

    ```
    Before you run the task I'm about to give you, please answer these two questions:
    1. What do you expect the result to be, and why?
    2. How would you know if this were broken?
    ```

    You should see the AI provide a prediction and a method for verification.

2.  **Perform your task**: Now, give your AI assistant the actual task you want it to complete.

    ```
    [Paste your task here]
    ```

    You should get the result of your task.

3.  **Compare the prediction to the result**: Check if the actual result matches the prediction the AI made in step 1. If there's a significant difference, it signals a potential error or misunderstanding.

    You should see the actual output from your AI assistant.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free tier viability**: This technique works on the free tier of most AI assistants.
*   **Common mistake**: Trusting the AI's explanation of a result without comparing it to your initial prediction. The explanation can often sound plausible even if the result is wrong.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be specific in your initial task prompt. The clearer your request, the more accurate the AI's prediction and the easier it will be to spot discrepancies.
