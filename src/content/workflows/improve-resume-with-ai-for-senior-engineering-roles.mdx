## What you'll get
You will get an improved resume summary and work experience section, tailored to be more impactful for job applications. This approach works by using AI to rephrase your experience in a way that highlights achievements and aligns with common job requirements, making your resume stand out.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chat Tool** (freemium): A website or app where you can chat with an AI to get help with writing and editing tasks. Examples include Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.

## Steps
1.  **Prepare your resume content**: Copy the text from your current resume that you want to improve. Focus on your summary and specific work experience entries.
2.  **Ask the AI to improve your summary**: Go to your chosen AI chat tool and paste your current resume summary. Then, ask the AI to rewrite it to be more impactful. The author doesn't share their exact prompt; a starting point:

    ```
    Rewrite this resume summary to be more impactful and highlight my key achievements for a Senior/Lead/Principal Protocol Engineer role in the Web3 blockchain domain. Focus on making it achievement-driven and suitable for remote positions:

    [Paste your current resume summary here]
    ```

    You should see a new, rewritten summary from the AI.
3.  **Ask the AI to improve work experience**: Copy a specific work experience entry from your resume. Paste it into the AI chat and ask it to make it more impact-driven. The author doesn't share their exact prompt; a starting point:

    ```
    Rewrite this work experience description to be more impact-driven, using strong action verbs and quantifying achievements where possible. Tailor it for a Senior/Lead/Principal Protocol Engineer role in Web3:

    [Paste your work experience description here]
    ```

    You should see a rewritten version of your work experience.
4.  **Review and refine**: Read the AI's suggestions carefully. Make sure the rewritten text accurately reflects your experience and sounds like you. You may need to edit it further or ask the AI for revisions.

## Original source
This workflow is based on advice shared by a software engineer on Reddit, who was seeking feedback on their job search challenges. They mentioned using AI to format and improve their resume content, while also emphasizing their own review and tailoring process.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free tier alternative**: All major AI chat tools (like Claude.ai, ChatGPT, Gemini) offer free tiers that are more than sufficient for this task.
*   **Common mistake**: Do not just copy-paste the AI's output without reviewing it. The AI might misunderstand context or use language that doesn't sound like you. Always edit the output to ensure accuracy and authenticity.
*   **Tip for better results**: When asking the AI to improve your work experience, be specific about the type of role you are targeting (e.g., 'Senior Protocol Engineer in Web3') and mention any specific skills or technologies you want to emphasize.
