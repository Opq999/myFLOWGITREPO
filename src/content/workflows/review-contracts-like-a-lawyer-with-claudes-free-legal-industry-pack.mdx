## What you'll get
You will get an expert-level review of a contract, identifying clauses that differ from standard terms, classifying risks (low, medium, high, critical), and providing redlined suggestions. You'll also receive a summary of the top three points to negotiate. This approach helps you understand which parts of a contract are genuinely dangerous versus standard wording, so you can focus your negotiations effectively.

## Tools you need
*   **Claude** (freemium): An AI chat assistant that can be enhanced with specialized 'industry packs' for expert tasks.

## Steps
1.  **Open Claude and enable Industry Packs**: Go to `https://claude.ai` in your web browser. If you don't have an account, sign up for free. Once logged in, look for the "Plugins" or "Integrations" menu option, usually found in the sidebar or settings. Within this menu, find and enable the "Industry Packs" bundle. The author notes these are bundled, meaning you activate them all at once, not individually. You should see a confirmation that the Industry Packs are now active and available for use in your chats.
2.  **Start a new chat with the Legal Industry Pack**: Start a new conversation. You might need to select the "Legal Industry Pack" from a dropdown or option list before typing your prompt, or Claude might automatically detect the intent once you paste a contract and use the specific prompt. You should see an indication (e.g., a label or a change in Claude's persona) that the chat is now using the specialized Legal Industry Pack.
3.  **Paste your contract and ask for a review**: Copy the full text of the contract you want to review. Paste it into the chat window and use the following prompt:

    ```
    Review the attached contract. Flag every clause that deviates from standard terms, classify each risk as low, medium, high or critical, and generate redlined suggestions I can send back. Summarise the three things I should push back on hardest before signing.
    ```

    You should get a detailed analysis of the contract, with specific clauses flagged, risk levels assigned, suggested changes, and a clear summary of key negotiation points.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: While Claude's freemium tier is generous, for very long or complex documents, you might hit limits. Other general-purpose AI chatbots like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) can also summarize documents, but they won't offer the specialized risk classification and redlining suggestions that the Legal Industry Pack provides.
*   **Common mistake**: A common mistake is to treat Claude's output as definitive legal advice. The author explicitly states, "You still get a lawyer for anything with real stakes." This tool is for identifying potential issues and preparing for negotiations, not replacing professional legal counsel.
*   **Tip for better results**: For best results, ensure your contract text is clean and complete before pasting it. If the contract is an image or PDF, use an OCR (Optical Character Recognition) tool first to convert it into editable text.
*   **Other packs**: The author also mentions other useful packs: a finance one for month-end reconciliation, a sales one for building competitor battlecards, and an HR one for screening job applications. Explore these within Claude's plugin menu for other business needs.
