## What you'll get
Ready-to-use marketing copy ideas, a clear understanding of customer pain points, and honest critiques of your content ideas. This approach works by forcing the AI to think from the customer's perspective and critically evaluate its own suggestions, leading to more original and effective output.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A popular AI chat tool to paste prompts and get marketing ideas.

## Steps
1.  **Find customer pain points**: Open your AI chat tool and paste this prompt. Replace the bracketed `[customer type]`, `[problem area]`, and `[product/service]` with your specific details.

    ```
    List 15 problems that [customer type] face with [problem area]. Write each one the way the customer would actually say it (e.g. "I never know where to start"). Score each from 1-5 on how often they face it and how willing they are to pay to fix it, then sort by total score. For the top 3, add one line on how [product/service] solves it.
    ```

    You should get a list of customer problems, scored and sorted, with a brief explanation of how your product or service solves the top three. This gives you ready-made language for ads and headlines.

2.  **Stress-test a content idea**: Paste this prompt into your AI chat tool. Replace `[idea]` with your content concept and `[customer type]` with your target audience.

    ```
    Here is a content idea I plan to post: [idea] Evaluate it honestly from the point of view of [customer type]. Will they stop scrolling in the first 3 seconds? Is there a reason to keep watching? Is it worth saving or sharing? Score each out of 10 and explain. Then give 3 specific changes that would raise the scores. Skip the flattery.
    ```

    You should receive a critical evaluation of your content idea, including scores and specific suggestions for improvement. The "Skip the flattery" instruction helps ensure the feedback is genuinely useful.

3.  **Generate video hooks**: Paste this prompt into your AI chat tool. Replace `[topic]` with the subject of your short video.

    ```
    I am making a Reel / TikTok / Short about [topic]. Write 20 hooks to say or show on screen in the firs...
    ```

    The author did not provide the full prompt for this step. You should get a list of 20 engaging opening lines or visuals for your short video, designed to grab attention quickly.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Besides ChatGPT, you can use the free tiers of Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude.ai (claude.ai) for similar results.
*   **Common mistake**: Forgetting to replace the bracketed placeholders (like `[customer type]` or `[idea]`) in the prompts. Always fill these in with your specific information for the best results.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific as possible when describing your `[customer type]`, `[problem area]`, and `[product/service]`. The more detail you provide, the more tailored and useful the AI's output will be.
