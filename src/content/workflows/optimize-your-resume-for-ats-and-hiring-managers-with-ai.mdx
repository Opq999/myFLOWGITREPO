## What you'll get
You will get detailed feedback on your resume, first from the perspective of an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) and then from a human hiring manager. The AI will then provide a final, optimized version of your resume. This approach helps you tailor your resume to pass automated filters and also appeal to the person making the hiring decision, increasing your chances of getting an interview.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can understand and generate human-like text.
*   **Claude** (freemium): Another AI chatbot known for its strong text analysis and generation capabilities.
*   **Gemini** (freemium): Google's AI chatbot, useful for various text-based tasks.

## Steps
1.  **Prepare your job ad and resume**: Open the job advertisement you want to apply for. Copy the full text of the ad. Also, open your current resume and copy its full text.
    You should have both texts ready to paste.
2.  **Open your chosen AI tool**: Go to one of the AI chatbot websites (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) and log in or create a free account if you don't have one. Start a new chat.
    You should see a blank chat window where you can type or paste.
3.  **Paste the prompt**: Copy the entire prompt below and paste it into the chat window. Replace `[PASTE THE AD]` with the full job advertisement text you copied earlier, and `[PASTE RESUME]` with your full resume text.

    ```
    Review my resume twice for this job: [PASTE THE AD]
    PASS 1 - Act as an applicant tracking system with an AI ranker. Read my resume as plain text. Tell me: which sections you can't read or classify, which key requirements from the ad you can't find evidence for, and how you would rank me from "strong match" to "weak match", and why.
    PASS 2 - Act as the human hiring manager who reads the resumes that passed. Tell me what convinces you, what sounds generic or AI-written, and what makes you want to call me. Then give me one final version that works for both, in plain text with standard section names. Don't invent anything.
    My resume: [PASTE RESUME]
    ```
    The AI starts processing your request and generates a response with two passes of feedback and a final resume version.
4.  **Review the feedback and optimized resume**: Read through the AI's response. Pay close attention to the 'PASS 1' feedback to understand how an automated system (an Applicant Tracking System, or ATS, is software that scans resumes for keywords) might see your resume, and 'PASS 2' for insights from a human perspective. Then, review the 'final version' provided by the AI.
    You should have clear points on what to improve and a suggested resume text.

## Notes & variations
All the listed tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) offer free tiers that are suitable for this task. You can use any of them based on your preference.
A common mistake is to summarize the job ad instead of pasting the full text. The AI needs the complete job description to accurately compare it against your resume. Also, do not try to trick the AI with hidden text or instructions, as this can lead to immediate rejection if detected by a human reviewer.
Always paste the *real* job advertisement and your *actual* resume without any changes. The prompt includes 'Don't invent anything' to prevent the AI from adding skills you don't have, which is crucial for an honest application. If the AI adds skills you don't have, you must edit them out.
