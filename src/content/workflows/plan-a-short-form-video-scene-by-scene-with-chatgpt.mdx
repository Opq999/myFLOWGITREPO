## What you'll get
You will get a detailed, scene-by-scene plan for a short-form video, complete with suggested voiceovers, on-screen text, visual ideas, and editing directions. This approach works by breaking down the video creation into manageable parts, making it easier to produce a coherent and engaging video.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that generates text based on your prompts.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website (chatgpt.com) on your phone or computer and log in or create a free account. You will see a chat window where you can type your requests.

2.  **Paste the video planning prompt**: Copy the entire prompt below and paste it into the ChatGPT chat window. Before sending, replace the bracketed information `[LIKE THIS]` with your specific details for the video you want to create.

    ```
    Role: Act as a short-form video strategist and editor.
    Topic: Create a video about: [INSERT TOPIC]
    Audience: The target viewer is: [INSERT AUDIENCE]
    Platform: Optimize it for: [TIKTOK / YOUTUBE SHORTS / INSTAGRAM REELS]
    Goal: The viewer should: [LEARN / COMMENT / FOLLOW / CLICK / BUY / SHARE]
    Length: Keep the finished video between [30–45] seconds.
    Hooks: Generate 3 opening hooks under 12 words. Avoid generic phrases and choose the strongest one for the final script.
    Structure: Build the video using: Hook Problem or curiosity gap Main idea Practical example Payoff CTA
    Scene planning: Break the script into short scenes. For every scene return: • duration • voiceover • on-screen text • visual or B-roll idea • editing direction
    Voiceover: Use short, natural sentences that sound good when spoken aloud.
    Captions: Highlight only the most important words or ideas instead of copying the entire voiceover.
    Editing: Suggest useful cuts, zooms, screenshots, B-roll or transitions only when they improve clarity. Avoid unnecessary effects.
    Final check: Before finishing, check whether the first 3 seconds create enough curiosity, whether every scene moves the story forward, and if the call to action is clear.
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT generate a detailed plan, broken down into individual scenes with specific instructions for each part.

3.  **Review and refine your plan**: Read through the generated video plan. Check if the voiceover sounds natural, if the on-screen text highlights key points, and if the visual ideas make sense for your topic. If you need changes, you can ask ChatGPT to revise specific scenes or elements (e.g., "Make the hook more surprising" or "Suggest different visuals for scene 3").

    You should have a clear, actionable plan that you can use to start creating your short-form video.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If ChatGPT's free tier is not available or sufficient, you can try similar freemium AI chatbots like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) with a similar prompt structure.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is asking the AI to simply "write a viral script." This often leads to generic or unhelpful results. The key is to guide the AI to build the video scene by scene, as this prompt does, for more structured and usable output.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific as possible when filling in the bracketed information in the prompt. For example, instead of "a general audience," specify "small business owners looking for marketing tips." The more detail you provide, the better and more tailored the video plan will be.
