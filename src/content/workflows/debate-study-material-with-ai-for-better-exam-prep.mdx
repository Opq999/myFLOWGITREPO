## What you'll get
You'll get a deeper understanding of your study material and improved memory for exams. This approach works by forcing you to actively recall and explain concepts, then immediately getting feedback and engaging in a 'debate' with the AI to clarify any misunderstandings. It's especially useful for subjects that require strong memorization or conceptual understanding.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A conversational AI (Artificial Intelligence) chatbot that can understand your questions and respond in a human-like way, used here to act as your study partner.

## Steps

1.  **Read your study material**: Read your textbook, module, or watch a video on a specific topic you need to learn. Focus on understanding the main ideas.

    You should feel like you have a basic grasp of the topic, even if you can't explain it perfectly yet.

2.  **Explain the topic to the AI**: Open ChatGPT and explain the topic you just studied in your own words. Ask the AI to check your understanding.

    The author doesn't share their exact prompt; a starting point:

    ```
    I'm studying [Topic Name, e.g., Photosynthesis]. Here's my understanding of it: [Your explanation of the topic]. Is my explanation accurate and complete? What did I miss or get wrong?
    ```

    You should get a response from the AI that confirms what you got right and points out any inaccuracies or missing details.

3.  **Debate and clarify with the AI**: Engage in a conversation with the AI, asking it to challenge your understanding, explain points you got wrong, or dive deeper into specific aspects. This is where the 'argument style' learning happens.

    The author doesn't share their exact prompts for this stage; a starting point:

    ```
    Can you challenge my understanding of [specific point you're struggling with]? What are common misconceptions about [another specific point]? Explain [sub-topic] in more detail, as if I'm struggling to grasp it.
    ```

    You should see the AI provide counter-arguments, deeper explanations, or ask clarifying questions, helping you to refine your knowledge. Keep asking questions until you feel confident.

4.  **Repeat until perfect**: Continue explaining and debating the topic with the AI until you can explain it correctly and completely without significant errors. The author notes this can be time-consuming but is very effective.

    You should feel confident that you can explain the topic clearly and accurately, as if you were teaching it to someone else.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If ChatGPT's free tier isn't enough, you can also use other freemium AI chatbots like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) for similar results.
*   **Common mistake**: Don't just accept the AI's first answer. The power of this method comes from actively debating and digging deeper. If you don't understand something, ask the AI to explain it in a different way or provide examples.
*   **Tip for better results**: Break down complex topics into smaller, manageable sections. Debate each section individually before trying to explain the entire topic. This prevents you from getting overwhelmed and helps you build understanding step-by-step.
