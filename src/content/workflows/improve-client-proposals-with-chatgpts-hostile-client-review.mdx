## What you'll get
You'll get a list of 5 critical reasons why a skeptical client might reject your proposal, ranked by likelihood. This helps you identify weaknesses and refine your pitch before sending it. This approach works because it forces the AI to think like a busy, critical human, giving you sharper, more actionable feedback than a generic review.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can simulate different personas and provide critical feedback on text.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to `https://chatgpt.com` on your phone or computer.

    You should see the ChatGPT chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Paste your proposal**: Copy the full text of your client proposal or pitch deck (without images) and paste it into the chat window.

    The proposal text will appear in the chat.

3.  **Ask for critical feedback**: Immediately after pasting your proposal, enter the following prompt. Make sure to paste your proposal *before* this prompt, or paste this prompt *after* your proposal.

    ```
    You're the client receiving this proposal. You're busy, a bit skeptical, and you've seen 5 other agency pitches this week. Give me the 5 reasons you'd reject this or ghost us, ranked by how likely they are. Don't be polite.
    ```

    You should get a numbered list of 5 specific, critical reasons why the "client" would reject your proposal, often with direct quotes or rephrased concerns.

4.  **Review and refine**: Read through the feedback carefully. Look for common themes or major oversights, like not connecting features to client benefits (Example 1 from the author), unclear pricing (Example 2), or focusing too much on your agency instead of the client's problem (Example 3).

    You'll have clear points to improve in your proposal.

5.  **Ask for the "yes" factor (optional)**: If you want to find a strong opening line or a key selling point, follow up with this prompt:

    ```
    Now tell me the one thing that would make you say yes today.
    ```

    You should get a single, powerful statement or idea that could be used to hook the client.

## Notes & variations
Other freemium AI chatbots like Google Gemini (`https://gemini.google.com`) or Claude (`https://claude.ai`) can also be used for this workflow with similar results. A common mistake is asking for generic feedback like "review this proposal." The author emphasizes that giving the AI a specific, high-pressure persona ("busy, skeptical, seen 5 pitches") leads to much sharper and more useful feedback. Also, avoid pasting very long documents, as free tiers often have character limits. To get even better results, provide context about your client (their industry, specific challenges, or goals) before pasting your proposal.
