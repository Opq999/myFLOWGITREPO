## What you'll get
You will get AI guidance on your health symptoms and vital signs, helping you decide if you need urgent medical attention. This approach uses a large language model (LLM), like ChatGPT, to cross-reference your reported symptoms and vital signs (such as heart rate) against its vast knowledge base, potentially flagging unusual patterns that warrant professional medical advice. Remember, AI advice is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a doctor for health concerns.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can understand and generate human-like text, used here to analyze symptoms and provide guidance.
*   **Fitbit** (paid for device, data is free): A wearable device that tracks health metrics like heart rate. You will need access to your Fitbit data (e.g., through the app) to provide it to ChatGPT.

## Steps
1.  **Gather your health data**: Open your Fitbit app or check your device to find your current resting heart rate. Note down any other relevant symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever or cough, and their severity.
    You should have your symptoms and resting heart rate ready.
2.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the ChatGPT chat interface load.
3.  **Describe your symptoms and vital signs**: In the chat box, provide your symptoms and your resting heart rate. The author didn't share their exact prompt, but a good starting point is to be clear and specific. The author mentioned a fever of 100.4 degrees Fahrenheit and an incomplete resting heart rate of "10...". You should use your own full resting heart rate.

    ```
    I have a fever and a cough. My fever is 100.4 degrees Fahrenheit. My resting heart rate is [your resting heart rate here].
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT respond by analyzing your symptoms. In the author's case, ChatGPT repeatedly insisted on seeking urgent care due to the combination of symptoms and heart rate. It might suggest your symptoms are "off" or indicate something serious.
4.  **Follow up (if needed)**: If ChatGPT's initial response is concerning or unclear, you can ask follow-up questions, but be prepared for it to reiterate its advice if it detects a serious pattern.

    ```
    Can you explain why you think this is serious?
    ```

    ChatGPT will likely explain its reasoning or continue to advise seeking professional medical help, as it did for the author.

## Original source
This workflow is inspired by a Reddit post by user /u/liveLetLive21, who shared their experience using ChatGPT to get guidance on concerning health symptoms. The AI's persistent advice led them to seek medical attention, which revealed a severe bacterial pneumonia.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: ChatGPT has a free tier that is sufficient for this workflow. Other freemium AI chatbots like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) could also be used, but their responses might vary.
*   **Common mistake or pitfall**: A common mistake is to treat AI advice as a definitive diagnosis. AI models are not doctors and cannot perform medical diagnoses. Always confirm any health concerns with a qualified medical professional.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific as possible when describing your symptoms. Include details like when they started, how severe they are, and any other relevant health information. The more context you provide, the better the AI can analyze your situation.
