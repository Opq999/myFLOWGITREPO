## What you'll get

You'll get a structured plan for a social media carousel, designed to keep people swiping through your content. This approach works by focusing on tension and payoff, rather than just summarizing an article, making your carousels much more engaging.

## Tools you need

*   **AI Chatbot** (freemium): A conversational AI tool like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, used to process your article and generate carousel content.

## Steps

1.  **Prepare your article**: Find the article you want to turn into a carousel. Copy its full text to your clipboard.

    You should have the complete article text ready to paste.

2.  **Find the 'spine' of your content**: Open your chosen AI chatbot (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) on your phone or computer. Paste the following prompt into the chatbot, replacing `{{paste}}` with your article content.

    ```
    Here's an article: {{paste}}. Don't make slides yet. First tell me:
    - The single most surprising or contrarian point in here.
    - The one belief the reader probably holds that this challenges.
    - The one thing they can do differently after reading it.
    Answer in three short lines.
    ```

    You should receive three distinct short lines: a surprising point (your hook), a challenged belief (your tension), and an action item (your payoff). If the article doesn't yield clear answers, it might not be suitable for this carousel technique.

3.  **Build your carousel cards**: Using the three points you just received, tell the AI chatbot to create the carousel cards. You will need to decide how many cards you want (e.g., 5, 7, 10). Replace `{{number}}` with your desired card count.

    ```
    Using the surprising point as card 1 (as a hook, not a title), build a {{number}}-card carousel.
    - Each card should raise a question the next card answers, so people keep swiping.
    - One idea per card, short bold line plus 1-2 sentences.
    - Put the "do differently" point as the payoff near the end.
    - No card should just restate the article. Every card either sets up tension or resolves it.
    ```

    You should get a sequence of carousel card ideas, each with a bold line and 1-2 sentences, designed to flow from one to the next and build engagement. Review them to ensure they follow the tension-and-payoff structure.

## Notes & variations

Most major AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini offer free tiers that are sufficient for this workflow. You can use any of these without needing a paid subscription.

A common mistake is asking the AI to simply 'summarize' an article into a carousel. This often leads to bland, forgettable content. This workflow specifically avoids that by focusing on tension and payoff.

Experiment with the `{{number}}` of cards in the second prompt. A shorter carousel (e.g., 5-7 cards) can be more impactful for quick reads, while a longer one (e.g., 8-10 cards) might be better for more complex topics, as long as each card truly earns the next swipe.
