## What you'll get
You'll get 10 varied hooks for your YouTube Shorts, 5 options for seamless loop endings, and a detailed weekly performance review based on your channel's stats. This approach helps you create engaging Shorts that encourage rewatches and provides actionable insights to improve your content strategy, moving beyond generic AI suggestions.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot to generate text based on your prompts.
*   **YouTube** (free): The platform where your Shorts are published and where you get your performance data.

## Steps
1.  **Generate varied hooks**: Open your AI chatbot and paste the prompt below. Replace `[TOPIC]` with the subject of your Short and `[TARGET VIEWER]` with who you want to reach (e.g., "people learning to cook"). Hooks are short, attention-grabbing phrases at the start of your video.

    ```
    Write 10 different hooks (max 12 words each, spoken in under 3 seconds) for a Short about [TOPIC] for [TARGET VIEWER]. Use a different technique for each: bold claim, question, shocking stat, negative ("stop doing X"), story-in-progress, "you've been lied to", countdown, before/after, direct callout, and visual pattern interrupt. Label each technique.
    ```

    You should get a list of 10 hooks, each labeled with a different technique. Read them out loud and pick the most specific one that fits your video.

2.  **Create a seamless loop ending**: A loop ending makes your video flow smoothly from the end back to the beginning when it replays, encouraging viewers to watch again without noticing. In your AI chatbot, paste the prompt below. Replace `[FIRST LINE]` with the exact first line of your Short's script and `[SCRIPT]` with the full script of your Short.

    ```
    Rewrite the last line of this Short so it flows grammatically into the first line, creating a seamless loop when replayed. First line: [FIRST LINE]. Script: [SCRIPT]. Give 5 options.
    ```

    You should get 5 different options for your Short's last line. Choose the one that best connects to your first line.

3.  **Perform a weekly performance review**: Gather your YouTube Shorts performance data for the last 7 days. This includes metrics like views, average view duration, percentage viewed versus swiped, click-through rate (CTR), and subscribers gained per video. Then, paste the prompt into your AI chatbot, replacing `[views, avg view duration, % viewed vs swiped, CTR, subs per video]` with your actual numbers.

    ```
    Here are my last 7 days of stats: [views, avg view duration, % viewed vs swiped, CTR, subs per video]. Identify the top 2 performers and why, the bottom 2 and why, one pattern across the winners, one experiment for next week, and one thing to stop doing. Be blunt.
    ```

    You should receive a detailed analysis of your Shorts' performance, including insights into what worked, what didn't, and actionable suggestions for your next steps.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If ChatGPT's free tier isn't sufficient, you can use other freemium AI chatbots like Claude.ai or Gemini.google.com for similar results.
*   **Common mistake**: For the weekly review, always compare your stats against your *own* channel's average performance, not against other creators. Comparing to others often leads to generic advice like "post more consistently" that isn't tailored to your specific content.
*   **Tip for better hooks**: When generating hooks, the author suggests reading them out loud. This helps you identify which ones sound natural and specific, rather than just clever, making them more effective for your audience.
