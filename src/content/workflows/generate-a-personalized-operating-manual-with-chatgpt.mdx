## What you'll get
You will receive a detailed "operating manual" about yourself, generated by ChatGPT based on your past conversations with it. This approach works by having the AI analyze your interaction history to identify your recurring goals, patterns, and decision-making style, offering a unique perspective for self-improvement.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can understand and generate human-like text.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website and log in.
    You should see the chat interface, ready for you to type a message.
2.  **Paste the prompt**: Copy the entire prompt below and paste it into the message box.
    ```
    Using only information you actually have from my conversation history, memory, files, or other context available to you, build a practical “operating manual” for me. Do not flatter me, diagnose me, or invent psychological explanations. Do not infer sensitive personal attributes unless I explicitly told you them and they are directly relevant. Separate clearly: What you know Recurring goals Long-term projects Constraints I repeatedly work around Skills, resources, and advantages I appear to have Preferences I consistently express Patterns you observe Problems I repeatedly return to Strategies that seem to work well for me Strategies I repeatedly try that seem not to work Situations where I tend to overcomplicate or underthink things Recurring tensions between my goals Your inferences For every inference, state: the inference, the evidence that led you there, your confidence: low / medium / high, and at least one plausible alternative explanation. Blind spots or underused opportunities Identify things already present in my skills, resources, relationships, projects, or habits that I may not be taking full advantage of. My apparent decision-making style Describe how I tend to approach uncertainty, risk, planning, experimentation, and changing my mind. Use examples where possible. Highest-leverage changes Give me 3–5 concrete changes that could noticeably improve my life or work over the next three months. Prefer small structural changes over vague advice like “be more disciplin…
    ```
    You should see the prompt appear in the message box.
3.  **Send the prompt**: Press the "Enter" key or click the send button (usually a paper airplane icon).
    ChatGPT will start generating a response, which might take a moment.
4.  **Review your operating manual**: Read through the generated manual.
    You should see a structured response with sections like "What you know," "Recurring goals," "Your inferences," and "Highest-leverage changes," offering insights based on your past conversations.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Other freemium AI chatbots like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) can also be used with this prompt, though the quality of the output might vary depending on their ability to access and process your conversation history.
*   **Common mistake**: Not having enough conversation history with the AI. If you've only used ChatGPT a few times, it won't have enough data to generate a detailed or accurate manual. The more you've chatted, the better the results.
*   **Tip for better results**: Before using this prompt, have several detailed conversations with ChatGPT about your work, projects, challenges, and goals. This will give the AI more relevant information to draw from when creating your manual.
