## Words you'll see, explained
*   **ComfyUI**: A visual tool that lets you create AI images by connecting different steps (called "nodes") like a flowchart on your computer.
*   **Node-based interface**: A way to build complex tasks by linking small, self-contained blocks (nodes) together, where each node performs a specific function.
*   **Dataset**: A collection of structured information, in this case, a large list of text prompts specifically designed for AI image generation.
*   **Hugging Face**: A popular website where people share AI models, datasets, and tools, making them accessible to others.
*   **Workflow**: A pre-arranged set of steps or connections within a tool like ComfyUI, designed to achieve a specific task, often saved as a file.
*   **LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation)**: A small add-on file that modifies an AI image model to generate specific styles, characters, or objects without retraining the entire model.
*   **GPU (Graphics Processing Unit)**: A special computer chip that is very good at handling the complex calculations needed for AI tasks, making processes like generating images much faster.
