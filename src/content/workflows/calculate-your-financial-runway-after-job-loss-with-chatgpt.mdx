## What you'll get
You'll get a detailed, month-by-month breakdown of how long your money (savings, severance, and potential unemployment benefits) will last after a job loss. This approach helps you understand your financial runway under different spending habits (current, moderate cuts, severe cuts) and accounts for often-missed costs like health insurance and yearly expenses, giving a more realistic picture than simple savings-to-expense ratios.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can process text prompts and perform calculations based on the information you provide and its web search capabilities.

## Steps
1.  **Gather your financial details**: Before you start, collect the following information. Be as accurate as possible, as the AI's output depends on your input:
    *   Your filing status (single or married filing jointly).
    *   Your location (city and state).
    *   Your current monthly income (before job loss).
    *   Your current monthly expenses (list major categories or a total).
    *   Your total savings (e.g., in a checking or savings account).
    *   Any severance amount you received.
    *   Your 401k balance (or equivalent retirement savings).
    *   Number of dependents (e.g., children).
    *   Your spouse's part-time income, if applicable.
    *   Any significant yearly costs (like gifts, travel, clothes, car maintenance) that need to be spread across months.

2.  **Open ChatGPT and enable web search**: Go to the ChatGPT website. If you have a version of ChatGPT that allows web browsing (often indicated by a globe icon or a specific model like GPT-4), make sure this feature is turned on. This allows the AI to look up current unemployment benefit figures for your state.

3.  **Paste and customize the prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into ChatGPT. Carefully replace all the text in the square brackets `[]` with your specific financial details. Do not include the square brackets in your final prompt.

    ```
    I want to know how long my money lasts if my income drops: as I live now, if I cut back moderately, and if I cut everything that isn't fixed. Use current figures for my state's unemployment benefits. Show everything in today's dollars.

    Here are my financial details:
    Filing status: [single / married filing jointly]
    Location: [city, state, e.g., Columbus, Ohio]

    Current monthly income (before job loss): [e.g., $8,000]
    Current monthly expenses: [e.g., $6,000 - list major categories like rent/mortgage, food, transport, utilities, subscriptions, entertainment, etc., or provide a total]

    Savings: [e.g., $50,000 in a checking/savings account]
    Severance amount: [e.g., $15,000]
    401k balance: [e.g., $100,000]

    Number of dependents (children): [e.g., 1]
    Spouse's part-time income (if applicable): [e.g., $1,500 per month]

    Yearly costs (gifts, travel, clothes, car maintenance, etc.) to spread across months: [e.g., $3,000]

    For each of the three scenarios (as I live now, moderate cuts, everything non-fixed cut), tell me:
    1. The month cash runs out.
    2. The month I have to touch retirement (401k).
    3. Exactly what got cut (for the cutback scenarios).

    Please consider:
    - My state's current unemployment benefit and how many weeks it lasts.
    - Marketplace health insurance costs at my new, lower income.
    - The order of draining accounts, including potential tax implications and the 10% penalty for early 401k withdrawal if applicable.
    ```

    You should get a detailed breakdown for each scenario, showing when your cash and retirement funds are projected to run out, along with a list of suggested cuts for the reduced spending scenarios.

4.  **Review the results**: Read through ChatGPT's response carefully. It should provide a month-by-month projection for each of the three spending scenarios, indicating when your cash savings will be depleted and when you might need to access retirement funds. It will also list the specific expenses that would be cut in the moderate and severe reduction scenarios. Use this information to inform your financial planning.

## Notes & variations
*   **Double-check AI calculations**: While AI can be helpful, always verify critical financial figures and advice with a human financial advisor or official government resources, especially regarding taxes, penalties, and specific benefit eligibility. The AI's information might not always be perfectly up-to-date or tailored to every unique situation.
*   **Alternative tools**: Other AI chatbots like Gemini (freemium, https://gemini.google.com) or Claude (freemium, https://claude.ai) could potentially perform similar calculations, though the prompt might need slight adjustments to fit their specific capabilities and web search integration.
*   **Common pitfall**: Not providing enough detail in the prompt. The more specific you are about your income, expenses, and assets, the more accurate and useful ChatGPT's analysis will be. Also, ensure web search is enabled so it can find current unemployment benefit data.
