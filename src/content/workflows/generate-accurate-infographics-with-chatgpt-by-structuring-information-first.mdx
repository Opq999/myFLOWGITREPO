## What you'll get
You will get a structured outline of facts and key concepts, designed specifically to help an AI image generator create a factually accurate infographic. This approach works by forcing the AI to first understand and organize the information, preventing common errors where AI might misrepresent facts in its visuals.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot to help structure information and generate infographic ideas.

## Steps
1.  **Prepare your topic and audience**: Before you start, decide on the topic you want to visualize and who the infographic is for. For example, 'Photosynthesis for 5th-grade students' or 'Blockchain for adult learners'.

2.  **Structure the information with a research prompt**: Copy and paste the prompt below into ChatGPT. Replace `[Change this: ...]` sections with your chosen details. The author's original prompt was cut off, so this version completes the structure based on common infographic elements.

    ```
    Part 1 **Target Audience:** [Change this: e.g., 5th-grade students, high school beginners, adult learners]
    **Topic/Source Material:** [Change this: Describe the Topic/Source Material you wish to visualise Here, e.g., Photosynthesis, Blockchain, Ancient Egypt briefly]

    Act as an expert educational content designer. Thoroughly analyze the [Source Material] aimed for [Target Audience]. Extract and structure the core elements needed to build a highly visual educational infographic or diagram based only on the facts provided in the [Source Material].

    Structure your output exactly as follows so it can be easily plugged into an AI image generator:
    **#Concept Hierarchy:** Define the main theme of the [Source Material].
    **#Core Concepts:** List the essential terms and their brief definitions.
    **#Key Relationships:** Explain how the core concepts connect to each other.
    **#Supporting Details:** Provide important facts, examples, or data points for each concept.
    **#Visual Metaphors/Icons:** Suggest visual ideas or icons that represent each concept or relationship.
    ```

    You should receive a detailed, structured text output, breaking down your topic into clear sections like 'Concept Hierarchy', 'Core Concepts', and 'Visual Metaphors'. This is your factual blueprint.

3.  **Generate your infographic (or its description)**: The author doesn't share their exact prompt for generating the infographic itself. You can use the structured information from the previous step in two ways:

    *   **Option A (using ChatGPT for description):** Ask ChatGPT to describe an infographic based on the structured output. For example:

        ```
        Based on the structured information I just provided, describe a detailed infographic, including its layout, colors, and specific visual elements, suitable for an AI image generator.
        ```

        You will get a text description of what your infographic could look like. You can then copy this description to a dedicated AI image generator.

    *   **Option B (using a dedicated AI image generator):** Copy the structured output directly into an AI image generator (like Midjourney, DALL-E, or Canva's Magic Media) and ask it to create an infographic. You might need to refine the prompt for the specific tool.

        You should see an infographic that visually represents the facts you provided, reducing the chance of factual errors.

## Original source
This workflow was shared by Reddit user /u/telultra, a learning designer, who discovered that AI-generated infographics often contain factual errors. They proposed a simple fix: pre-structure the information using an extra prompt before generating the visual.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives:** Other freemium chatbots like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) can also be used for the information structuring step, as they offer similar text generation capabilities.
*   **Common mistake:** The biggest pitfall is trusting AI-generated visuals without fact-checking. Always review the content of any infographic created by AI, even with this method, to ensure accuracy.
*   **Tip for better results:** Provide as much detailed source material as possible in your initial prompt. The more information ChatGPT has to work with, the more accurate and comprehensive its structured output will be.
