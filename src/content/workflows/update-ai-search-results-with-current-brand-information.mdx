## What you'll get
You will learn how to check what AI chatbots like ChatGPT and Gemini are saying about a business, and identify outdated or incorrect information. This approach helps you understand how AI tools might be misrepresenting a brand and provides a starting point for correcting those inaccuracies.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A popular AI chatbot used here to ask questions about a brand and see what information it provides.
*   **Gemini** (freemium): Google's AI chatbot, used to check for brand information and search relevance.
*   **Pallas** (paid): A tool used to analyze a website and identify potential issues or outdated content.

## Steps
1.  **Check AI Chatbots for Brand Information**: Ask AI chatbots specific questions about the brand you are investigating to see what information they return. For example, ask about products, pricing, or specific services.
    *   For ChatGPT, you might ask:

    ```
    Tell me about [Brand Name] and their serum for sensitive skin. What is the price?
    ```

    *   For Gemini, you might ask:

    ```
    What is [Brand Name]'s best serum for sensitive skin?
    ```
    You should see responses that may contain outdated pricing, discontinued products, or incorrect service recommendations.

2.  **Analyze the Website with Pallas**: Use a website analysis tool like Pallas to scan the brand's website for issues. The author used Pallas to check the site after noticing discrepancies in the AI responses.
    *   The author does not share their exact steps for using Pallas; a starting point would be to input the brand's website URL into Pallas and run an analysis.
    You should get a report detailing website content, product listings, and potential SEO issues.

3.  **Compare AI Responses to Website Analysis**: Review the information provided by the AI chatbots and compare it with the findings from your website analysis. Look for specific instances where the AI has outdated pricing, mentions discontinued products, or misrepresents the brand's offerings.
    You should be able to pinpoint exactly where the AI is getting its incorrect information from.

4.  **Formulate a Response to the Client**: Based on your findings, explain to the client that the issues stem from outdated information that was not part of your original work. Clearly state that changes made after your engagement are likely the cause of the AI's errors.
    You should have a clear explanation ready to send to the client, outlining the problem and your findings.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternative**: If Pallas is too expensive, you can manually check the brand's website for outdated product listings and pricing. You can also use Google Search to see how the brand ranks for relevant terms and check for old cached pages.
*   **Common mistake**: Assuming the AI is always correct. AI chatbots can easily pull and present outdated information if the source material they were trained on is not current.
*   **Tip for better results**: When asking AI chatbots questions, be as specific as possible. Instead of asking generally about the brand, ask about specific products, services, or pricing to get more targeted and potentially more accurate (or clearly inaccurate) responses.
