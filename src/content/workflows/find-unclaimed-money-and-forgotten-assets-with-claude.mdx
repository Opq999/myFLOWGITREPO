## What you'll get
You'll receive a personalized list of potential unclaimed money or forgotten assets, including details like the source, amount (if available), and direct links to start the claim process. This approach works by using an AI chatbot to efficiently search and organize information from various official databases, making it simpler to uncover money you might have forgotten about.

## Tools you need
*   **Claude** (freemium): An AI chatbot that helps you search, summarize, and organize information from various sources.
*   **MissingMoney.com** (free): An official, multi-state database in the United States for searching unclaimed property.

## Steps
1.  **Gather your personal information**: Before you start, collect all relevant names you've used (e.g., full name, maiden name, old names) and a complete list of every state or place you have lived. Also, list all your past employers.

2.  **Ask Claude to search for unclaimed property**: Open Claude (or your preferred AI chatbot) and paste the following prompt. Remember to replace the bracketed placeholders `[ ]` with your actual information.

    ```
    Search my state's and my country's official unclaimed property databases for money listed under my name, and check every state I've ever lived in too. Use official sources like MissingMoney.com and each state's treasury site. My name is [full name], also check [maiden name or any past names]. I've lived in [list every state or place]. For anything you find, give me the source, the amount if shown, and the link to start the claim. The names and places matter more than anything else here. Unclaimed property is filed by name and location, so an old address or a maiden name is often the entire difference between a match and a miss. Give it every version of your name and every place you've lived.
    ```

    You should get a list of potential unclaimed properties, including the source (e.g., a state treasury site), any listed amount, and a link to begin the claim process. The AI might also suggest other official sites to check.

3.  **Ask Claude to search for other forgotten assets**: In the same chat with Claude, paste the following prompt. Again, replace `[list]` with your actual past employers.

    ```
    Now check the places everyone forgets. Look for old 401(k)s or pensions from past jobs, unclaimed tax refunds, and any class-action settlements I might qualify for. My past employers were: [list]. Point me to the official tool to search each one, tell me what I likely qualify for, and exactly how to claim it. Class actions especially are worth running…
    ```

    Claude will provide information on where to search for these types of assets, such as official government tools for tax refunds or specific websites for pension searches, and explain how to proceed with a claim.

4.  **Review and act on findings**: Carefully review all the information provided by Claude. Click on the links to official sources and follow the instructions to verify any potential claims and start the process of recovering your money. Be cautious of any unofficial sites or requests for personal information that seem suspicious.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can achieve similar results using other freemium AI chatbots like ChatGPT (at chatgpt.com) or Gemini (at gemini.google.com) by adapting the prompts slightly.
*   **Common mistake**: The most common pitfall is not providing enough detail. The more names (including maiden names or old names) and locations (every state or place you've lived) you give the AI, the better its chances of finding a match. Be as thorough as possible.
*   **Tip for better results**: After getting initial results, you can ask Claude follow-up questions, such as "Can you find the official unclaimed property website for [specific state]?" or "What are the typical steps to claim a forgotten 401(k) from [past employer]?" This helps you get more precise information for your specific situation.
