## What you'll get
You will get an AI-generated website that looks more unique and professionally designed, rather than a generic template. This approach works by avoiding the common default fonts and pure white backgrounds that make many AI-built sites look cheap, helping your site stand out.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chatbot** (freemium): To give instructions for building your website with specific design choices.
*   **Google Fonts** (free): To explore and confirm the font names mentioned in the recipe, or find alternatives.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI Chatbot**: Go to your preferred AI chatbot (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Paste the design rules and your request**: Copy the following text and paste it into the chatbot. Remember to replace the placeholder `[Then tell it what to build, e.g., "Build me a simple portfolio website for a freelance graphic designer, showcasing my best work and contact information."]` with your actual request for the website content and structure.

    ```
    Two hard rules for this build:
    1. Do not use Inter, Roboto, Arial, or any system font. Use "Fraunces" for headings and "Source Sans 3" for body, both from Google Fonts.
    2. Never use pure white (#FFFFFF) as the page background. Use a warm off-white: #FAFAF8. Also: no purple, no gradients. One accent colour only, a warm ochre #B0731F, used sparingly.

    [Then tell it what to build, e.g., "Build me a simple portfolio website for a freelance graphic designer, showcasing my best work and contact information."]
    ```

    The chatbot will begin processing your request, aiming to generate a website design that follows your specific font and color instructions.

3.  **Review the AI's output**: Look at the website design or code the AI provides. Check if it has used the specified fonts (Fraunces for headings, Source Sans 3 for body) and the warm off-white background (#FAFAF8) with the ochre accent color (#B0731F).
    You should see a design that avoids the typical AI-generated look, with a more intentional and professional feel.

4.  **Refine if needed**: If the AI didn't perfectly follow the instructions, you can ask it to make adjustments. For example, "Please ensure the background is #FAFAF8 and not pure white."
    Continue refining until the design meets your expectations for fonts and colors.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can use other freemium AI chatbots like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) to achieve similar results, as they also allow for detailed instructions.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is forgetting to include the design rules *before* your main request for the website content. Always provide the design constraints first so the AI incorporates them from the start.
*   **Tip for better results**: Experiment with other font pairings from Google Fonts and different warm color palettes. The key is to avoid the most common default choices and create a unique visual identity for your site.
