## What you'll get
You'll learn four specific ChatGPT prompts designed to help you resume any plan (like a business project, study schedule, or fitness routine) without feeling overwhelmed. These prompts make it easy to pick up exactly where you left off, even after a break or when you have low energy. This approach tackles the common challenge of losing momentum, helping you stay consistent by breaking down re-engagement into small, manageable steps.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A conversational AI tool to generate responses based on your prompts.

## Steps
1.  **Leave a "breadcrumb" before stopping**: Before you end your work session, open ChatGPT and use this prompt, replacing `[your plan]` with a brief description of what you're working on.

    ```
    I'm stopping for today. In one line, write where I left off on [your plan] and the next step, small enough to start in 5 minutes. Nothing else.
    ```

    You should get a single, concise line describing your stopping point and the very next tiny action. Save this line somewhere you'll easily see it tomorrow, like in your phone's notes app or as an alarm label.

2.  **Get the next step on a fresh day**: The next day, open ChatGPT (you can use a new chat or the old one) and paste this prompt. Replace `[paste yesterday's line here]` with the "breadcrumb" line you saved from Step 1.

    ```
    Don't recap the plan. Give me only the next step, small enough to start in 5 minutes, and tell me I'm allowed to stop after it. Yesterday I stopped here: [paste yesterday's line here]
    ```

    ChatGPT will give you just one small, actionable step for today, without reviewing your entire plan. This makes starting less daunting.

3.  **Re-engage after a few days away**: If you've been away from your plan for several days, use this prompt in ChatGPT. Replace `[your plan]` with a brief description of your plan.

    ```
    I haven't touched [your plan] in a few days. Don't make me catch up and don't recap the plan. Push back or drop whatever no longer fits, then give me one step for today.
    ```

    ChatGPT will adjust your plan, if needed, and provide a single, manageable step for you to take today, without making you feel guilty about missed days. The author's test resulted in a 5-minute walk.

4.  **Shrink a step on a low-energy day**: When you feel low on energy but still want to make progress, use this prompt. Replace `[today's step]` with the specific task you're facing.

    ```
    My energy is low today. What's the smallest version of [today's step] that still counts?
    ```

    You'll receive a much smaller, easier version of your task, ensuring you can still make progress even when you're tired. A small step is always better than skipping entirely.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can use other freemium chat AIs like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) for similar results, as the core idea is prompt engineering for consistency.
*   **Common mistake or pitfall**: A common pitfall is that ChatGPT tends to agree with whatever plan you give it. If a suggested step still feels too big, even after using the "shrink it" prompt, explicitly tell ChatGPT to make it even smaller.
*   **Tip for better results**: To get the best results, be as specific as possible when describing your plan or the step you need help with. The more detail you provide, the better ChatGPT can tailor its suggestions.
