## What you'll get
You will learn how to tailor your resume by directly incorporating keywords from a job description. This method helps your resume get past automated systems (Applicant Tracking Systems or ATS) that scan for specific terms, making it more likely to be seen by a human recruiter. It works because ATS are programmed to look for exact matches between the job posting and the resume.

## Tools you need
*   **Text Editor or Word Processor** (free): To write and edit your resume. This could be Google Docs, Microsoft Word, or even a simple notes app on your phone.
*   **AI Chat Assistant** (freemium): Tools like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini can help you identify and integrate keywords. You'll use their free tier.

## Steps
1.  **Find a job description**: Locate a job posting for a role you are interested in. Copy the entire text of the job description.
2.  **Identify keywords with an AI assistant**: Paste the job description into your AI chat assistant and ask it to extract the most important keywords and phrases. The author of this tip used the literal phrasing from the job description.

    ```
    Please extract the most important skills, keywords, and phrases from the following job description. Focus on the exact wording used in the posting:

    [Paste the full job description here]
    ```

    You should get a list of terms that are frequently used in the job description. Review this list to ensure it captures the core requirements.
3.  **Update your resume**: Open your resume in your text editor or word processor. Carefully read through the list of keywords you got from the AI assistant. Integrate these exact keywords and phrases into your resume where they naturally fit, especially in your skills section, experience descriptions, and summary. Do not just use similar words; use the literal phrasing from the job description.

    You should end up with a resume that closely matches the language used in the job posting.

## Original source
This tip comes from a Reddit post by user /u/Bishoprizo, who shared their experience of improving their resume's effectiveness by matching keywords directly from job descriptions. They found this small change significantly increased their interview callback rate.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: Avoid simply listing keywords without context. Ensure they are woven into your experience and skills descriptions naturally. Overstuffing can look unnatural.
*   **Tip for better results**: After integrating the keywords, read your resume aloud. This helps you catch awkward phrasing and ensures it still flows well for a human reader.
*   **Free alternative**: If you don't want to use an AI assistant, you can manually read through the job description and highlight key terms yourself. This takes more time but is completely free.
