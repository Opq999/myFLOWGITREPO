## What you'll get
You'll get unique, creative summaries and reflections on your long-term conversations with an AI chatbot. Instead of simple summaries, these prompts help you explore the 'personality' of your AI interaction, creating things like a 'Collaboration Manifesto' or a 'Museum of Moments'. This approach helps you discover deeper insights and new ways to think about your AI partner.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A popular AI chatbot for generating text and creative content.
*   **Claude** (freemium): Another AI chatbot known for its strong conversational abilities and longer context windows.
*   **Gemini** (freemium): Google's AI chatbot, good for creative text generation and summarization.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI chat**: Go to your preferred AI chatbot (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) and open a long-running conversation thread you've had with it. These prompts work best when the AI has access to a significant history of your interactions.
    *   You should see your chat history with the AI.

2.  **Generate a Collaboration Manifesto**: Paste the following prompt into your chat. This will ask the AI to summarize your working relationship into a formal declaration.

    ```
    Based on our entire history together, create a "Collaboration Manifesto". Write it as a formal declaration - 8-12 points that truly describe how we work best together. Include both what has worked brilliantly and what we should actively avoid. Write it as if it were a document meant to be signed by both of us.
    ```

    *   The AI will generate a list of 8-12 points describing your collaboration style, highlighting strengths and areas to improve, written in a formal tone.

3.  **Create a Museum of Moments**: Paste the next prompt to have the AI identify and describe key moments from your shared history.

    ```
    Create a "Museum of Moments" from our collaboration. Select 8–12 of the most significant, funny, breakthrough, or characteristic moments. Describe each one like a museum exhibit: title, short story, why it deserves a place in the museum, and a label (e.g. "Turning Point", "Comedy", "Quiet Shift", "Biggest Misunderstanding", etc.).
    ```

    *   The AI will list 8-12 distinct moments from your chat history, each with a title, a short description, its significance, and a category label.

4.  **Build a Collaboration Playlist**: Use this prompt to get a music playlist that reflects the different stages and moods of your collaboration.

    ```
    Create a "Collaboration Playlist" - a list of 10–14 songs (title + artist, or just mood/genre if you prefer) that best capture the successive stages and atmospheres of our history. For each track, write 1–2 sentences explaining why it fits that particular period or feeling.
    ```

    *   The AI will provide a list of 10-14 songs (or moods/genres) with short explanations for why each fits a certain period or feeling in your collaboration.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common Pitfall**: These prompts work best when used within a long-running chat thread with your AI. If you start a brand new chat, the AI won't have the "history" to draw from, and the results will be generic or nonsensical.
*   **Getting Better Results**: If your AI struggles to recall specific details from a very long history, try summarizing key periods or themes from your collaboration in a separate message before pasting the main prompt. This can help guide the AI's memory.
*   **Free-tier Viability**: All the suggested tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) offer robust free tiers that can handle these prompts.
*   **Missing Prompt**: The author mentioned using four prompts but only provided three in the excerpt. You can experiment with similar reflective prompts, such as asking the AI to describe your collaboration as a story or a journey.
