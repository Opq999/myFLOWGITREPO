## How it actually works

1.  **Set up Custom Instructions in ChatGPT**: These are rules you give ChatGPT that apply to every conversation. They define how the AI should generally behave, such as when to remember project details, when to save information, how to tell ideas apart from final decisions, and when it needs your explicit approval before acting. You can find Custom Instructions in your ChatGPT settings.

2.  **Use ChatGPT as a "Library"**: This component acts like a temporary working memory or a quick-access file system within ChatGPT itself. It's where project context, temporary notes, pending items, and consolidated information are stored for fast retrieval. The author doesn't specify the exact method for this "library," but it implies using specific chat threads or a dedicated chat for memory storage that ChatGPT can reference.

3.  **Store permanent data in Google Drive**: This is your long-term, reliable storage. Important operating rules, final decisions, and official project documents are kept here. If any temporary information in the ChatGPT "Library" is lost, the critical, durable information should still be safe and recoverable in Google Drive.

4.  **Implement "Scheduled ChatGPT Work tasks"**: These are recurring tasks that prompt ChatGPT to periodically read, organize, combine, and verify the information it holds. The author mentions these tasks but does not detail *how* they are scheduled or what specific prompts are used. This would likely involve setting reminders to manually prompt ChatGPT or using external automation tools if you have the technical skills.

5.  **Connect the components**: The core idea is that these parts don't work in isolation. Your Custom Instructions guide ChatGPT on how to use its "Library" and when to refer to or store information in Google Drive. The scheduled tasks ensure the "Library" stays organized and up-to-date. ## Notes & variations
*   **Do you even need this?** For simpler project management or occasional research, a single ChatGPT chat or a basic note-taking app might be sufficient. This advanced system is best for those who manage complex, ongoing projects and need a more integrated AI assistant.
*   **Free-tier limits**: While ChatGPT and Google Drive offer free tiers, heavy usage of ChatGPT might eventually lead to slower responses or encourage upgrading to a paid plan for better performance. Google Drive's free storage is 15GB, which is usually ample for text documents.
*   **Common pitfall**: Over-reliance on ChatGPT's "working memory" without a robust permanent storage solution (like Google Drive) can lead to lost information if chats are deleted or context is forgotten. Always ensure critical data is backed up externally.
