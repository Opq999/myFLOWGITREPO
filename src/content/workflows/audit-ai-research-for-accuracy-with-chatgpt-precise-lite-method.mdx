## What you'll get
You will learn a four-stage method to conduct AI research, verify sources, and audit reports for accuracy. This approach helps ensure that every claim in your report is directly supported by evidence from reliable sources, preventing overstatements or unsupported conclusions.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot used to guide you through a structured research and auditing process.

## Steps
1.  **Set up ChatGPT for PRECISE LITE**: Instruct ChatGPT on its role and the methodology. This prompt tells ChatGPT to act as a research assistant and follow the PRECISE LITE method, which is limited to one search loop and up to 3 sources.

    ```
    You are an AI research assistant using the PRECISE LITE methodology. Your goal is to help me conduct rigorous research, verify sources, and audit reports for accuracy. We will follow four stages: Clarify, Remix & Sharpen, Research & Verify, and Deliver & Audit. PRECISE LITE is limited to one search loop and up to 3 sources. Please acknowledge this and then start by asking me to define my research topic, audience, purpose, intended use, constraints, date range, and desired source types.
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT acknowledge its role and ask you for the details of your research project.

2.  **Clarify your research needs**: Provide ChatGPT with the specific details of your research. Fill in the bracketed sections with your own information.

    ```
    My research topic is: [Your specific topic, e.g., "The impact of AI on small businesses in Nigeria"].
    My audience is: [e.g., "Small business owners and freelancers"].
    My purpose is: [e.g., "To provide actionable insights for adopting AI tools"].
    Intended use: [e.g., "A short guide or blog post"].
    Constraints: [e.g., "Focus on free or freemium tools, no complex technical jargon"].
    Date range: [e.g., "Last 2 years (2022-2024)"].
    Source types: [e.g., "Reputable business blogs, industry reports, news articles"].
    ```

    ChatGPT should confirm it understands your request and indicate it's ready to move to the next stage, or ask clarifying questions if needed.

3.  **Remix and sharpen the request**: Ask ChatGPT to refine your clarified topic into a precise research mission. This helps focus your search efforts.

    ```
    Based on my clarification, please remix and sharpen this into a precise retrieval mission. Do not change my original intent, add new scope, or drop any explicit constraints.
    ```

    You should get a refined research question or a set of precise search terms. For example: "Research Question: What are the practical, free or freemium AI tools that Nigerian small businesses and freelancers can adopt, and what are their specific impacts, based on sources from 2022-2024?"

4.  **Research and verify sources (User-led)**: This step requires you to find and review sources outside of ChatGPT. The AI will then help you process the information.

    *   **Find sources**: Use the sharpened request from ChatGPT to search online (e.g., Google, Bing). Look for up to 3 reputable sources that directly address your refined research question.
    *   **Read and extract facts**: Open each source, read it carefully, and identify specific facts, figures, or claims that directly support your research. For each fact, note down the exact wording and the source (e.g., "According to [Source Name], [Fact]").
    *   **Provide facts to ChatGPT**: Return to ChatGPT and provide the extracted facts, making sure to cite each one clearly. The author doesn't share their exact prompt; a starting point:

    ```
    I have found and reviewed up to 3 sources. Here are the key facts and their sources: 

    Source 1 (Title of Article, Website Name, Date, URL):
    - Fact 1: [Exact quote or summarized fact]
    - Fact 2: [Exact quote or summarized fact]

    Source 2 (Title of Article, Website Name, Date, URL):
    - Fact 1: [Exact quote or summarized fact]
    - Fact 2: [Exact quote or summarized fact]

    Source 3 (Title of Article, Website Name, Date, URL):
    - Fact 1: [Exact quote or summarized fact]
    - Fact 2: [Exact quote or summarized fact]

    Please review these facts. For each fact, confirm if it directly supports our research question. Identify any gaps or limitations in the evidence provided for our overall research goal.
    ```

    ChatGPT should process the facts, confirm their relevance, and point out any areas where the evidence might be insufficient for your original goal.

5.  **Deliver and audit the report**: Ask ChatGPT to draft a report based only on the verified facts, and then to audit its own work for accuracy against those facts.

    ```
    Now, please draft a short report or summary based *only* on the verified evidence I provided in the previous step. Do not introduce new information or make claims not directly supported by the sources.

    Once the draft is complete, please audit every heading and factual sentence against the evidence I provided. Point out any wording that changes the actor, action, scope, date, number, attribution, or level of certainty. Suggest corrections or removals where necessary.
    ```

    You will first get a draft report from ChatGPT. Following that, you should receive an audit of the report, highlighting any unsupported claims or inaccuracies based on the facts you provided.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is to over-rely on the AI to find and verify sources. Remember that ChatGPT's free tier may not always have up-to-date web browsing, and even when it does, critically reading the original sources yourself is crucial for true accuracy. The AI is a guide and auditor, not a replacement for your critical thinking.
*   **Tip for better results**: Always read the original sources yourself to fully understand the context and nuances of the information. The AI helps structure your research and audit for consistency, but your human judgment is essential for evaluating source quality and relevance.
*   **For more comprehensive research**: The author mentions a full PRECISE version designed for more extensive research and tailored deliverables. While PRECISE LITE is a free demo, the full version would likely involve more advanced features or a paid service, though details are not provided in the source.
