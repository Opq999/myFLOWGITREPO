    *   Testing the chatbot with sample questions.
    You should see a chatbot interface where you can interact with your AI.
4.  **Automate production tasks**: For production, the author describes a process where motherboards are connected to a cable, and software scans for new devices, flashes software, and performs quality checks. ## Notes & variations
*   **Free tier limitations**: The free tier of Claude.ai may have usage limits. If you experience high volumes of customer queries, you might need to consider a paid plan or manage usage carefully.
*   **Common pitfall**: Relying solely on the AI without a comprehensive and accurate knowledge base will lead to incorrect answers. Ensure your information is up-to-date and covers most scenarios.
*   **Tip for better results**: Clearly define the persona and tone for your customer support chatbot. For example, instruct it to be friendly, professional, and empathetic. For production tasks, break down the repetitive job into the smallest possible steps and ask Claude to help script or describe each one.
