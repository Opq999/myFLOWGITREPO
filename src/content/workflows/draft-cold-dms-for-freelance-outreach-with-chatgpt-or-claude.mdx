## What you'll get
Ready-to-send messages for initial client outreach, designed to open a conversation without immediately pitching your services. This approach uses AI to draft professional, concise, and human-sounding communication, saving time and reducing stress for freelancers.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot for generating text and conversations.
*   **Claude** (freemium): Another AI chatbot known for its conversational abilities and longer context windows.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI chat tool**: Go to either ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) or Claude (claude.ai) in your web browser or mobile app. You will need to sign in or create a free account.

    *You should see the chat interface ready for you to type a message.*

2.  **Draft a cold DM opener**: Copy the prompt below. Replace the bracketed placeholders `[PLATFORM]`, `[PROSPECT_NAME]`, `[OBSERVATION]`, and `[YOUR_FIELD]` with your specific details. Then, paste the complete prompt into the chat tool and send it.

    ```
    You are a freelancer writing a first-touch DM to a potential client on [PLATFORM: LinkedIn/Instagram]. You are NOT pitching yet — only opening a conversation. Inputs: - Prospect's name: [PROSPECT_NAME] - Something specific and real you noticed about them (a post, their business, a recent launch): [OBSERVATION] - Your area of work (one line, no pitch): [YOUR_FIELD] Write a DM under 40 words that: 1. References [OBSERVATION] specifically — not a generic compliment. 2. Asks ONE genuine question related to [OBSERVATION]. 3. Does NOT mention your services, price, or availability. 4. Sounds like a real person typing on their phone, not a copywriter. Output the DM only.
    ```

    *You should get a short, personal message under 40 words, ready to send to a potential client without sounding like a sales pitch. The author mentioned sharing three prompts, but only this one was included in the provided excerpt.*

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: Not being specific enough when filling in the bracketed information. Generic inputs lead to generic outputs. Ensure `[OBSERVATION]` is a real, unique detail about the prospect.
*   **Tip for better results**: Experiment with the tone. If the output sounds too formal, you can add a line to the prompt like "Make it sound more casual" or "Use a friendly tone." Always review the AI's output to ensure it matches your personal style before sending.
*   **Free-tier viability**: Both ChatGPT and Claude offer robust free tiers that are perfectly capable of handling this text-based workflow without needing paid upgrades.
