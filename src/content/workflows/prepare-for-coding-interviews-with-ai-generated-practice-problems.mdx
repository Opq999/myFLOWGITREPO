## What you'll get
You will get a set of practice coding problems tailored to your needs, generated by an AI. This approach helps you learn at a comfortable pace by starting with easier problems and gradually increasing the difficulty, building confidence without feeling overwhelmed.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A conversational AI tool that can generate text, answer questions, and create custom content like practice problems.

## Steps
1.  **Start a new chat**: Open the ChatGPT website or app and begin a new conversation.
2.  **Ask for practice problems**: Tell ChatGPT what kind of coding problems you want to practice. Be specific about the topic and difficulty level. For example, you can ask for 2-3 easy problems on a specific data structure or algorithm.

    ```
    I have a coding interview coming up and need to practice. Please give me 3 easy problems related to array manipulation. I want to focus on problems that require iterating through the array and performing simple operations.
    ```

    You should see a list of coding problems generated by ChatGPT.
3.  **Solve the problems**: Work through each problem one by one. The author suggests aiming for 2 to 3 problems per day, depending on their difficulty. As you solve them, pay attention to where you get stuck. Common friction points include not fully understanding the problem, writing too much code at once, self-doubt, or getting flustered by bugs.
4.  **Reflect on your process**: After attempting a problem, think about what made it difficult. The author recommends noting down assumptions, verifying your setup, manually tracing the algorithm, and talking through any roadblocks out loud. This helps you understand your thought process and identify areas for improvement.
5.  **Consider recording yourself**: For advanced practice, the author suggests recording yourself while solving problems. You can then watch the footage back to evaluate your pacing, how you explain your thinking, and your overall presence. This step is optional but can provide valuable insights.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free tier alternative**: The free tier of ChatGPT is sufficient for generating practice problems. Ensure you are using the main ChatGPT interface (chat.openai.com) and not a paid developer product.
*   **Common mistake**: Trying to solve overly complex problems too early can be discouraging. Start with easier problems and gradually increase the difficulty as your confidence grows.
*   **Tip for better results**: When asking ChatGPT for problems, be as specific as possible about the concepts you want to practice. For instance, instead of just asking for "algorithm problems," ask for "problems involving dynamic programming" or "questions about linked lists."
