## What you'll get
A detailed analysis of your communication style, thinking patterns, and interaction habits, based on your past conversations with the AI. This approach helps you understand how you present yourself, focusing on your actual behavior and language without relying on generic personality labels.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chatbot** (freemium): A conversational AI like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini to analyze your past interactions.

## Steps
1.  **Choose your AI Chatbot**: Open your preferred AI chatbot (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) on your phone or computer. Ensure you are using a chat where you have a significant conversation history with the AI. You should see the chat interface ready for a new conversation.

2.  **Paste the analysis prompt**: Copy the full prompt below and paste it into the chatbot. This prompt instructs the AI to analyze your past conversations with it.

    ```
    GOAL: Conduct a full-spectrum audit of how I come across across our past conversations. Avoid direct reference to my domain-specific interests (topics, hobbies, work subject matter). Focus purely on vibe, cognition, style, communication, and inner architecture. Extract truth and tension, not praise or surface observation.

    METHOD (mandatory): Before writing any section, search across our conversation history for concrete instances: specific phrasings, decisions, moments of pushback or avoidance, recurring patterns. Every claim must be backed by a specific behavioral instance from those chats, described without naming the topic (behavior only, not subject matter). Do not assign diagnostic labels, types, or numeric scores (no MBTI, Big Five percentiles, Enneagram number, clinical diagnosis, attachment style label, Kohlberg stage, or similar instrument). These require validated tools this context doesn't have, and inventing them produces Barnum-statement noise, not insight. Where a scale is genuinely useful (e.g. "how directive vs. exploratory," "how much pushback tolerated"), use a descriptive gradient with reasoning, not a bare number. Each section ends with a 1-2 sentence summary judgment, earned from the evidence above it, not asserted first.
    ```

3.  **Review the analysis**: Read through the chatbot's response. It should provide detailed observations about your communication style, backed by examples from your past chats. The analysis will focus on your behavior and language patterns, not specific topics.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can use the free tiers of popular AI chatbots like ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), or Gemini (gemini.google.com) for this analysis.
*   **Common pitfall**: This prompt relies on the AI having access to your past conversations. If you start a new chat, the AI will not have enough context to perform the analysis. Ensure you use a chat session with a significant history, or paste relevant conversation snippets if your chatbot allows it.
*   **Tip for better results**: The more conversation history the AI has with you, the richer and more accurate its analysis will be. Try to use a chat session that spans several days or weeks of interaction.
