## What you'll get
You will get a faster, more reliable home internet connection, especially useful for tasks like live streaming or video calls. This approach works by using an AI chatbot to guide you through common troubleshooting steps, helping you find simple fixes before spending money on expensive repairs or upgrades.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can guide you through troubleshooting steps and suggest solutions.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI assistant**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface, ready for your input.

2.  **Describe your internet problem**: Start a new chat and explain what's wrong with your internet connection. Be specific about where the problem occurs (e.g., "upstairs office") and what devices are affected.

    ```
    Something is wrong with my internet. Can you check my PC? My office is two floors up from the router, and I'm getting slow speeds like 22 Mbps down.
    ```

    The AI will likely ask you follow-up questions about your setup or suggest initial checks.

3.  **Follow troubleshooting advice**: The AI will guide you through steps. The original author was advised to restart their Google mesh points (small devices that extend Wi-Fi coverage). You might be asked to restart your main router, Wi-Fi extenders, or mesh devices.
    *   **Action**: Locate your main internet router and any Wi-Fi extenders or mesh points. Unplug them from power, wait 30 seconds, then plug them back in. Wait a few minutes for them to fully restart.
    *   **Action**: Test your internet speed again in the problem area. You can search "internet speed test" on Google and use one of the free tools.
    You should see an improvement in your internet speed, or the AI will suggest further steps based on your new speed test results. The author's speed went from 22 Mbps to 219 Mbps down after this step.

4.  **Discuss permanent solutions (optional)**: If you still need more speed or a more stable connection (e.g., for live streaming), ask the AI about long-term solutions like wired connections.

    ```
    I'm doing more livestreams now. Should I look into getting Ethernet hardwired to my office?
    ```

    The AI will explain the benefits of a wired connection and discuss if it's a good option for your situation.

5.  **Estimate costs (optional)**: If a wired connection seems like the right path, you can ask the AI for a general idea of potential costs.

    ```
    That's going to be expensive. How much do you think it'll cost to hardwire Ethernet to my office?
    ```

    The AI will provide general cost estimates or advise you on how to get accurate quotes from local contractors.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can also use other freemium AI chatbots like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) for similar troubleshooting guidance.
*   **Common mistake**: Don't skip testing your internet speed after each change. This helps you know if a step worked and gives the AI accurate information for the next suggestion.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific as possible when describing your problem and your home setup (e.g., "My router is in the basement, and my office is two floors up"). This helps the AI give more tailored advice.
