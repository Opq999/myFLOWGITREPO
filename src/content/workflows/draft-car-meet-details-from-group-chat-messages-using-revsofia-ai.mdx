## What you'll get
You will get a clear, organized summary of a car meet, including the date, time, meeting point, destination, who is coming, and a ready-to-paste announcement. This approach uses an AI model to quickly extract key information from messy group chat messages, saving you time and ensuring all important details are clear for participants.

## Tools you need
*   **RevSofia Planner** (free): A web tool that uses AI to turn group chat messages into structured car meet details.
*   **Discord** (freemium): A popular chat app where group discussions about car meets often happen.

## Steps
1.  **Open the RevSofia Planner**: Go to the planner website.

    Open your web browser and navigate to `https://georgievalex.github.io/revsofia/plan.html`.

    You should see a page titled "RevSofia Planner" with a large text box for chat messages and a "Draft the meet" button.

2.  **Paste your group chat messages**: Copy the relevant chat history.

    Copy the group chat messages where the car meet details were discussed (from Discord or any other chat app). Paste them into the large text box on the RevSofia Planner page. The tool supports both English and Bulgarian chats.

    ```
    Ники: Кой е за Витоша в неделя сутринта?
    Мария: Аз! Идвам с голфа
    Иво: и аз, с 335-ицата. В колко?
    Ники: 9:30 на бензиностанцията на Симеоновско шосе
    Мария: окей, после кафе на Златните мостове
    Стоян: тази неделя не мога, следващия път
    ```

    Your chat messages should now appear in the text box.

3.  **Confirm you are human**: Tick the security box.

    Below the text box, find and tick the checkbox labeled "I'm human".

    The checkbox should now be marked.

4.  **Draft the meet details**: Click the button to process the chat.

    Click the "Draft the meet" button.

    After a few seconds (the author notes it took about 5.4 seconds in a test), the page will display the drafted meet details. This includes the start time, meeting point, destination, who is coming, the car club, any undecided items, and an announcement ready to paste.

    ```
    Start: Sunday 11 Oct, 09:30 (Sofia time)
    Meeting point: the petrol station on Simeonovsko shose
    Destination: Zlatnite Mostove
    Coming: Niki, Maria (Golf), Ivo (BMW 335i). Stoyan said he can't make it, so he's correctly not on the list.
    Club: General, because it's mixed brands
    Still undecided: what time it ends
    Announcement: in Bulgarian, ready to paste
    ```

5.  **Review and use the drafted details**: Check for accuracy and share.

    Read through the generated details carefully. The author notes you should "fix whatever it got wrong" before sharing. Once you are satisfied, copy the "Announcement" or any other details you need.

    You now have a clear, structured summary of your car meet, ready to share with your group.

## Original source
This workflow is based on the "RevSofia" car meetup app, created by alexgeorgiev17 and shared on the DEV Community platform. It was developed as a submission for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: The AI works best with clear, direct chat messages. If your chat is very long or contains many unrelated topics, the AI might struggle to extract the correct details. Try to paste only the most relevant parts of the conversation.
*   **Tip for better results**: Always review the AI's output. The tool is designed to provide a draft, and a human touch is needed to ensure all details are perfectly accurate before publishing. The author emphasizes that nothing is published until a human approves it.
*   **Free-tier viability**: The RevSofia Planner is hosted on GitHub Pages, making it completely free to use. The underlying AI model (Gemma 4) is an open-weight model, meaning it's freely available for use in this context.
