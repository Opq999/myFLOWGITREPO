## What you'll get
You will get a revised resume that highlights your skills and experience more effectively, increasing your chances of getting interview calls. This approach works by using AI to analyze your current resume and suggest improvements based on common hiring practices.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A chatbot that can help you rewrite and improve your resume content.
*   **Overleaf** (freemium): A platform for creating and editing LaTeX documents, often used for resumes.

## Steps
1.  **Copy your current resume**: Open your resume in Overleaf and copy all the text.
2.  **Ask ChatGPT for feedback**: Go to ChatGPT and paste your resume text. Ask for specific improvements.

    ```
    Please review my resume and suggest improvements to help me get more interview calls. Focus on making my skills and experience clearer and more impactful. Here is my resume:
    [Paste your resume text here]
    ```

    You should get a detailed response from ChatGPT with suggestions for rephrasing, adding or removing content, and improving the overall structure.
3.  **Incorporate suggestions**: Carefully review ChatGPT's suggestions. Decide which ones to use and edit your resume in Overleaf accordingly. You might want to ask follow-up questions to ChatGPT if you need clarification or further ideas.

## Original source
This workflow is inspired by a Reddit user who was seeking feedback on their resume created in Overleaf to improve their job application success. The user shared their resume for review to get advice on potential improvements and alternative templates.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternative**: If you don't have access to Overleaf's premium features or want to try a different editor, you can use Google Docs, which also has free resume templates.
*   **Common mistake**: Do not blindly copy all AI suggestions. Always use your judgment to ensure the changes accurately reflect your experience and sound like you.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be specific in your prompts to ChatGPT. For example, you can ask it to "rewrite the 'experience' section to emphasize quantifiable achievements" or "suggest stronger action verbs for my bullet points."
