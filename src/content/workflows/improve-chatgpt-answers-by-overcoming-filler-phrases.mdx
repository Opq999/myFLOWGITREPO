## What you'll get
You will learn to recognize common phrases that ChatGPT uses to avoid giving a direct answer. For each phrase, you'll get a follow-up prompt to ask for more specific information, leading to better, more actionable responses. This approach works because these phrases are signals that ChatGPT is holding back crucial details, and the follow-up prompts are designed to extract those missing details.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A conversational AI tool that generates human-like text responses based on user prompts.

## Steps
1.  **Start a conversation with ChatGPT**: Open ChatGPT in your web browser and begin a new chat.
2.  **Ask a question that might elicit a non-committal answer**: For example, ask about a topic that has many variables or has changed recently.
3.  **Identify filler phrases**: Look for phrases like "It depends," "Generally," "As of my last update," "Here's a simplified example," or "There are several approaches you could take."
4.  **Respond with a clarifying prompt**: Based on the filler phrase used, paste one of the following prompts into ChatGPT:
    *   If ChatGPT says "It depends.":
        ```
        Assume the most common version of my situation and answer for that. Then tell me the one detail that would change your answer.
        ```
    *   If ChatGPT says "Generally", "In most cases", or "Typically":
        ```
        Which parts of that apply to what I told you, and which parts are just the general rule?
        ```
    *   If ChatGPT says "As of my last update":
        ```
        Search for this and tell me what's changed since then.
        ```
    *   If ChatGPT says "Here's a simplified example":
        ```
        What did the simplified version leave out that I'll hit when I do this for real?
        ```
    *   If ChatGPT says "There are several approaches you could take":
        ```
        Pick one for my case and tell me why not the others.
        ```
    You should receive a more specific and helpful answer from ChatGPT.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternative**: This workflow uses the standard ChatGPT interface, which has a robust free tier.
*   **Common mistake**: Simply accepting the vague answer without asking a follow-up prompt. This misses the opportunity to get the detailed information you need.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific as possible in your initial prompt to ChatGPT. The more context you provide, the less likely it is to resort to generalities.
