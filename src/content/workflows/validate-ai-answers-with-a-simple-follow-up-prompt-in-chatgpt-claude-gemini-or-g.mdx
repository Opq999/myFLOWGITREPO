## What you'll get
You will learn how to quickly check the reliability of any AI-generated answer and understand its hidden assumptions. This approach works because AI models often present all information with the same confident tone, regardless of how certain they are, and this prompt forces them to reveal their underlying logic and potential weaknesses.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot for generating text and answering questions.
*   **Claude** (freemium): An AI chatbot known for its conversational abilities and longer context windows.
*   **Gemini** (freemium): Google's AI chatbot, integrated with Google services.
*   **Grok** (paid): An AI chatbot from xAI, available to X Premium subscribers.

## Steps
1.  **Ask your initial question**: Open your preferred AI chat tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok) and type your question or request. This could be anything you need information or advice on.

    You should get a confident answer from the AI.

2.  **Paste the validation prompt**: Immediately after the AI provides its answer, paste the following prompt into the chat and send it:

    ```
    What would change your answer? Tell me:
    The one detail about my situation that, if different, would flip your recommendation.
    The part of your answer you're least sure about, and why.
    Anything you assumed about me that I didn't actually say.
    ```

    The AI will then provide a follow-up response, detailing its assumptions, uncertainties, and conditions that would alter its initial advice.

3.  **Review the AI's self-critique**: Read the AI's response to your validation prompt. Pay close attention to the three points it addresses: the 'flip condition,' the 'least sure part,' and 'unstated assumptions.'

    You should now have a clearer understanding of how solid the AI's initial answer is, what factors might change it, and any hidden assumptions it made about your situation.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier viability**: ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini all offer robust free tiers that are suitable for this workflow. Grok requires a paid X Premium subscription to access.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is to trust the AI's first answer without question, assuming its confident tone means it's always correct or complete. This workflow helps you avoid that by actively probing for weaknesses.
*   **Tip for better results**: Save the validation prompt in a notes app on your phone or computer. This way, you can quickly copy and paste it after any AI response, making it a habit to critically evaluate the information you receive.
