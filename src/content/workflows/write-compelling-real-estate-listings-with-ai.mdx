## What you'll get
You will get a well-written, aspirational real estate listing description, ready to use for your property. This approach works because it uses specific instructions (tone, word limit) to guide the AI, preventing generic outputs.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can generate text based on your instructions.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.

    You should see the chat interface, ready for your input.
2.  **Prepare your property details**: Gather the key information about your property. You will need:

    *   The `[property type]` (e.g., "3-bedroom bungalow", "luxury apartment", "commercial office space")
    *   The `[city]` where the property is located (e.g., "Lagos", "Abuja", "Port Harcourt")
    *   The `[top 3 features]` that make the property stand out (e.g., "spacious living area, modern kitchen, secure estate", "prime location, ample parking, high foot traffic")
    *   The `$[X]` price of the property (e.g., "50,000,000")

    The author specifies a "Tone: aspirational" and "Max 200 words. End with a strong CTA." These parts of the prompt are fixed.
3.  **Paste and customize the prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into the chat box. Replace the bracketed placeholders `[ ]` with your specific property details.

    ```
    Write a compelling MLS listing for a [property type] in [city]. Features: [top 3]. Price: $[X]. Tone: aspirational. Max 200 words. End with a strong CTA.
    ```

    You should see the AI generate a detailed listing description based on your input.

## Original source

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can also use other freemium AI chatbots like Claude.ai or Gemini.google.com for this workflow.
*   **Common mistake**: The author notes that without specifying the tone (like 'aspirational') and a word limit, AI outputs tend to be generic and less useful. Always include these details.
*   **Tip for better results**: Experiment with different tones (e.g., 'luxurious', 'family-friendly', 'investor-focused') to see what best fits your property and target audience.
