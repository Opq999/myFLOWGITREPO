## Notes & variations
*   **Free Tier Tip**: Free tiers for AI video generation tools can be limited in video length, resolution, or processing speed. Be patient, and check for daily or monthly usage caps.
*   **Common Pitfall**: Overly complex or ambiguous prompts can lead to unexpected or nonsensical video outputs. Ensure your prompt is clear, sequential, and describes the desired actions and visuals precisely.
*   **Get Better Results**: Experiment with different styles (e.g., anime, realistic, cartoon) and add more specific details about camera angles, lighting, and character emotions to guide the AI more effectively.
