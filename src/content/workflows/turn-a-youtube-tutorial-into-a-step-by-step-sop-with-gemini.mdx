## What you'll get
You will get a detailed Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) created directly from a YouTube tutorial. This goes beyond a simple summary, providing actionable steps, prerequisites, validation checkpoints, and a completion checklist. This approach helps you move from understanding a tutorial to actually applying it in practice.

## Tools you need
*   **Gemini** (freemium): An AI chat assistant that can process YouTube video content.
*   **YouTube** (free): The platform where you will find the tutorial video.

## Steps
1.  **Find your YouTube tutorial**: Open YouTube and find a tutorial video you want to turn into an SOP. Choose a video that clearly explains a process or task.
    You should have the video open and its URL ready to copy.
2.  **Open Gemini**: Go to the Gemini website in your browser.
    You should see the Gemini chat interface, ready for you to type a new message.
3.  **Paste the prompt and video link**: Copy the prompt provided below. Paste it into the Gemini chat box. Replace `[paste YouTube video link here]` with the actual link to your chosen YouTube tutorial.

    ```
    Turn this tutorial into a step-by-step SOP. Use this structure: 1. Prerequisites 2. Initial Setup 3. Main Workflow 4. Validation and Checkpoints 5. Common Errors Mentioned 6. Recovery or Rollback 7. Final Verification 8. Completion Checklist For each important step, include its approximate timestamp when identifiable. Clearly distinguish between: - Steps explicitly shown - Steps verbally mentioned but not demonstrated - Information that is missing Do not invent steps that the tutorial does not show or explain.
    [paste YouTube video link here]
    ```

    Gemini will process the video and generate a detailed SOP based on the structure you provided. It should include timestamps for key steps and highlight any information that was mentioned but not shown, or that was missing from the tutorial.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: The original author also maintains a free collection of prompts for Ask Gemini on YouTube, which might offer more ideas. Other freemium AI chat tools like Claude or ChatGPT might also be able to perform a similar task, though the prompt is specifically designed for Gemini's YouTube integration.
*   **Common mistake**: Do not expect the AI to invent steps or details that are not present in the video. The prompt specifically instructs Gemini not to do this, ensuring the SOP is faithful to the tutorial.
*   **Tip for better results**: Choose tutorials that are well-structured and clear. If the video itself is vague, the resulting SOP will also lack detail. You can also experiment with adding more specific sections to the SOP structure in the prompt if your task requires it.
