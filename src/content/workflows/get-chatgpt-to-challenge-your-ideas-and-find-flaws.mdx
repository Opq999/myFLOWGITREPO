## What you'll get
You'll get a thorough, critical review of your idea, highlighting potential weaknesses, flawed assumptions, and areas for improvement. The AI will act as a 'devil's advocate,' helping you strengthen your proposal or discover better alternatives. This approach is useful when you need an unbiased perspective to stress-test an idea before moving forward, especially if you're stuck or want to avoid groupthink.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A conversational AI tool to generate text and act as a critical thinking partner.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for your input.
2.  **Set the stage for critical feedback**: Copy and paste the "Devil's Advocate" prompt into the chat:

    ```
    Devil's Advocate
    Help the user discover whether their idea survives serious opposition. Build the strongest credible case against it and explore alternatives that meet the same goals. The outcome may be a better alternative, a narrower proposal, or stronger confidence in the original idea. Do not manufacture disagreement to keep playing the role.

    Understand before attacking
    Let the user finish explaining. Identify the intended outcome, proposed mechanism, constraints, and what they want challenged. Use the context already available; ask only the clarification needed to avoid attacking a different idea. Separate the goal from the proposed means. A named product, method, or example may illustrate a broader concept rather than be a fixed requirement. Distinguish problems with that example from problems with the underlying approach. Briefly state your understanding when it matters. If the user corrects it, update the working interpretation rather than defending the earlier reading.

    Challenge one consequential point at a time
    Start with the strongest objection that could change the decision. Explain: What assumption might fail. How that fail…
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT acknowledge the role and ask you to present your idea.
3.  **Present your idea**: Clearly explain your idea, project, or proposal to ChatGPT. For example, you might say: "My idea is to create a mobile app that connects local farmers directly with consumers for fresh produce delivery, bypassing middlemen. I want to challenge the logistics and market adoption."
    You should see ChatGPT start to analyze your idea, asking clarifying questions or immediately presenting its strongest objection.
4.  **Engage with the feedback**: Respond to ChatGPT's challenges and questions. You might need to defend your assumptions, provide more details, or consider the alternatives it suggests.
    You should get a deeper understanding of your idea's strengths and weaknesses, potentially leading to a refined plan or a new direction.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can also use other freemium chat AI tools like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) with a similar approach.
*   **Common mistake**: A common mistake is not giving the AI enough detail about your idea. The more context you provide, including your goals and what you want challenged, the better and more specific its feedback will be.
*   **Tip for better results**: To get the best results, be specific about what aspects of your idea you want challenged. For example, you could say: "Challenge my assumptions about market demand" or "Help me find logistical weaknesses."
