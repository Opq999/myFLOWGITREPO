## What you'll get
You will get a clear identification of the single biggest bottleneck in your business, along with three ranked actions to fix it. You will also get a complete Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for a manual process you want to document. This approach works by using specialized AI prompts that guide the chatbot to act as a business analyst and operations architect, helping you uncover critical issues and structure your knowledge.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that will act as your business analyst and operations architect.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI Chatbot**: Go to `https://chatgpt.com` (or your preferred AI chatbot). You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Use the Bottleneck Finder prompt**: Copy the "Bottleneck Finder" prompt below. Paste it into the chat window. Replace `[describe leads to sales to delivery to revenue cycle]` with a clear, concise description of your business's flow. Replace `[paste what you know]` with any conversion rates or points where customers drop off. Send the message.

    ```
    You are a business systems analyst specializing in constraint theory and throughput optimization. Context: My business is generating activity but not converting it to revenue at the rate it should. Something is the constraint — I may not be seeing it clearly. Task: Analyze my business flow below and identify the single constraint that, if removed, would have the highest downstream impact on revenue. Do not give me a list of problems. Give me the one. My current flow: [describe leads to sales to delivery to revenue cycle] Current conversion rates or drop-off points: [paste what you know] Format: (1) Identified constraint — one sentence. (2) Why this is the constraint, not a symptom. (3) Three actions to break it, ranked by speed of impact. Constraints : No generic advice. If you don't have enough data, ask one specific question to get it.
    ```

    The AI should respond by identifying a single constraint, explaining why it's a constraint (not a symptom), and suggesting three actions to break it. If it asks for more data, provide it.

3.  **Use the SOP Generator prompt**: Start a new chat (or clear the current one, if your chatbot allows). This helps keep the AI focused. Copy the "SOP Generator" prompt below. Paste it into the chat window. Send the message.

    ```
    You are an operations architect specializing in turning founder knowledge into repeatable systems. Context: I have a process I do manually and inconsistently. I need it documented as a standard operating procedure so it can be handed off, automated, or run without me. Task: Interview me to extract the full process. Ask me the questions needed to document it completely. Then produce a finished SOP. Format: SOP structure — Purpose, T…
    ```

    The AI should begin by asking you questions about the process you want to document. Answer its questions clearly and completely. It will continue asking until it has enough information.

4.  **Collaborate to build the SOP**: Respond to the AI's questions about your process. Be as detailed as possible. Once the AI has gathered enough information, it will present a draft of the SOP (Standard Operating Procedure). You should see a structured SOP, starting with "Purpose," and detailing the steps of your process.

## Notes & variations
You can use other freemium AI chatbots like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) for this workflow. The prompts will work similarly across these platforms. A common mistake is not providing enough detail when describing your business flow or the process for the SOP. The AI's output quality depends heavily on the quality and completeness of your input. Be specific and thorough. For the Bottleneck Finder, be honest about your conversion rates and drop-off points. For the SOP Generator, break down complex processes into smaller, manageable steps before you start, and be ready to answer follow-up questions from the AI.
