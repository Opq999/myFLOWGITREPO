## What you'll get
You will get a structured, slide-by-slide outline for a presentation, complete with titles, bullet points, and a 'so what' takeaway for each slide. This approach helps you quickly organize complex information from long documents into a clear, audience-focused presentation structure before you even start designing slides.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot to process your document and generate the presentation outline.
*   **Gamma** (freemium): A tool to quickly turn your outline into actual presentation slides.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to chatgpt.com on your phone or computer and log in.
    *   You should see the ChatGPT chat interface, ready for your input.
2.  **Prepare your document**: Copy the full text of the document you want to turn into a presentation. This could be a report, an article, or any long piece of text.
    *   You should have the document text ready to paste.
3.  **Paste the prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into ChatGPT. Replace `[paste your document here]` with the text you copied in the previous step.

    ```
    Turn the content below into a slide-by-slide outline for a 10-slide presentation aimed at your audience. Rules: one core idea per slide, and if a slide has two ideas, split it. For each slide give me a short slide title of max 6 words, 3 to 4 bullet points in plain language, and a one-line so what that says why this slide matters to the audience. Start with a title slide and end with a single clear takeaway slide. Do not copy full sentences from the source, compress into phrases a person can read in a couple of seconds. Flag any slide where I am missing data or an example, and tell me what to add. Then paste your doc as the content.
    [paste your document here]
    ```

    *   ChatGPT will generate a detailed outline, broken down by slide, with titles, bullet points, and "so what" lines. It will also flag any missing information or examples.
4.  **Review and refine the outline**: Read through the generated outline. The author suggests deleting or merging any "weak slides" that don't add much value. If the outline feels too dense, you can ask ChatGPT to make it more concise (e.g., "Make this outline readable from the back of a room").
    *   You should have a refined outline that clearly presents the key ideas for your presentation.
5.  **Create slides in Gamma (optional)**: Once you are satisfied with your outline, you can paste it into a presentation tool like Gamma to quickly generate the actual slides. Gamma can often turn a text outline into a visual presentation with minimal effort.
    *   Gamma will use your outline to create a draft presentation, saving you time on design.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Other AI chatbots like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) can also be used with similar prompts to generate outlines. For creating the actual slides, free tools like Google Slides or Microsoft PowerPoint are available, though they require you to manually build the slides from your outline.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is trying to get the AI to generate a *finished* presentation in one go. This prompt is designed to create a structured *outline*, which you then refine and use in a separate presentation tool. This two-step process often yields better results than expecting a perfect, ready-to-present deck immediately.
*   **Tip for better results**: The author suggests adding a line like "the deck should be readable from the back of a room" if the initial output is too dense. Also, remember to clearly specify your "audience" in the prompt for more tailored and relevant results.
