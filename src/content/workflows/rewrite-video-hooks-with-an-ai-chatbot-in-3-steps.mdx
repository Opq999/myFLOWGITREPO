## What you'll get
You will get three different rewritten versions of your video hook, each applying a specific strategy: adding detail, creating tension, or shortening it. Each rewrite comes with a one-line explanation of the change. This approach helps you see concrete ways to improve your hooks while keeping your unique voice.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chatbot** (freemium): Helps rewrite your video hooks with specific strategies and explanations.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI Chatbot**: Go to your preferred AI chatbot (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) on your phone or computer. You will use its chat interface.
    *   You should see the chat interface, ready for you to type a message.
2.  **Prepare your hook details**: Before you paste the prompt, gather the following information:
    *   `[HOOK]`: Your original video hook (e.g., "How to save money").
    *   `[TOPIC]`: The main topic of your video (e.g., "personal finance").
    *   `[DETAIL]`: A specific, interesting detail related to your hook that you want to add (e.g., "cancelling one subscription"). If you don't have one, the AI can suggest one, but it's best if you provide it.
    *   `[VOICE NOTES]`: One or two sentences describing your natural speaking style or brand voice (e.g., "I talk in a friendly, direct way, using simple words, not like a formal ad"). This helps the AI match your tone.
    *   You should have these four pieces of information ready to insert into the prompt.
3.  **Paste the prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into the AI chatbot. Replace the bracketed placeholders (`[HOOK]`, `[TOPIC]`, `[DETAIL]`, `[VOICE NOTES]`) with your specific information.

    ```
    My hook: "[HOOK]". Topic: [TOPIC]. Show 3 rewrites: (1) add a specific detail [DETAIL], (2) add tension/mistake, (3) shorten to under 8 words. Explain in one line what changed each time. Keep my voice: [VOICE NOTES].
    ```

    *   You should receive three rewritten hooks, each with a short explanation of the change, in a style similar to your `[VOICE NOTES]`.
4.  **Review and refine**: Look at the three rewritten hooks. The author notes that if the third rewrite (the shortened one) loses the original meaning, it cut too much. If this happens, tell the AI chatbot to try again, specifically asking it to keep the main noun and any numbers from your original hook.
    *   You should get a refined version of the third hook that keeps its core meaning while being short.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If you don't use ChatGPT, you can use other freemium AI chatbots like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) for this workflow. They offer similar capabilities in their free tiers.
*   **Common mistake**: The most common issue is when the third, shortened hook loses its original meaning. Always check this specific rewrite carefully. If it's too vague, tell the AI to focus on keeping the main subject and any key numbers.
*   **Tip for better results**: The `[VOICE NOTES]` part is very important. Spend a moment to describe your natural way of speaking. This helps the AI avoid sounding generic or like an advertisement, making the rewritten hooks feel more authentic to your content.
