## What you'll get
You'll get a detailed analysis of a business decision, including arguments for and against, critical assumptions, and new questions to consider. You'll also be able to identify a potential bottleneck in your business operations. This approach works because it gives the AI a clear role, context, task, format, and constraints, leading to much more focused and useful answers on the first try.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot for general conversations and tasks.
*   **Claude** (freemium): Another AI chatbot known for its long context window and nuanced responses.
*   **Gemini** (freemium): Google's AI chatbot, integrated with Google services.

## Steps
1.  **Choose your AI tool**: Open your preferred AI chat tool (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) on your phone or computer.
    *   You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Understand the prompt structure**: The author uses a five-part structure: Role, Context, Task, Format, and Constraints. This helps the AI understand exactly what you need.
    *   This step is informational, preparing you for the next ones.

3.  **Pressure-test a business decision**: Copy and paste the "Decision Pressure Test" prompt into the chat. Replace `[describe the decision and your current lean]` with your actual business decision and what you are currently leaning towards.

    ```
    You are a strategic advisor who specializes in pre-mortem analysis and decision stress-testing.
    Context: I am about to make a significant business decision. Before committing, I want to pressure-test it from every angle.
    Task: Run a full decision audit on the choice below. Argue both sides with equal force. Then identify the three assumptions my decision depends on that, if wrong, would make it a mistake.
    Decision: [describe the decision and your current lean]
    Format: (1) Case FOR — 4 strongest arguments. (2) Case AGAINST — 4 strongest arguments. (3) Three critical assumptions I'm making. (4) One question I haven't asked that I should.
    Constraints: Do not tell me what to do. Do not soften either side. Your job is clarity, not comfort.
    ```

    *   You should get a structured response with arguments for and against your decision, key assumptions, and a new question to consider.

4.  **Identify a business bottleneck**: (Optional) If you want to find a bottleneck, copy and paste the "Bottleneck Finder" prompt into the chat. Replace `[describe your business flow and activity here]` with details about your business operations. The author only shared the beginning of this prompt; the `Format` and `Constraints` sections were not provided. You may need to add your own based on the structure from the first prompt and your specific needs.

    ```
    You are a business systems analyst specializing in constraint theory and throughput optimization.
    Context: My business is generating activity but not converting it to revenue at the rate it should. Something is the constraint — I may not be seeing it clearly.
    Task: Analyze my business flow below and identify the single constraint that, if addressed, would most significantly improve my conversion to revenue.
    [describe your business flow and activity here]
    ```

    *   You should receive an analysis identifying a potential bottleneck in your business process.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: Forgetting to replace the bracketed placeholders like `[describe the decision and your current lean]` with your actual information. The AI needs specific details to give you useful answers.
*   **Tip for better results**: Experiment with different "Roles" for the AI (e.g., "You are a marketing expert" or "You are a financial analyst") or refine the `Context` and `Constraints` to match your specific needs. The more precise you are, the better the AI's output will be.
*   **Free-tier viability**: All the listed AI chat tools offer robust free tiers that are sufficient for these workflows. You won't need to pay to get good results.
