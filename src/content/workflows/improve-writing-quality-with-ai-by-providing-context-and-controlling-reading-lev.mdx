## What you'll get
You will get a rewritten version of your text that is clearer, easier to understand, and better tailored to your audience. This approach works by giving the AI (Artificial Intelligence) more specific information about your writing goals and audience, leading to more accurate and useful revisions than a single, long prompt.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A popular AI chatbot for generating and refining text.
*   **Claude** (freemium): Another AI chatbot known for its strong writing and summarization abilities.
*   **Gemini** (freemium): Google's AI chatbot, useful for various text tasks.

## Steps
1.  **Open your chosen AI chat tool**: Go to the website of your preferred AI chatbot (ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini). You should see a chat interface ready for your input.
2.  **Provide your draft and ask for context questions**: Paste your writing draft into the chat. Then, use the following prompt to ask the AI to understand your text better:

    ```
    Before rewriting this, ask me questions that would help you understand the context and what I'm trying to communicate.
    ```

    The AI should respond by asking clarifying questions about your audience, purpose, or specific terms.
3.  **Answer the AI's questions**: Respond to each question the AI asks. Be as clear and detailed as possible.
    You should see the AI acknowledge your answers, building up its understanding of your text.
4.  **Ask the AI to rewrite at a specific reading level**: Once you've answered its questions, instruct the AI to rewrite your draft, specifying a reading level. The author suggests an 8th-grade reading level for a general audience.

    ```
    Rewrite this at an 8th-grade reading level while keeping the meaning and key details.
    ```

    You should receive a rewritten version of your text that is simpler and easier to follow, without losing important information.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is not answering the AI's questions thoroughly, or trying to skip the context-gathering step. The quality of the rewrite depends heavily on the AI's understanding of your goals.
*   **Tip for better results**: The author mentions using the Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level as a target for more specific control over reading level. You can try prompts like "Rewrite this at a Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level of 8" if your chosen AI tool understands this metric. Experiment with different reading levels (e.g., 6th grade for very simple, 10th grade for slightly more complex) to find what best suits your audience.
