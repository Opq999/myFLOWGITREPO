## What you'll get
You will get instant notifications on your phone whenever a new email related to a refund or cancellation arrives in your Gmail inbox. Each alert will show you the sender's name and a short summary of the email's content. This approach is efficient because the system only runs when a new email arrives, rather than constantly checking your inbox.

## Tools you need
*   **Gmail** (freemium): To receive and store your emails.
*   **PocketClaw** (paid): An iOS app that acts as an AI agent to monitor your emails and send alerts.

## Steps
1.  **Install PocketClaw**: Search for "PocketClaw: Business AI Agent" on the Apple App Store and install the app on your iPhone.
    You should see the PocketClaw app icon appear on your phone's home screen.

2.  **Open the template**: Open the PocketClaw app. Look for a section like "Templates" or "Ready-made workflows" and select the "Refund and cancellation emails" template.
    You should see the details of this specific workflow, likely showing how it connects to Gmail and what kind of alert it sends.

3.  **Connect PocketClaw to your Gmail account**: The template will prompt you to connect your Gmail account. Follow the on-screen instructions to authorize PocketClaw to access your emails. This usually involves signing in through Google's secure portal and granting specific permissions.
    PocketClaw should confirm that your Gmail account is successfully linked.

4.  **Activate the workflow**: Once your Gmail is connected, look for a button or toggle to "Activate," "Enable," or "Turn On" the "Refund and cancellation emails" workflow.
    The workflow is now active and waiting for new emails in your Gmail account.

5.  **Test the alert**: From another email address (or ask a friend to help), send an email to your connected Gmail account. In the subject line or body of this test email, include words like "refund" or "cancellation."
    Within a few minutes (the author notes it took about 2 minutes in their tests), you should receive a notification on your phone from PocketClaw, showing the sender and a summary of your test email.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: While PocketClaw is a paid app, you might be able to set up similar, though less intelligent, automations using free tiers of tools like IFTTT (If This Then That) or Zapier. However, these often rely on simpler keyword matching and might not offer the same level of AI analysis or instant triggering without paid upgrades.
*   **Common mistake**: Ensure you grant PocketClaw the necessary permissions to access your Gmail when prompted. If permissions are not correctly set, the app cannot read your emails and trigger alerts.
*   **Tip for better results**: The AI works best when keywords like "refund" or "cancellation" are clearly present in the email's subject or body. Encourage customers to use clear language when contacting you about these issues.
