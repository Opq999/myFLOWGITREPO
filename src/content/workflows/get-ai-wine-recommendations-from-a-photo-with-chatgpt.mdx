## What you'll get
You will get a quick, personalized recommendation for a supermarket wine based on a photo you take of the wine fridge. ChatGPT will analyze the bottle labels, including prices, and explain why its choice is a good match for your needs. This approach is useful when you're unsure which wine to buy and need a fast, informed decision without being a wine expert.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can understand images and provide text responses.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Open the ChatGPT app on your phone or go to chatgpt.com in your mobile browser. You should see the chat interface ready for a new conversation.
2.  **Take a photo of the wines**: In the supermarket, take a clear photo of the entire wine fridge or shelf you are choosing from. Make sure the bottle labels and prices are visible. The author notes it works even if the picture is a little blurry or has many wines.
3.  **Upload the photo to ChatGPT**: In the ChatGPT chat window, look for the 'attach file' or 'upload image' icon (it often looks like a paperclip or a square with a plus sign). Tap it and select the photo you just took. You should see the photo appear in the chat input area.
4.  **Ask for a recommendation**: Type your request into the chat box. Be specific about what you need the wine for. The author's example is pairing with food:

    ```
    Which one would be good with curry?
    ```

    You should receive a recommendation from ChatGPT, along with an explanation of why it chose that particular wine, based on the details it read from your photo.

## Original source
This workflow was shared by Reddit user /u/brain_scientist_lady on the r/ChatGPT subreddit. They described how they use ChatGPT to quickly choose wines from a supermarket fridge by taking a photo and asking for recommendations, saving time and ensuring a good choice.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: ChatGPT's free tier (GPT-3.5) can handle this task. Other AI chatbots like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) also have image understanding capabilities and free tiers, offering a similar experience.
*   **Common mistake or pitfall**: Ensure your photo is as clear as possible, especially for small text on labels. While the author notes it works even with blurry images, a clearer photo will lead to more accurate readings of grape varieties, regions, and prices.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be specific in your prompt. Instead of just 'Which one is good?', try 'Which dry white wine would pair well with grilled fish, considering these options?' or 'I prefer a sweet red wine under ₦5000, what do you recommend?'
