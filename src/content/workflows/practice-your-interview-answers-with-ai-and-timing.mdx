## What you'll get
You will practice and refine your answers to common interview questions, ensuring they are concise and hit key points within a time limit. This method helps you make a strong first impression by preparing for the conversational parts of an interview.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chat Tool (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini)** (freemium): To help you structure your answers and get feedback.
*   **Voice Recorder App** (free): To record yourself practicing your answers and check your timing.

## Steps
1.  **Identify key points for your answer**: Before you start, decide on the 2-3 most important things you want to say for a specific question. For example, for "Tell me about yourself," your points might be: your main skill, a key achievement, and your career goal.
2.  **Ask the AI to help draft an answer**: Use an AI chat tool to help you write a concise answer that includes your key points. You can start with a prompt like this:

    ```
    Help me draft a concise answer to "Tell me about yourself" for a job interview. I want to highlight my skills in [Your Skill 1] and [Your Skill 2], mention my experience with [Key Achievement], and state my goal of [Your Career Goal]. Keep it under 90 seconds when spoken.
    ```

    You should get a draft answer from the AI.
3.  **Refine the AI's answer**: Read the AI's response. Does it sound like you? Does it clearly include your key points? Edit it until you are happy with it.
4.  **Practice and record your answer**: Open your voice recorder app. Say your refined answer out loud. Try to stick to your time limit (e.g., 90 seconds for "Tell me about yourself").
5.  **Listen and check**: Listen to your recording. Did you cover your 2-3 key points? Did you stay within the time limit? If not, adjust your answer and record again.
6.  **Repeat for other questions**: Do this for other common interview questions like "Why are you interested in this company?" or "What are your strengths and weaknesses?" Remember to set your key points and time limit for each.

## Original source
This method was shared by Reddit user /u/Avengerfx, who found that practicing conversational interview answers out loud and timing them significantly improved their job application success. They engineered a personal system to ensure their answers hit specific points within a set duration.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternative**: If your preferred AI chat tool has limits, you can use a different free AI chat service or focus on refining your answers manually based on common interview advice.
*   **Common mistake**: Trying to memorize a long, complex answer instead of focusing on the key points and speaking naturally. This can lead to sounding robotic or forgetting parts of your answer.
*   **Tip for better results**: Practice answering questions in front of a mirror to also work on your body language and eye contact, even if you are only recording your voice.
