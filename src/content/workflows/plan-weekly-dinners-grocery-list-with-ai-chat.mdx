## What you'll get
You'll receive a personalized meal plan for five weeknights, complete with simple recipes and a grocery list. This approach helps you save money by reusing ingredients across meals, reducing food waste, and making weekly planning much faster.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can understand your requests and generate text, like meal plans and grocery lists.

## Steps

1.  **Open your AI chat tool**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer. If you don't have an account, you can sign up for free.
    You should see a chat interface where you can type messages to the AI.

2.  **Craft your meal planning prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into the chat window. Replace the bracketed `[PLACEHOLDERS]` with your specific details. Be as clear as possible about your preferences, budget, and what you already have.

    ```
    Plan 5 weeknight dinners for [NUMBER] people.
    Budget is about [AMOUNT] for the week.
    Each dinner should take 30 minutes or less.
    We like: [FOODS YOU LIKE, e.g., chicken, pasta, rice, vegetables, spicy food]
    We don't eat: [ALLERGIES / DISLIKES, e.g., nuts, seafood, mushrooms]
    Already in my fridge and pantry: [WHAT YOU HAVE, e.g., eggs, milk, rice, onions, tomatoes, ground beef]
    Reuse ingredients across meals so nothing goes to waste.
    Then give me one grocery list grouped by store section, leaving out what I already have.
    Keep each recipe to 6 steps or fewer.
    ```

    You should get a detailed meal plan for five days, with short recipes and a grocery list organized by store section.

3.  **Review and refine the plan**: Read through the suggested meal plan and grocery list. If you don't like a particular meal or want to make a change, tell the AI. For example, you can say:

    ```
    No, I don't like Wednesday's meal. Swap it with something else.
    ```

    The AI will adjust the plan and grocery list based on your feedback. You can keep refining until you have a plan you like.

## Original source
This clever method for generating a weekly meal plan and grocery list was shared by Reddit user /u/thegritmaster. They emphasized the importance of being specific with your prompt to get the best results, especially by listing ingredients you already have to avoid waste.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If you prefer other AI chat tools, you can use similar prompts with Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai), both of which offer free tiers.
*   **Common mistake**: Forgetting to list what you already have in your fridge and pantry. This is crucial for the AI to minimize waste and avoid suggesting you buy ingredients you already own.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be very specific with your preferences and constraints. You can also add timeframes, like "my kid has soccer practice at 6pm on Wednesday," and the AI will consider that when planning meals.
