## What you'll get
You will get an immediate assessment of potential stroke symptoms and urgent advice on whether to seek emergency medical care. This approach helps quickly identify situations that require immediate attention, especially when symptoms are confusing or seem to improve. It works by comparing your described symptoms against a vast knowledge base of medical information, highlighting critical signs that should not be ignored.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can answer questions and provide information.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface, ready for you to type a message. If you don't have an account, you'll need to sign up for a free one.
2.  **Describe the symptoms**: In the chat box, clearly list all the symptoms you observed, exactly as they happened, and mention if they improved or changed. The author doesn't share their exact prompt; a starting point:

    ```
    I need urgent advice. Someone is experiencing these symptoms: dropped things with their right hand, numbness, mouth looked uneven, speech sounded weird, lost their balance for a second, and now seems better. What could this mean, and what should I do immediately?
    ```

    ChatGPT will process your input and provide a response.
3.  **Read the AI's advice**: Carefully read the response from ChatGPT. Pay close attention to any warnings or recommendations for immediate action.
    The author received a response that "basically screamed: possible stroke or TIA, emergency care now, do not wait even if the symptoms are going away." You should see a similar urgent recommendation if the symptoms are serious.
4.  **Seek medical help**: If the AI suggests emergency care, do not delay. Contact emergency services or go to the nearest hospital immediately.
    You will be taking appropriate action based on the urgent advice received.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can also use other freemium AI chatbots like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) for similar advice.
*   **Common mistake**: Never use AI as a substitute for professional medical advice. AI tools can help identify potential issues and urgency, but only a qualified doctor can diagnose and treat medical conditions. Always confirm AI-generated health information with a medical professional.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific and detailed as possible when describing symptoms, including when they started, how long they lasted, and any changes over time. Mentioning age and any known medical conditions (like high blood pressure) can also help the AI provide more relevant information. Do not wait for symptoms to worsen or disappear before seeking help if the AI suggests urgency.
