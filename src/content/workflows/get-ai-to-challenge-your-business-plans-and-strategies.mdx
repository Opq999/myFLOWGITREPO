## What you'll get
You'll get a critical evaluation of your business plans, content strategies, or any other approach you've developed. Instead of just helping you build, the AI will act as a devil's advocate, highlighting potential flaws and weaknesses.
This approach works by explicitly shifting the AI from an 'assistant' role to a 'critic' role, forcing it to provide more honest and useful feedback, especially when you're already convinced your idea is solid.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A widely available AI chatbot that can understand and generate human-like text.

## Steps
1.  **Prepare your plan**: Before you start, have a clear plan, strategy, or idea ready that you want the AI to critique. This could be a business strategy, a content plan, or an architectural decision.
    You should have your plan written down or clearly in mind.
2.  **Open your AI chat tool**: Go to ChatGPT (or your preferred AI chat tool) in your web browser or mobile app.
    You should see the chat interface, ready for your input.
3.  **Ask the AI to challenge your thinking**: Instead of asking the AI to help you build your plan, ask it to find flaws first. The author suggests two powerful prompts. Choose one and paste it, replacing `[Your Plan Here]` with your actual plan:

    ```
    Assume this approach has a serious flaw. What is it? My plan: [Your Plan Here]
    ```

    Or, for a deeper critique:

    ```
    What would a smart person who disagrees with me say about this plan? My plan: [Your Plan Here]
    ```

    The AI will respond by listing potential weaknesses, counter-arguments, or reasons why your plan might be wrong. It will not try to help you build it yet.
4.  **Review the critique**: Read through the AI's response carefully. Look for genuine insights or perspectives you might have overlooked. This feedback is designed to make your plan stronger.
    You should have a clearer understanding of potential risks or areas for improvement in your plan.
5.  **(Optional) Refine your plan or ask for execution help**: Once you've considered the critiques, you can either refine your plan based on the feedback or, if you're satisfied, then ask the AI to help you execute the improved plan.
    A more robust plan or the AI helping you with the next steps, now that potential flaws have been addressed.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Most major AI chat tools like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) and Claude (claude.ai) offer similar freemium tiers and can be used for this workflow.
*   **Common mistake**: A common mistake is to be too vague with your plan. The more detail you provide about your strategy, the more specific and useful the AI's critique will be.
*   **Tip for better results**: If the AI's initial critique isn't deep enough, try follow-up prompts like 'Elaborate on the biggest weakness you identified' or 'What's a counter-argument to that flaw?' to dig deeper.
