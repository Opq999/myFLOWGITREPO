## What you'll get
This workflow helps you create highly personalized outbound messages that actually get replies from potential clients. You'll gain a deeper understanding of your target decision-maker's psychology, allowing you to frame your pitch in a way that resonates with their specific needs and priorities. This approach works because it focuses on the human element of buying, ensuring your message is aimed at the right person with the right framing.

## Tools you need
*   **Claude** (freemium): An AI chatbot used to research and understand your target decision-maker's profile.

## Steps
1.  **Identify your target company and decision-maker**: Choose a company you want to work with and identify the specific person you want to reach. This could be a Head of Operations, a Chief Operating Officer (COO), or another relevant leader. Make sure you have their name and role.
    You should have a clear target in mind.

2.  **Research the decision-maker's profile**: Use public information sources like LinkedIn, the company's official website, and recent news articles to gather details about your target decision-maker. Look for their current role, how long they've been there, the company's size, its industry, and any public information about their background or typical responsibilities.
    You'll have a collection of facts about the person and their company.

3.  **Ask Claude to analyze the decision-maker**: Open Claude in your web browser. Paste the following prompt, replacing the bracketed text with the specific information you gathered in the previous step. The author doesn't share their exact prompt, but the goal is to understand the decision-maker's approximate age and who likely controls the budget for problems related to their role.

    ```
    Based on the following information about a decision-maker at [Company Name], help me understand two key things:
    1. Their approximate age range.
    2. Who likely owns the budget for problems related to their role.

    Here's what I know:
    - Role: [e.g., Head of Operations, COO]
    - Company size: [e.g., 50 people, $400M revenue]
    - Industry: [e.g., tech, manufacturing]
    - Any other relevant details: [e.g., "They've been in this role for 5 years," "Company recently expanded"]
    ```

    Claude will provide insights into the decision-maker's likely age range and who typically controls the budget for their department or problems. For example, it might suggest that a "Head of Operations" in a 50-person company likely feels the problem daily, while a "C-suite" member signs off on the budget.

4.  **Draft your outbound message**: Use the insights from Claude to tailor your message. Focus on the specific problems and benefits that someone of their age and budget ownership profile would care about. For example, a younger Head of Operations might respond to messages about efficiency gains and new technology, while an older Chief Operating Officer might prioritize strategic impact, risk reduction, or significant cost savings.
    You will have a personalized message ready to send, designed to resonate with your specific target.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) are also freemium AI chatbots that can perform similar analysis if you prefer.
*   **Common mistake**: Don't skip the research step. The quality of Claude's insights depends entirely on the information you provide. Generic inputs will lead to generic outputs, making your message less effective.
*   **Tip for better results**: After Claude provides its initial insights, ask follow-up questions like "What kind of language or benefits would appeal most to someone with this profile?" or "What common objections might someone like this have?" This will help you further refine your message and anticipate potential concerns.
