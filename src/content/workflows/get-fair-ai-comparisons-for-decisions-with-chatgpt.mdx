## What you'll get
You will get a balanced comparison and a clear recommendation from ChatGPT when deciding between two options, such as job offers, apartments, or project structures. This approach works by making sure both options are described equally, preventing the AI from favoring the one you initially explained in more detail.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): To help you compare two options and make a decision.

## Steps
1.  **Prepare your options for fair comparison**: Open ChatGPT and paste the following prompt. Replace `[option A]` and `[option B]` with descriptions of the two things you want to compare. Make sure to include details you know, but don't worry if one option has more information than the other at this stage.

    ```
    Here are two options I'm deciding between: [option A] and [option B]. Don't compare them yet. Rewrite both in the same structure and about the same length: what it costs, how long it takes, the risk, the upside, and what I'd give up. Where I didn't give you something, write 'unknown' instead of filling it in.
    ```

    You should get two rewritten descriptions, each following the same structure and clearly marking any missing information as "unknown."

2.  **Identify critical missing information**: After ChatGPT has rewritten your options, ask it to highlight the most important missing details. This helps you see where the comparison is weakest.

    ```
    List the unknowns that would change this comparison the most, most important first.
    ```

    You should get a list of key pieces of information that, if known, would significantly impact the decision.

3.  **Get the AI's recommendation**: Now that both options are described fairly and you know what information is still missing, ask ChatGPT for its final comparison and pick.

    ```
    Now compare the two evened-out versions. Give the strongest case for each one, then your pick, and say which unknown your pick depends on most.
    ```

    You should receive a balanced argument for each option, ChatGPT's recommended choice, and an explanation of which unknown factor most influenced its decision.

## Original source
This workflow was shared by Reddit user /u/Ok_Negotiation_2587 on the platform Reddit. They observed that AI models tend to favor options described with more detail and developed a three-step process to ensure a more balanced and fair comparison.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: You can use other freemium AI chat tools like Claude.ai or Gemini.google.com to follow these steps.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is not providing enough initial detail for *either* option. If both options have too many "unknowns" after Step 1, the comparison in Step 3 will be less useful. Try to gather as much information as possible before you start.
*   **Tip for better results**: After Step 2, if you can easily find answers to some of the listed "unknowns," go back and provide that new information to ChatGPT. Then, repeat Step 3 for an even more informed recommendation.
