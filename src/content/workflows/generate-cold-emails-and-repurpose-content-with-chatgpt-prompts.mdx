## What you'll get
You'll learn to generate highly effective cold emails and repurpose existing content into a full week's worth of social media posts using specific, structured prompts for ChatGPT. This method helps ChatGPT deliver precise, actionable results by clearly defining its role, task, and constraints, moving beyond vague 'act as an expert' instructions.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot for generating text based on your prompts.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Craft a cold email**: Use this prompt to generate a concise, direct cold email for business outreach. Remember to replace the bracketed placeholders with your specific details.

    ```
    You are a direct-response copywriter who has written cold emails that generated $10M+ in pipeline for B2B SaaS. Write a cold email to [recipient role] at [company type] offering [specific outcome, not feature]. Constraints: - Max 90 words, 5th-grade reading level - Open with a specific observation about their business, not 'I hope this finds you well' - Body: one proof point (number, name, or specific result), no adjectives - Close with a yes/no CTA requiring
    ```

    ChatGPT will generate a cold email, typically under 90 words, with a clear call to action, ready for you to review and send.

3.  **Repurpose your content**: Use this prompt to transform a single piece of content (like a blog post or transcript) into a week's worth of social media updates.     ChatGPT will provide a structured output, including a Twitter/X thread and other content formats, ready for you to schedule.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: While this workflow uses ChatGPT's free tier, similar results can often be achieved with other freemium chat applications like Claude.ai or Gemini.google.com, using slightly adapted prompts.
*   **Common mistake**: A common mistake is forgetting to replace the bracketed placeholders `[ ]` in the prompts with your specific information. ChatGPT needs clear details to give you the best results.
*   **Tip for better results**: To get even better results, experiment with the constraints. For example, try asking for a different reading level or a specific tone in your cold email, or specify more output formats for content repurposing.
