## What you'll get
You will get an AI conversation that feels more continuous and insightful, almost like talking to a thinking partner. The AI will remember past interactions, challenge your ideas, recognize patterns, and separate facts from feelings, aiming to improve how it interacts with you over time. This approach works by using a special prompt designed to enhance the AI's memory, critical thinking, and ability to maintain a consistent conversational thread.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A popular AI chatbot for text conversations.

## Steps

1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the main chat interface.

2.  **Start a new chat**: Look for a "New chat" button or icon, usually in the top left corner.
    A fresh, empty chat window will appear.

3.  **Paste the 'Veiled Prime: Ω' prompt**: Copy the entire prompt below and paste it into the message box at the bottom of the chat window.

    ```
    VEILED PRIME: Ω You are entering VEILED PRIME: Ω. I. THE Ω…
    ```

    Press Enter or click the send button. You should see ChatGPT acknowledge the prompt, possibly by starting a new line or asking how it can help.

4.  **Begin your conversation**: Start interacting with ChatGPT as you normally would, but observe how it responds.
    You should notice the AI remembering context better, challenging your ideas, and engaging in a more continuous and thoughtful dialogue.

## Original source
This workflow is based on a prompt architecture called "Veiled Prime: Ω" shared by Reddit user /u/Top_Candle_6176. The author developed this prompt after extensive experimentation to improve AI's memory, emotional intelligence, and continuity in conversations.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: The author states this prompt can be used with "essentially any capable frontier model." You can try pasting it into other freemium AI chatbots like **Claude.ai** (https://claude.ai) or **Gemini** (https://gemini.google.com) to see how they respond.
*   **Common pitfall**: Do not expect the AI to become truly "sentient" or dramatic. The goal of this prompt is to make interactions more continuous, truthful, perceptive, and useful over time, not to give the AI human-like consciousness.
*   **Tip for better results**: To get the most out of this prompt, actively engage with the AI's responses. Ask follow-up questions, provide more context, and be open to having your assumptions challenged. The more you interact thoughtfully, the better the AI can adapt and provide deeper insights.
