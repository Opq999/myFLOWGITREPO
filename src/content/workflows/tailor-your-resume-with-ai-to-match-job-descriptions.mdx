## What you'll get
You will get a version of your resume content, such as a bullet point or an entire experience section, that has been customized to better match a specific job description. This approach helps your resume include relevant keywords, making it more likely to pass through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), which are automated software programs that scan applications for specific terms.

## Tools you need
*   **Gemini** (freemium): An AI chat assistant to help you identify keywords and rephrase your resume content.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI assistant**: Go to Gemini (or your preferred AI chat tool) in your web browser or app.

    You should see the chat interface, ready for you to type your first message.

2.  **Extract key skills and keywords**: Copy the full job description from the job posting. Then, paste it into the AI chat and ask it to identify the most important terms:

    ```
    Extract the 15 most important skills and keywords from this job description:

    [Paste the full job description here]
    ```

    You should get a clear list of relevant skills and keywords that the AI has identified from the job description.

3.  **Tailor your resume content**: Now, copy the specific section of your resume that you want to customize (for example, a single bullet point, an entire job experience, or your summary statement). Paste it into the AI chat along with the keywords you just extracted, and ask the AI to rewrite it:

    ```
    I want to update my resume to better match the job description. Here is a section from my current resume:

    [Paste your resume section here]

    Using the following keywords, rewrite this section to incorporate them naturally, without adding false information. Focus on showing how my experience relates to these terms:

    [Paste the keywords from Step 2 here]
    ```

    You should receive a revised version of your resume content that naturally includes many of the target keywords.

4.  **Review and refine**: Carefully read the AI's suggestions. Make sure that all the information is accurate and truly reflects your experience. Adjust any wording to ensure it sounds natural and professional.

    You should have a polished, keyword-optimized resume section ready to be added to your job application.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Other freemium AI chat tools like ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) or Claude (claude.ai) can perform the same tasks effectively.
*   **Common mistake**: Never copy-paste AI-generated text directly into your resume without reviewing it. AI can sometimes make up details or use awkward phrasing. Always verify accuracy and ensure the language sounds like you.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be specific with your prompts. If you want the AI to focus on a particular achievement or skill, mention it clearly. You can also ask it to rewrite the text in a specific tone (e.g., "more action-oriented") or to fit a certain length.
