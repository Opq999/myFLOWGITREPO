## What you'll get
You'll get visual learning aids like handwritten-style notes, mind maps, flowcharts, or diagrams generated directly from your text. These images are designed to help you remember information better than just reading text. This approach leverages the power of visual memory, making complex information easier to understand and recall, especially useful for studying or quickly grasping new concepts.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can understand your requests and generate text and images.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to chatgpt.com on your phone or computer. You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Define the visual commands**: The author suggests defining special keywords (like "commands") at the start of your chat. This tells ChatGPT what kind of image to create when you use that keyword later. Copy and paste this prompt into ChatGPT:

    ```
    From now on, when I use a specific keyword, generate a visual image based on the text I provide. Here are the keywords and what they mean:
    *   /generatehandwrittenimage: Create a full page of handwritten-style notes as an image, including headings, arrows, and boxes.
    *   /mindmapimage: Generate a single visual mind map of the entire topic.
    *   /flowchartimage: Draw a proper flowchart explaining a process.
    *   /labeleddiagram: Produce a labeled diagram of the concept.
    *   /cheatsheetimage: Make a one-page visual cheat sheet.
    *   /comicstrip: Explain the concept as a comic strip.
    *   /memorypalaceimage: Draw a single scene with items from my list placed within it, to help with memorization.
    Is that clear?
    ```

    You should get a response confirming that ChatGPT understands these instructions.

3.  **Generate handwritten notes**: Now, use the `/generatehandwrittenimage` command with the text you want to turn into notes. Replace `[paste your notes or chapter here]` with your actual text.

    ```
    /generatehandwrittenimage [paste your notes or chapter here]
    ```

    ChatGPT will generate an image of handwritten-style notes. You can screenshot this image on your phone or download it.

4.  **Generate a mind map (optional)**: In the same chat, you can ask for a mind map of a topic. Replace `[paste your topic or text here]` with your content.

    ```
    /mindmapimage [paste your topic or text here]
    ```

    ChatGPT will generate a visual mind map of the topic.

5.  **Generate a memory palace image (optional)**: If you have a list you need to remember in order, try the memory palace technique. Replace `[paste your list of items here]` with your list.

    ```
    /memorypalaceimage [paste your list of items here, e.g., "1. Apples 2. Bananas 3. Oranges"]
    ```

    ChatGPT will generate an image of a scene where your list items are visually placed to aid memory.

6.  **Combine commands for deeper learning (optional)**: The author suggests combining these commands in a single chat session to refine your learning. For example, you could first generate handwritten notes from a text, and then, in the next turn, ask for a mind map of the same content. This helps you process information in different visual ways.

    ```
    /generatehandwrittenimage [paste your chapter text here]
    ```

    *Wait for the image to be generated.*

    ```
    Now, using the same chapter text, /mindmapimage
    ```

    You will first get an image of handwritten notes, and then a mind map, both based on your input text, helping you review the material from different angles.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Gemini (gemini.google.com) also offers image generation capabilities and has a usable free tier, which you could explore for similar visual learning aids.
*   **Common mistake**: A common mistake is not defining the 'commands' clearly at the start of the chat. If ChatGPT doesn't understand what `/mindmapimage` means, it won't generate the desired image. Always start a new chat by explaining what each keyword should do, as shown in Step 2.
*   **Tip for better results**: For better results, provide clear and concise text for the AI to work with. If your input is too long or vague, the generated image might not be as focused or accurate. Break down complex topics into smaller chunks. Experiment with different 'commands' and see which visual style helps you learn best. The 'memory palace' technique, for instance, can be surprisingly effective for ordered lists.
