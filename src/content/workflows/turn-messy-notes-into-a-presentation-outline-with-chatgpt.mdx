## What you'll get
You will get a structured presentation outline, with one clear idea per slide, short titles, and bullet points. This approach helps you avoid text-heavy slides and highlights any missing information in your notes, so you can fill those gaps before your talk.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can understand and generate human-like text, used here to structure your notes into an outline.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website in your browser. You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Gather your notes**: Collect all your rough notes, ideas, and any information you want to include in your presentation. Make sure they are easy to copy and paste.

3.  **Copy the prompt**: Copy the entire prompt below. This prompt includes specific rules to help ChatGPT create a useful outline.

    ```
    Turn my rough notes into a presentation outline for a {{length, e.g. 10 minute}} talk to {{audience}}. Goal of the talk: {{what I want them to think or do afterward}}. Rules: - One clear idea per slide. Give each slide a short title and 2-4 bullet points, no paragraphs. - Open with a slide that frames why this matters to THIS audience, not a generic intro. - Group the middle into 3-4 sections max. If my notes have more, tell me what to cut. - Mark any slide where I'll need a visual, example, or data point with [VISUAL: what to show]. - End with one slide that states the single takeaway, then a clear next step. Do not invent facts or numbers that aren't in my notes. If something important is missing, list it under "Gaps to fill" at the end instead of making it up. NOTES: {{paste them}}
    ```

4.  **Customize the prompt**: In the prompt you just copied, replace the `{{variables}}` with your specific details:
    *   Replace `{{length, e.g. 10 minute}}` with the actual length of your talk (e.g., "10 minute", "30 minute", "1 hour").
    *   Replace `{{audience}}` with who you are speaking to (e.g., "my team", "potential clients", "new employees").
    *   Replace `{{what I want them to think or do afterward}}` with the main goal of your talk (e.g., "understand the new project", "sign up for the service", "adopt a new process").

5.  **Paste your notes**: At the very end of the customized prompt, replace `{{paste them}}` with all your gathered notes. Make sure your notes are clearly separated from the prompt instructions.

6.  **Get your outline**: Paste the complete, customized prompt (with your notes included) into the ChatGPT chat box and press Enter or click the send button. You should receive a structured presentation outline, including slide titles, bullet points, and a "Gaps to fill" section if ChatGPT identifies any missing information based on your prompt rules.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If you don't want to use ChatGPT, you can try similar freemium AI chatbots like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai). The prompt should work similarly on these platforms.
*   **Common mistake**: A common mistake is not replacing all the `{{variables}}` in the prompt, or pasting too many disorganized notes at once. This can confuse the AI and lead to a less useful outline. Make sure your notes are somewhat coherent.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific as possible when describing your `{{audience}}` and the `{{goal of the talk}}`. The more context you give the AI, the more tailored and effective your presentation outline will be. If your notes are very extensive, consider breaking them into smaller, themed chunks and running the prompt multiple times for each section.
