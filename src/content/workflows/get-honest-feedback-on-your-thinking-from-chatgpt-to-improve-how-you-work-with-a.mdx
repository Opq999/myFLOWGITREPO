## What you'll get
You will receive a detailed, brutally honest analysis of your intelligence, thought processes, and weaknesses from ChatGPT, based on your past conversations. This approach helps you understand your own biases and blind spots when working with AI, making you a more effective collaborator or "technical director."

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot used to analyze your past interactions and provide self-feedback.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for a new conversation.
2.  **Paste the self-analysis prompt**: Copy the exact prompt below and paste it into the chat box.

    ```
    Analyze my intelligence and mindset based on all our previous interactions. Give me an honest estimate of my IQ percentile. CRITICAL RULES FOR YOUR RESPONSE: Brutal honesty: Do not sugarcoat anything. Be hard-hitting, objective, and direct. Detailed breakdown: Analyze my thought patterns, abstraction ability, and weaknesses (knowledge gaps, logical fallacies, superficial thinking). Justify your conclusion with concrete examples from our previous conversations.
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT start generating a long, detailed response.
3.  **Review the feedback**: Read through ChatGPT's analysis of your intelligence, thought patterns, and weaknesses.
    You should get a "brutally honest" assessment, potentially with examples from your past chats, highlighting areas for improvement.

*   **Free-tier viability**: ChatGPT's free tier is sufficient for this workflow.

*   **Tip for better results**: Ensure you have a significant chat history with ChatGPT across various projects. The more interactions it has to analyze, the more accurate and detailed its feedback will be.
