## What you'll get
You will get a structured presentation outline that builds a clear argument, rather than just a list of bullet points. The outline will include 5 to 8 main sections (called "beats"), each with a compelling slide title (a claim, not just a topic) and 2 to 3 supporting lines taken directly from your meeting notes. This approach works by forcing the AI to create a narrative first, ensuring your presentation has a logical flow and a clear message.

## Tools you need
*   **AI Chatbot** (freemium): An online tool that uses artificial intelligence to understand your requests and generate text responses. You will use it to process your meeting notes and create the outline.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI Chatbot**: Go to your preferred AI chatbot's website (e.g., chatgpt.com) on your phone or computer. If you don't have an account, you may need to sign up, but most offer a free tier.
    You should see a chat interface where you can type messages.

2.  **Prepare your notes and audience details**: Gather your raw meeting notes and clearly identify your presentation's audience, along with what you want them to decide or do after seeing your presentation. This information is crucial for the AI to build a relevant outline.
    You should have your notes ready to copy and paste, and a clear idea of your audience and desired outcome.

3.  **Paste the prompt and your information**: Copy the entire prompt below. Paste it into the chat window of your AI chatbot. Then, replace `[PASTE YOUR RAW MEETING NOTES HERE]` with your actual notes, and `[DESCRIBE YOUR AUDIENCE AND WHAT THEY NEED TO DECIDE OR DO]` with your audience details.

    ```
    When you feed rough notes to a model and ask for slides, it just chops the notes into bullet points, one note per slide. You get a deck with no argument, just a transcript with borders. This makes it build a narrative spine first, then map slides onto it.

    Here are my raw meeting notes: [PASTE YOUR RAW MEETING NOTES HERE]

    Audience for the presentation: [DESCRIBE YOUR AUDIENCE AND WHAT THEY NEED TO DECIDE OR DO] and what they need to decide or do after.

    Step 1: From these notes, state the one thing this presentation needs the audience to walk away believing.

    Step 2: Lay out 5 to 8 beats that get them there: where they are now, the problem, why it matters to them, the shift, what it means, the ask.

    Step 3: For each beat, give a slide title (a claim, not a topic) and 2 to 3 supporting lines from my notes. Do not use a note that does not support a beat. Tell me which notes you dropped and why.
    ```

    You should see the AI chatbot processing your request and then generating a structured outline, including a core message, a sequence of beats, slide titles, supporting points, and a list of dropped notes with explanations.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If ChatGPT's free tier isn't available or suitable, you can try other freemium AI chatbots like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com). The prompt should work similarly across these platforms.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is not providing enough detail for the `Audience for the presentation` part. The more specific you are about who they are and what decision or action you want from them, the better the AI can tailor the narrative and claims.
*   **Tip for better results**: The author highlights that a "slide title that is a claim, not a topic" is key. For example, "Q3 Results" is a topic, but "Q3 missed on one metric we can fix by Friday" is a claim. Aim for titles that make an argument. Also, the author suggests that having the AI report which notes it dropped helps ensure the outline is focused and avoids padding weak slides. You may still need to hand-tune the order of slides after the initial output, but this gets you most of the way there.
