## What you'll get
You will get personalized quizzes and flashcards generated directly from your study notes, designed to test your understanding and improve your memory. This approach helps you actively recall information, which is much more effective for learning than passively reading explanations.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can generate text, answer questions, and create study materials.

## Steps
1.  **Prepare your study notes**: Before you start, gather the notes you want to study. The author emphasizes that using your own notes is key to making these prompts effective. You can type them directly into the AI chat or copy-paste them from a document.
    You should have your study material ready to paste.
2.  **Start a quiz with graded questions**: Paste your notes into the chat, then use this prompt to have the AI quiz you on the material, starting with easier questions and moving to harder ones. The AI will wait for your answer after each question.

    ```
    I just studied [topic] from [chapter]. Don't explain anything yet. Ask me 5 questions, easiest to hardest. Wait for my answer after each one.
    ```

    The AI will ask its first question and wait for your response.
3.  **Get hints, not answers**: If you answer a question incorrectly, ask the AI for a hint. It will give you a short hint and let you try again before revealing the answer.

    ```
    Hints, not answers If I get one wrong, give me a one-sentence hint and let me try again. Only give the answer after my second miss.
    ```

    If you answer incorrectly, the AI will provide a one-sentence hint and prompt you to try again.
4.  **Create flashcards from your notes**: To review key terms and concepts, use this prompt to turn your notes into 15 flashcards. The AI will categorize each card as a definition, process, or common mistake.

    ```
    Turn these notes into 15 flashcards. No yes/no questions. Tag each one: definition, process, or common mistake.
    [paste notes here]
    ```

    You will receive a list of 15 flashcard-style questions and answers, each with a category tag.
5.  **Teach back a concept**: To solidify your understanding, explain a concept to the AI as if you're teaching a friend. The AI will interrupt you immediately if you say something wrong or vague.

    ```
    I'm going to explain [concept] like I'm teaching a friend who missed class. Stop me the second I say something wrong or vague.
    ```

    The AI will confirm it's ready for you to start explaining the concept.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: The author mentions that any AI chatbot with a free tier should work for these prompts. Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) is another excellent freemium option that can handle these tasks.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is asking the AI about a topic "cold" without providing your own notes first. The author warns that this often leads to the AI lecturing you, which is less effective for retention. Always paste your own study material first.
*   **Tip for better results**: To get the best results, ensure your notes are clear and well-organized before pasting them into the AI. The quality of the AI's output depends heavily on the quality of your input.
