## What you'll get
You'll transform a standard AI chatbot into a highly autonomous assistant, capable of taking your initial ideas and turning them into detailed, outcome-focused plans. This approach works by giving the AI a powerful set of instructions at the start of your conversation, telling it to act like a project manager that won't stop until the task is done.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): A popular AI chatbot for general conversations and tasks.
*   **Claude** (freemium): Another AI chatbot known for its strong reasoning and longer context windows.
*   **Gemini** (freemium): Google's AI chatbot, good for various tasks and integrated with Google services.

## Steps
1.  **Open your chosen AI chatbot**: Go to the website of your preferred freemium AI chatbot (like ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) on your phone or computer.
    *   You should see the main chat interface, ready for you to type a message.
2.  **Start a new conversation**: If you're not already in a new chat, find the "New Chat" or "Start a new conversation" button and click it.
    *   You should see a blank chat window, ready for your first message.
3.  **Paste the orchestration prompt**: Copy the entire prompt below and paste it into the message box of your AI chatbot.

    ```
    At the beginning of every conversation and throughout every subsequent exchange, automatically transform my needs, inquiries, and instructions into a sophisticated, comprehensive, outcome-directed prompt that establishes my exact intended objective, reconstructs all necessary logical and causal relationships, identifies missing information, and formulates one definitive autonomous orchestration plan beginning with the final outcome and its verifiable completion criteria. Present this transformed prompt in an editable text input interface at the bottom of every response, accompanied by a functional authorization control. Operate with relentless, uncompromising determination to achieve the highest attainable degree of successful task completion, treating every unfinished requirement, unresolved dependency, implementation failure, and unrealized opportunity for improvement as an immediate operational problem to investigate and resolve rather than a reason to stop, defer, or return to planning. Once the objective and execution pathway are established and authorized, immediately enter an intensive execution process i…
    ```

    *   You should see the long prompt appear in the message box. Press Enter or click the send button.
    *   The AI will process the prompt and might ask for your first task or objective. It may also try to simulate the "transformed prompt" as described in the instructions, though the "editable text input interface" and "authorization control" are conceptual instructions for the AI's behavior, not actual user interface elements you will see in a standard chat app.
4.  **Give your first task**: Now, tell the AI what you want to achieve. Start with a simple request or a complex problem you need help planning.
    *   Example: "I need to plan a marketing campaign for a new local bakery. Help me outline the steps."
    *   The AI should respond by trying to clarify your objective, ask for more information, and propose a detailed plan, acting as your "orchestration engine." It will likely break down the task into smaller steps and ask for your approval or input.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: All the suggested tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) offer free tiers that are sufficient for using this prompt. You might encounter limits on message length or speed, but the core functionality will be available.
*   **Common mistake**: Don't expect the AI to magically create new user interface elements like "editable text input interface" or "authorization control." These are instructions for *how the AI should think and present information*, not actual buttons or text boxes that will appear in your chat app. The AI will *simulate* this behavior by asking for your approval or presenting its refined prompt in text.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be very clear and specific with your initial task or objective after pasting the prompt. The more context you give the AI, the better it can apply its "orchestration plan." If it gets stuck, remind it of its "relentless, uncompromising determination" from the initial prompt.
