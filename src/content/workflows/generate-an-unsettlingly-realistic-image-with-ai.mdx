## What you'll get
You will get an image that looks like a real, unedited photograph, designed to be unsettlingly realistic. This approach works by focusing the AI on natural lighting, accurate materials, and believable imperfections, which helps avoid the typical "AI look" in generated images.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chat tool that can generate images from text descriptions.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI image generator**: Go to `chatgpt.com` in your web browser. If you don't have an account, you'll need to sign up (it's free to start).
    *   You should see: The chat interface ready for your input.
2.  **Paste the detailed prompt**: Copy the exact prompt below and paste it into the chat box.

    ```
    Use the full extent of your capabilities to create the most unsettlingly realistic image possible. Every element must be executed with absolute precision: natural lighting, physically accurate materials, authentic textures, believable imperfections, realistic depth, and coherent environmental detail. Avoid anything stylised, artificial, glossy, exaggerated, or recognisably AI-generated. The final image should look like an unedited photograph of a real moment, indistinguishable from reality even under close inspection. What do you get?
    ```

    *   You should see: The AI tool processing your request and then generating one or more images based on the prompt.
3.  **Review and refine (optional)**: Look closely at the generated image. Does it meet the criteria for realism? If not, you can ask the AI to make adjustments (for example, "Make the lighting softer," or "Add more subtle imperfections to the surface").
    *   You should see: The AI generating a revised image based on your feedback.

## Notes & variations
Other freemium tools like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com) can also generate images and might offer different results. A common mistake is expecting perfect realism on the first try. AI models sometimes struggle with subtle details or can introduce artifacts, so be prepared to iterate or refine the prompt. To get even better results, try adding a specific subject or scene to the prompt, like "...of a forgotten toy in a dusty corner" or "...of a single dewdrop on a spiderweb at dawn." This gives the AI more context to work with while maintaining the focus on realism.
