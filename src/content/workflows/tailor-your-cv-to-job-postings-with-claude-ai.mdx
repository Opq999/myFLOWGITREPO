## What you'll get
You will get a CV and cover message that are specifically tailored to a job posting you are interested in. This approach helps your application stand out by using the exact language and keywords found in the job description, making it more likely to pass automated screening and catch the recruiter's eye.

## Tools you need
*   **Claude** (freemium): An AI assistant you can chat with to help you write and rewrite text, like your CV and cover letters.

## Steps
1.  **Find a job posting**: Locate a job description for a role you want to apply for.
2.  **Open Claude**: Go to the Claude website and start a new chat.
3.  **Paste the job description**: Copy the entire job description text and paste it into the chat window.
4.  **Ask Claude to rewrite your CV**: Tell Claude to adapt your CV for the job. The author's process involved asking for a new headline, profile paragraph, reordered skills, and reworded bullet points to match the job's keywords. You should provide your current CV text when asking.

    ```
    Please rewrite my CV for the following job description. Focus on creating a new headline, a new profile paragraph, reordering my skills, and rewording my experience bullet points to use keywords from the job posting. Here is my current CV:

    [Paste your current CV text here]

    And here is the job description:

    [Paste the job description text here]
    ```

    You should receive a rewritten version of your CV that is tailored to the job posting.
5.  **Refine your CV**: Ensure the rewritten CV is one page long and maintains a consistent layout. You may need to manually add or adjust keywords or edits based on the specific posting.
6.  **Write a cover message (optional)**: If needed for the role, ask Claude to help you write a short cover message or recruiter note based on the job description and your tailored CV.

    ```
    Based on the job description and my tailored CV above, please write a short cover message for this role.
    ```

    You should get a draft cover message that you can further refine.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free tier alternative**: If Claude's free tier has usage limits that are too restrictive, you could try other free AI chat tools like ChatGPT (chat.openai.com) or Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) for similar text rewriting tasks.
*   **Common mistake**: Simply asking the AI to "make my CV better" without providing the job description will result in generic advice. Always provide the specific job posting for the best results.
*   **Tip for better results**: After the AI generates the tailored CV, read through it carefully. Ensure it sounds like you and accurately reflects your experience. You might want to add specific metrics or achievements that the AI might have missed.
