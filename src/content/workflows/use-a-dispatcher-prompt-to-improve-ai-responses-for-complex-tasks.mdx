## What you'll get
You will get a more focused and consistent AI response for complex requests. This approach works by first identifying the core need of your request and assigning it to a specialized AI role before generating an answer, preventing the AI from trying to do too many things at once.
## Tools you need
*   **AI Chat Tool** (freemium): A website or app where you can chat with an AI model. This is where you will paste the dispatcher prompt and your requests.
## Steps
1.  **Open your AI Chat Tool**: Go to your preferred AI chat website (like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini) and start a new chat.
2.  **Paste the dispatcher prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into the message box. This prompt tells the AI how to act as a dispatcher.

    ```
    You are a dispatcher/operator AI assistant. Your job is to listen to the user's request and route it to the most appropriate specialized role. 1) Inputs - You will receive a user message containing a problem, question, or task. - Optionally, you may receive context such as goals, constraints, prior attempts, or preferences. 2) Core Task (Routing) 1. Read the user's message a…
    ```

    You should see the AI acknowledge its role as a dispatcher.
3.  **Send your request**: After pasting the dispatcher prompt, type your actual request or question into the message box and send it. For example, you could ask it to help plan a research paper, write a section of text, or troubleshoot a technical problem.

    ```
    [Paste your complex request here]
    ```

    The AI will first respond by identifying the main objective, selecting a relevant specialist role, and then answering from that perspective. If it needs more information, it will ask clarifying questions.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free Tier Tip**: Most major AI chat platforms offer a free tier that works well for this. Ensure you are using the main chat interface, not a paid developer API.
*   **Common Pitfall**: If the AI doesn't seem to be routing correctly, try rephrasing your request to be clearer about the primary goal. Sometimes, very complex or ambiguous requests can still confuse the dispatcher.
*   **Better Results**: You can customize the list of specialist roles in the prompt to better match your specific needs. For example, if you do a lot of coding, you might add roles like 'Python Expert' or 'Data Analyst'.
