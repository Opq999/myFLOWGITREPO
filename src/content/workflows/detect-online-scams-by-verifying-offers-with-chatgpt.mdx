## What you'll get
You'll learn to use ChatGPT to quickly assess if an online offer or link is legitimate or a potential scam. This approach works because ChatGPT can analyze text and URLs for common scam indicators, helping you avoid financial loss or sharing sensitive data.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): To analyze links and text for scam indicators.
*   **OLX** (freemium): A marketplace where you might find offers to verify.
*   **Allegro Lokalnie** (freemium): Another marketplace for finding offers.
*   **WhatsApp** (free): A messaging app where you might receive suspicious links.

## Steps
1.  **Find an online offer**: Browse marketplaces like OLX or Allegro Lokalnie for items you want to buy.
    You'll find an interesting offer and start communicating with a seller, potentially receiving a link to another platform or a payment page.
2.  **Copy the suspicious link or text**: If a seller sends you a link that seems slightly off, or asks you to pay through an unusual method, copy the full link or the relevant text.
    You'll have the URL or text ready to paste.
3.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You'll see the chat interface, ready for your input.
4.  **Paste the link and ask for verification**: Paste the link or text into the chat box and ask ChatGPT to check its legitimacy. The author doesn't share their exact prompt; a starting point:

    ```
    I received this link for an online offer. Can you help me check if it's legitimate or a potential scam? Please look for any red flags like unusual URLs, suspicious payment methods, or common phishing tactics.
    [paste the link or text here]
    ```

    ChatGPT will analyze the link or text and provide an assessment, pointing out any suspicious elements it finds. It might highlight things like domain mismatches, requests for unusual payment details, or pressure tactics.
5.  **Review ChatGPT's assessment**: Read ChatGPT's response carefully. If it identifies red flags, do not proceed with the offer.
    You'll have a clearer understanding of the offer's legitimacy and can decide whether to continue or abandon it.

## Original source
This workflow is inspired by Reddit user /u/KatteaLBN, who shared their experience on the platform. They used ChatGPT to verify a link for a pigeon loft they found on OLX, which led to the AI detecting a potential scam.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) can also be used for this purpose, offering similar free tiers for text analysis.
*   **Common mistake**: Relying solely on AI. While AI is helpful, always use your own judgment. If something feels too good to be true, it probably is. Scammers constantly evolve their methods.
*   **Tip for better results**: Provide as much context as possible to the AI. Instead of just the link, also mention where you found the offer (e.g., "I found this on OLX, the seller sent me this link via WhatsApp"). This helps the AI understand the full situation.
