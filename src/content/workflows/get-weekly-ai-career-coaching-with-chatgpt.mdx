## What you'll get
You'll get a personalized, weekly career development plan from an AI coach, delivered directly in your chat. This approach helps you build skills consistently without feeling overwhelmed, by breaking down large goals into small, manageable tasks.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that acts as your personal career coach.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to `chatgpt.com` on your phone or computer. You should see the chat interface.
2.  **Start a new chat**: Look for a "New chat" button or icon (usually a plus sign or a chat bubble). Click it to start a fresh conversation. You should see an empty chat window with a text input box at the bottom.
3.  **Paste the coaching prompt**: Copy the prompt below and paste it into the message box. Remember to replace `[My goal is to transition into Strategic Partnerships over the next few years]` and `[tailor examples to my current job and long-term career goals]` with your own specific career goal and context.

    ```
    Act as my career coach. My goal is to transition into Strategic Partnerships over the next few years. I get overwhelmed by large learning plans, so break everything into small, sequential weekly lessons. Every Monday, give me: One short video (10–20 minutes) One article or short reading One business concept to think about One practical exercise I can complete in 15–20 minutes Each week should build on the previous one. Prioritize consistency over volume, avoid overwhelming me, and tailor examples to my current job and long-term career goals.
    ```

    You should receive your first week's coaching plan, including a video, article, concept, and exercise.
4.  **Engage weekly**: Every Monday, return to this chat thread. You can simply type "Next week's plan, please" or "Continue with the coaching plan." The AI should provide the next set of learning materials and exercises, building on the previous week. You should see a new set of resources tailored to your progression.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Other freemium AI chatbots like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) can also be used for this workflow with similar results.
*   **Common mistake**: Forgetting to specify your *exact* career goal and current situation. The more detail you give the AI about your background and what you want to achieve, the more personalized and useful the advice will be.
*   **Tip for better results**: After each week, give the AI feedback on what worked well or what you'd like to focus on more. For example, "That exercise was really helpful, can we do more practical tasks next week?" or "The video was a bit too long, can you find shorter ones?" This helps the AI adapt and improve its coaching over time.
