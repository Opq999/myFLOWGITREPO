## What you'll get
You'll get a structured explanation of any new topic, with the AI explicitly stating its confidence (high, medium, or low) for each main point. It will also tell you which parts are worth verifying elsewhere, point out common beginner misunderstandings, and suggest a next question to deepen your knowledge. This approach helps you learn complex subjects accurately by guiding you on what information to double-check, preventing you from accepting potentially incorrect AI-generated facts.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot to generate the confidence-checked explanation of your chosen topic.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI chatbot**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    *   You should see the chat interface ready for you to type a message.

2.  **Prepare your learning prompt**: Copy the special prompt below. Replace `[Replace this with your topic, e.g., "quantum entanglement" or "the history of Nigerian currency"]` with the subject you want to learn.
    *   The prompt is designed to make the AI explain clearly and honestly about its confidence.

    ```
    Explain the following topic to me at the level of someone smart but new to it. After each main point, mark how confident you are, high, medium, or low, and for anything medium or low tell me it is worth verifying elsewhere. Do not smooth over areas where experts actually disagree, name the disagreement instead. At the end, list the three things beginners most commonly get wrong about this and the one question I should ask next to go deeper. If any part of this is outside what you reliably know, say so rather than filling the gap.
    Topic: [Replace this with your topic, e.g., "quantum entanglement" or "the history of Nigerian currency"]
    ```

    *   You should have the complete prompt ready to paste, with your chosen topic inserted.

3.  **Paste and send the prompt**: Paste the modified prompt into the chat box and press Enter or click the send button.
    *   The AI will start generating an explanation of your topic, including confidence levels, areas for verification, common mistakes, and a follow-up question.

4.  **Review the AI's explanation**: Read through the AI's response carefully. Pay close attention to points marked "medium" or "low" confidence, and note the areas it suggests verifying elsewhere.
    *   You should have a clear understanding of the topic, knowing which parts are reliable and which require further research from other sources.

## Notes & variations
*   Free-tier alternatives: This prompt works well with most freemium AI chatbots, including Claude (claude.ai) and Google Gemini (gemini.google.com).
*   Common mistake: A common pitfall is forgetting to replace `[Replace this with your topic...]` in the prompt, or choosing a topic that is too broad for a single, concise explanation. Try to narrow down your focus for better results.
*   Tip for better results: Be as specific as possible when defining your `Topic`. Instead of "history," try "the history of Nigerian currency from 1960 to 1990." This helps the AI provide a more focused and accurate response.
