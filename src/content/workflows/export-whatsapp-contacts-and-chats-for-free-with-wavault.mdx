## What you'll get
You will get a free, unlimited way to export your WhatsApp contacts, group members, and entire chat histories. This approach works because WAVault is an open-source browser extension that reads data directly from WhatsApp Web within your browser, without sending it to any servers.

## Tools you need
*   **WAVault** (free): A browser extension that adds export features to WhatsApp Web.
*   **WhatsApp Web** (freemium): The web version of WhatsApp that runs in your browser.
*   **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** (free): A modern web browser where you can install extensions.
*   **Excel** (paid): Spreadsheet software to open and manage exported data in .xlsx format. Free alternatives like Google Sheets can also be used.
*   **CSV** (free): A common file format for spreadsheets, usable by many programs.
*   **vCard** (free): A standard file format for contact information, usable by most phones and contact apps.
*   **Google Contacts** (free): A service to manage your contacts online.
*   **Outlook** (paid): Email and contact management software from Microsoft.

## Steps
1.  **Install the WAVault extension**: Open your browser (Chrome or Edge) and go to the Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons store. Search for "WAVault" and click "Add to Chrome" or "Add to Edge". You should see the WAVault icon appear in your browser's toolbar.
2.  **Open WhatsApp Web**: Go to `web.whatsapp.com` and log in using your phone. Make sure you can see your chats.
3.  **Access WAVault dashboard**: Click the WAVault icon in your browser's toolbar. A dashboard will open within WhatsApp Web, showing different sections like "Contacts", "Group members", "Full chat backup", and "Status downloader".
4.  **Export contacts**: Navigate to the "Contacts" section in the WAVault dashboard. You can choose from 15 different lists (e.g., Saved, Chatted, Group members) and apply filters. Click the "Export" button and select your desired format (Excel, CSV, Google Contacts CSV, vCard). The file will download to your computer.
5.  **Export group members**: Go to the "Group members" section. Select the groups you want to export. Click the "Export" button and choose the Excel format. You will get a separate sheet for each group in the downloaded file.
6.  **Backup full chats**: In the "Full chat backup" section, select the chats you want to export. WAVault will load the conversations page by page. Click "Export" to download a ZIP file containing an HTML viewer for your chats, including media.
7.  **Download statuses**: Go to the "Status downloader" section. Select the statuses you want to save. Click "Download" to get a ZIP file of the statuses with a gallery page.

## Notes & variations
*   **Common mistake**: Trying to export without WhatsApp Web fully loaded or while the WAVault dashboard is not visible can lead to incomplete exports. Ensure WhatsApp Web is active and the WAVault panel is open.
*   **Tip for better results**: For large exports, ensure you have a stable internet connection. If exporting chat history, the resulting HTML viewer can be opened offline, making it a great way to archive conversations.
*   **Free tier alternative**: WAVault itself is the free alternative. Most other tools on the Chrome Web Store have very limited free tiers (10-30 contacts) and require payment for full functionality.
