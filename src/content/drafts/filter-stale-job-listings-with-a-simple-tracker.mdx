## What you'll get
You will learn a simple habit to avoid wasting time on job listings that are no longer active. This approach helps you focus your energy on genuinely open positions by adding a quick check before you invest time in applying.

## Tools you need
*   **Spreadsheet Software** (free): To keep a simple tracker of job listings.

## Steps
1.  **Create a tracker**: Open your spreadsheet software and create a new sheet. Set up the following columns: Company, Role, Date Seen, Source, Resume Version.
2.  **Identify potential roles**: When browsing job boards, look for roles that seem recently posted, ideally within the last 24-72 hours.
3.  **Check the company career page**: Before spending significant time on an application, open the company's official career page. Look for the specific role you found.
4.  **Verify the role is live**: If the role is still listed on the company's career page, it's likely still active. If it's not there, or if the company page itself is gone, consider it stale.
5.  **Record the listing (if live)**: If the role is live, add its details to your tracker: the company name, the job title, today's date, where you found the listing (e.g., LinkedIn, company website), and which version of your resume you plan to use.
6.  **Skip obvious reposts or dead links**: If a listing appears to be an old one reposted, or if the company's career page is no longer available, do not proceed with it or record it. Move on to the next listing.
7.  **Apply strategically**: When you are ready to apply, use autofill for repetitive fields. However, always read through any custom questions on the application form before submitting.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternative**: If you don't have spreadsheet software, a simple text file or even a notebook can serve as a basic tracker, though it will be less organized.
*   **Common pitfall**: A common mistake is to skip checking the company's career page altogether, assuming the job board listing is always up-to-date. This can lead to wasted effort on closed positions.
*   **Tip for better results**: Regularly review your tracker. This can help you identify companies that frequently post jobs you're interested in, allowing you to check their career pages directly in the future.
