## What you'll get
You will get a hyper-realistic video clip showing a lightning storm from space, starting from a city view and ascending into Earth's atmosphere and deep space. This approach uses a detailed text prompt to guide an AI model in creating complex visual scenes, allowing for creative control without needing advanced video editing skills.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can generate text and, in some versions or with plugins, create images or interact with other tools.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer. You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Paste the video prompt**: Copy the detailed prompt below and paste it into the message box in ChatGPT:

    ```
    A hyper realistic real life high HD graphic footage. Shaky cam motion shot in 4k of nighttime, highly-detailed city. Fast moving clouds and lightening striking across the sky, the setting takes place at night outside a large city dark and eerie, city lights are off totall. And all of a sudden cam flies straight up into the sky quickly until your extremely high up showing only the glimpse of the city, oceans background continents that are bearly visible in the night from the bottom at high attitude and oceans, as you continue to fly straight up into you reach thick clouds of lightening covering the screen for a short moment before emerging up out of the clouds, revealing a extremely stunning and yet beautiful space scene, of heading out of the atmosphere from the night terminator side of the earth only. with real-time wavelength scattering of red in the atmosphere from behind earth, raise up slowly revealing the sun and flare brightly illuminating the front side of the planet in day making the deep dark space background completely black from light pollution highly realistically
    ```

3.  **Generate the video**: Press Enter or click the send button. The author states they received a "cosmetic lightening storm shot". Depending on your ChatGPT version and available features, you might get a video directly, a link to a video, or a message indicating that video generation is not supported or requires a specific plugin or subscription. If video generation is not available, you can use this prompt with a dedicated text-to-video AI tool (see Notes & variations).

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: As noted, ChatGPT's free tier typically does not generate video. For free or freemium text-to-video alternatives, consider tools like RunwayML (limited free tier) or Pika Labs (often has a free tier or trial). You would paste the same detailed prompt into these tools.
*   **Common mistake or pitfall**: A common pitfall is expecting a perfect video on the first try. AI video generation can be unpredictable. You might need to adjust the prompt, add more details, or try different phrasing to get the desired result.
*   **Tip for better results**: To get better results, be extremely specific in your prompt. Describe camera angles, lighting, motion, and desired emotions or atmosphere. Break down complex scenes into smaller, clearer descriptions if the AI struggles.
