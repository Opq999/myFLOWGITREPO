## What this is, in plain English
This workflow uses advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) to help you understand your personal interests by analyzing your past consumption history. The idea is to feed an AI model, specifically a Large Language Model (LLM), data from what you've read, watched, and listened to over time. The AI then processes this information to identify patterns, themes, and underlying interests that might not be obvious to you.

This approach is considered advanced because it requires significant effort to extract and prepare your personal data from various platforms. Many services do not offer easy, structured data exports. Additionally, interacting with advanced LLMs to perform complex analysis, especially using concepts like "agents" (specialized AI instructions), requires comfort with advanced prompting and data handling.

There isn't a simple copy-paste recipe because the exact steps for data extraction vary greatly by platform and user, and the AI analysis part depends heavily on the specific data you provide and your skill in guiding the AI.

## What you can use it for
*   **Uncover hidden interests**: Discover themes or topics you consistently engage with across different media, revealing passions you might not have consciously recognized.
*   **Inform content creation**: Understand what truly resonates with you, which can guide your own creative projects, writing, or business ventures.
*   **Personalized recommendations**: Use the identified interests to find new books, podcasts, or videos that genuinely align with your taste.
*   **Track personal growth**: See how your interests have evolved over time by analyzing consumption data from different periods.
*   **Understand decision-making**: Gain insight into the 'how' and 'why' behind your preferences, from products you use to content you consume.

## How it actually works
This workflow involves several high-level stages, each requiring technical comfort and careful execution:

1.  **Extract your consumption data**: The first and most challenging step is to gather your history from various platforms like Kindle, Spotify, YouTube, and Readwise. The author mentions using an AI assistant, which they refer to as "Codex," to help with this. This likely involves using platform-specific data export features, APIs (Application Programming Interfaces, which allow programs to talk to each other), or custom scripts. The author doesn't share the exact method, but it's rarely a simple download and often requires technical skill to get data in a usable format.

2.  **Consolidate and prepare data**: Once extracted, you'll need to collect all this data into a single, organized folder. The author doesn't specify the format (e.g., text files, CSV, JSON), but it needs to be in a structure that an AI can process. This often involves cleaning the data, removing irrelevant entries, and ensuring consistency.

3.  **Analyze with an advanced LLM**: The author then used Claude Opus 5.5, a powerful Large Language Model (LLM), to analyze the prepared data. This involves feeding the data to the AI and prompting it to look for patterns. The author asked the AI to "show me what my consumption looked like" and to "try a few different agents with no context to look at the data to see what it thinks my interests are." This implies using advanced prompting techniques or specialized AI configurations (agents) to guide the analysis.

4.  **Interpret AI insights**: The AI's output will be its interpretation of your interests. The author found that the AI reaffirmed nuanced interests, focusing on the "how" and "why" behind choices, rather than just broad categories like "business books." You'll need to review and interpret these insights to understand your true consumption patterns.

## Words you'll see, explained
*   **AI (Artificial Intelligence)**: Computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning, problem-solving, and understanding language.
*   **LLM (Large Language Model)**: A type of AI program trained on vast amounts of text data, capable of understanding, generating, and summarizing human-like text.
*   **Agent (AI agent)**: In the context of LLMs, an agent is an AI system designed to perform specific tasks or follow a set of instructions, often by interacting with tools or other systems.
*   **Consumption history**: A record of the content (books, music, videos, articles) that a person has engaged with over a period of time.

## Notes & variations
*   **Do you even need this?**: For many, a simpler approach like manually reviewing your top 10 books, podcasts, or articles from the past year, or keeping a journal of what you consume and why you liked it, might be sufficient to identify interests without the technical complexity of this AI workflow.
*   **Free-tier limits**: While some data sources like YouTube offer free access, advanced LLMs like Claude Opus often require a paid subscription or API credits. Extracting large amounts of data from services like Spotify or YouTube can also consume significant internet data.
*   **Common pitfall**: Data privacy is a major concern when exporting and processing your personal consumption history. Ensure you understand how your data is handled by any tools you use and be cautious about sharing sensitive information. Also, the accuracy of AI analysis depends heavily on the quality and completeness of the data you provide.
