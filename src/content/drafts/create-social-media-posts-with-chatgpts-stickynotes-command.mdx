## What you'll get
You will get a set of visually appealing 'sticky notes' that summarize your text, ready to be used as social media graphics. This approach helps you quickly turn long articles or notes into clear, readable sections, making it faster to create content for platforms like Instagram or Pinterest than using a separate design tool.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (paid): An AI chatbot that can generate text and images. For this workflow, you need a paid ChatGPT Plus subscription to use its image generation feature (DALL-E 3).

## Steps

1.  **Open ChatGPT and enable image generation**: Go to the ChatGPT website. Make sure you are logged in with a ChatGPT Plus account, as image generation is a paid feature. The model should automatically be set to a version that supports DALL-E 3 (e.g., GPT-4o or GPT-4 with DALL-E 3 enabled).

    You should see the chat interface ready for your prompt.

2.  **Start your prompt with /stickynotes**: In the message box, type `/stickynotes` at the very beginning of your prompt. This special command tells ChatGPT to format its output as visual sticky notes.

    The command will appear in the message box, ready for you to add more instructions.

3.  **Describe your content**: After `/stickynotes`, tell ChatGPT what you want to create and how. The author doesn't share their exact prompt; a starting point could be:

    ```
    /stickynotes Summarize this article about starting a small business in Nigeria into 5 key points, each on a separate sticky note. Make them suitable for an Instagram carousel. The article text is:
    [Paste your article or long notes here]
    ```

    You should see your full prompt in the message box.

4.  **Generate the sticky notes**: Press the 'Enter' key or click the send button (usually a paper airplane icon). ChatGPT will process your request and generate an image with your main ideas broken down into clear, readable sticky notes.

    You should see an image appear in the chat, showing your summarized content on a visual layout of sticky notes. You can then download this image to use on social media.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternative**: If you don't have a ChatGPT Plus subscription, you can still achieve a similar result in two steps. First, use a free AI chatbot (like Gemini's free tier or Copilot) to summarize your text into key points. Then, use a free graphic design tool like Canva's free tier to manually create sticky note-style graphics with the summarized text.
*   **Common mistake**: Forgetting to type `/stickynotes` at the very beginning of your prompt, or not having a ChatGPT Plus subscription enabled for image generation. Without the command, ChatGPT will likely just give you a text summary. Without the paid subscription, it won't generate images at all.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be very specific in your prompt. Tell ChatGPT the exact number of sticky notes you want, the style (e.g., 'colorful and playful' or 'minimalist and professional'), and the target platform (e.g., 'for an Instagram carousel' or 'for a Pinterest graphic'). This helps the AI create visuals that better match your needs.
