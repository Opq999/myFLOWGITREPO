Instead of you giving step-by-step instructions, an "orchestrator agent" (a main AI program) manages other smaller AI agents to complete a big goal.

## Words you'll see, explained

*   **Orchestrator Agent**: A main AI agent that manages and coordinates other, smaller AI agents to complete a complex task.
*   **Agentic AI**: A system where AI models work together as agents, delegating tasks and collaborating to achieve a goal, rather than just responding to single prompts.

## Notes & variations
*   **Do you even need this?** For simpler tasks like generating a single chart or doing basic research, you might not need a full agentic AI platform. You could use a freemium chat model (like the free tiers of Claude.ai, ChatGPT, or Gemini) to analyze data and suggest chart types, then use a separate, free charting tool (like Google Sheets or Canva) to create the visualization yourself.
*   **Free-tier limits**: Advanced agentic AI platforms like Claude Projects are typically paid services, designed for developers and businesses. They do not usually offer a free tier for complex agent workflows, unlike consumer chat applications.
*   **Common pitfall**: Over-delegating without clear objectives can lead to irrelevant or unhelpful results. Even with agentic AI, providing a clear initial goal and specific feedback during iterations is crucial for getting the desired outcome.
