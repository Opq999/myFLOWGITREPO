## What you'll get
You will get a version of your resume that is specifically tailored to a job description, making it more likely to catch the attention of recruiters. This approach helps you avoid "prompt drift" (when an AI's responses get worse over time in a conversation) and ensures the AI doesn't make your resume too long or rephrase points unnecessarily.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that helps you write and edit text, useful for tailoring your resume.
*   **AIPRM** (freemium): A browser extension that adds pre-made prompts to ChatGPT, which some users find helpful for specific tasks like resume generation.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT and start a new chat**: Go to the ChatGPT website (chatgpt.com) and click on "New chat" to begin a fresh conversation. This helps prevent the AI from getting confused by previous interactions.
    You should see a blank chat window ready for your input.

2.  **Prepare your resume and job description**: Have your full resume text and the job description you want to apply for ready. You will need to copy and paste these into the chat.
    You should have both documents open and ready to select text from.

3.  **Craft your tailoring prompt**: The author doesn't share their exact initial prompt; a good starting point is to ask the AI to tailor your resume, adding the specific instructions the author found helpful to prevent issues. Copy and paste the following prompt, replacing the bracketed sections with your actual resume and job description:

    ```
    Tailor my resume for this job description. Limit the resume length to 2 pages. Do not unnecessarily reword already good points, merge different points, or delete important points.

    Here is my resume:
    [Paste your full resume here]

    Here is the job description:
    [Paste the full job description here]
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT processing your request and then generating a tailored version of your resume.

4.  **Review the output**: Carefully read the tailored resume provided by ChatGPT. Check that it meets the 2-page limit, highlights relevant skills from the job description, and hasn't removed important information or reworded points poorly.
    You should have a resume that is more focused on the job you're applying for.

5.  **Repeat for new job applications**: For each new job description, start a completely new chat in ChatGPT (Step 1) and use the same prompt structure. This helps maintain the quality of the AI's responses and prevents "prompt drift."
    You should consistently get well-tailored resumes for each new application.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If you don't want to use ChatGPT, you can try similar AI chatbots like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai), which also offer free tiers for text generation tasks.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is not including specific constraints in your prompt, which can lead to the AI generating overly long resumes (e.g., 5 pages instead of 2) or rephrasing points unnecessarily. Always include the length and content constraints from Step 3.
*   **Tip for better results**: The original author found that tailoring one section of the resume at a time (instead of the whole document) can sometimes yield better results. You can also try challenging the AI if the first response isn't perfect; the author noted that asking it to improve its response often helped slightly.
