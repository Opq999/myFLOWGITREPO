## What this is, in plain English
This workflow explores how an advanced AI can play complex video games by "seeing" the game screen (through screenshots) and "acting" (through mouse controls). Instead of being given specific game rules, the AI learns to manage the game's economy and improve its own skills over time, much like a human player.

The original experiment used a future AI model, GPT 6 Astra, and the ChatGPT desktop app to achieve this. It highlights the potential for AI to understand and interact with dynamic visual environments without explicit programming for each game.

This is an advanced concept because it requires sophisticated AI capabilities to interpret visual information, make strategic decisions, and execute precise actions. The exact tools and methods used for such advanced automation are not yet widely available or easily reproducible for a beginner.

## How it actually works
The original author used a future AI model (GPT 6 Astra) and the ChatGPT desktop app. The process involved:

1.  **Setting up the game and AI interface**: The author likely configured the ChatGPT desktop app to capture screenshots of the game (Anno 117: Pax Romana) and send them to the AI. The author doesn't share the exact setup steps, as this functionality is not standard or publicly documented.
2.  **Providing high-level instructions**: The author gave the AI a general goal: "keep the economy healthy and improve its own game skills as it progresses." This is a key aspect of advanced AI agents, where they learn from broad objectives rather than specific commands.
3.  **AI interpretation and action**: The AI, using the visual input from screenshots, would then interpret the game state, make decisions to achieve its goals, and send mouse control commands back through the desktop app to interact with the game.
4.  **Continuous learning**: The AI continuously processed new screenshots, learned from its actions, and refined its strategy over 6 hours of gameplay.

To attempt something similar today, you would need to explore advanced AI agent frameworks that combine computer vision (to interpret screenshots) with large language models (to make decisions) and robotic process automation (RPA) tools (to control the mouse). This typically involves coding and integrating multiple technical components.

*   **AI Agent**: An artificial intelligence program designed to perceive its environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific goals.

*   **Mouse Control**: The ability of an AI or program to move the mouse cursor and click buttons on a computer screen, just like a human user.
*   **GPT 6 Astra**: A future, advanced AI model mentioned in the original experiment, expected to have sophisticated capabilities for understanding and interacting with complex environments.
