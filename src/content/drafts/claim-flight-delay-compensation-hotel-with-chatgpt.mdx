## What you'll get
You will learn your passenger rights for flight delays under European law, secure a hotel and dinner from the airline, and successfully claim compensation (e.g., 400 Euros) for your inconvenience. This approach uses AI to quickly get accurate legal information, saving you time and stress when dealing with airline staff.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot to understand your rights and draft claims.
*   **Booking.com** (free): A hotel comparison website to check hotel availability.

## Steps
1.  **Describe your situation to ChatGPT**: Explain your flight delay, missed connection, and the airline's refusal to provide a hotel.

    ```
    My flight was delayed, causing me to miss a connection. The airline rebooked me for the next day but refused to provide a hotel or dinner. This happened in Europe. What are my rights under European law regarding flight delays and compensation?
    ```

    You should get a detailed response outlining your rights, including the airline's obligation to provide accommodation and meals.

2.  **Verify hotel availability**: If the airline claims no hotels are available, use a hotel comparison website.
    *   Go to a hotel comparison website (e.g., Booking.com).
    *   Search for hotels near the airport for your overnight stay.

    You should see available hotels in the area, confirming the airline's claim is false.

3.  **Confront the airline with your rights**: Use the information from ChatGPT and your hotel search.
    *   Tell the airline staff that you know your rights under European law (e.g., EC 261/2004) and that they are obligated to provide a hotel and dinner.
    *   Mention that you have checked and found hotels available nearby.

    The airline should eventually agree to provide you with a hotel and meal vouchers.

4.  **Draft a compensation claim**: Ask ChatGPT to help you write a formal claim for compensation.

    ```
    Help me draft a formal claim for flight delay compensation. My flight was delayed, causing me to miss a connection and an overnight stay. The airline initially refused a hotel but later provided one after I insisted on my rights under European law. The delay was significant. What information do I need to include, and what is the typical compensation amount for a significant delay in Europe?
    ```

    You should receive guidance on what details to include (flight number, dates, delay duration, reason for delay if known) and an estimate of the compensation amount (e.g., 400 Euros for long-haul delays).

5.  **Follow the claim process**: Use ChatGPT to guide you through submitting the claim.

    ```
    I have drafted my claim. What are the next steps to submit it to the airline? Should I include any specific documents?
    ```

    You should get instructions on how to submit your claim, typically through the airline's customer service portal or a dedicated compensation form, and a list of documents to attach (e.g., boarding passes, booking confirmations).

## Notes & variations
*   The author noted that the free version of ChatGPT provided incorrect compensation amounts (250 Euros instead of 400 Euros) and less detail. For critical legal or financial advice, consider using the paid version (ChatGPT Plus) or cross-referencing with official sources.
*   A common mistake is accepting the airline's initial refusal without knowing your rights. Always verify their claims, especially regarding hotel availability or legal obligations.
*   Be specific in your prompts to ChatGPT. Include details like the region (e.g., "under European law"), the duration of the delay, and the airline's actions. This helps the AI provide more accurate and relevant information.
