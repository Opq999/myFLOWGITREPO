## What you'll get
You will get a unique editorial-style poster from your photo. Each poster is split into two halves: the top half preserves your original image, and the bottom half reinterprets it as a minimalist layered paper-cut artwork. This approach creates a clean, artistic look, perfect for social media, personal projects, or adding a creative touch to your images without needing complex design software.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT (with DALL-E 3)** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can generate images from text descriptions.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI image generator**: Go to ChatGPT (or your preferred AI image generator) on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for your input.
2.  **Prepare your photo**: Choose a photo with a clear subject that you want to transform into a poster.
    You should have a photo ready on your device.
3.  **Upload your photo and add the prompt**: First, upload your chosen photo to the chat. Then, copy and paste the following prompt into the message box. The AI will use your uploaded image as the "input photo" mentioned in the prompt.

    ```

You may gently extend the sky, ground, or surrounding background to fit the composition, but do not stretch, distort, or alter the subject.     ```

    The AI should start generating the image based on your photo and the prompt.
4.  **Review and download your poster**: Once the AI generates the image, look at the result. If you like it, download it to your device.
    You should see a new image appear in the chat, showing your photo transformed into a split-style poster. You can then save it.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Other freemium AI image generators like Microsoft Copilot (which also uses DALL-E 3) or Google Gemini (with its image generation capabilities) can also be used with similar prompts.
*   **Common mistake**: The provided prompt from the original source is cut off at the end. If your results aren't quite what you expect for the paper-cut bottom half, you might need to experiment by adding your own descriptive words to complete the thought, such as "Translate a few supporting elements into simplified paper-cut forms" or "Translate a few supporting details into abstract paper shapes."
*   **Tip for better results**: For best results, choose photos with a clear main subject and simple backgrounds. The AI will have an easier time separating the subject for the paper-cut effect. You can also try describing your photo briefly before the prompt, e.g., "Here is a photo of a person standing on a mountain. Create one standalone editorial-style poster..."
