## What you'll get
You'll get automated AI routines that run on a schedule, even when your computer is off. These routines can help you stay on top of follow-ups, track business activity, and plan your week without needing your device to be active. This works because Claude's "Routines" feature runs on Anthropic's servers, ensuring tasks are completed reliably in the cloud.

## Tools you need
*   **Claude** (freemium): An AI assistant that can run scheduled tasks and interact with your email and calendar.

## Steps
1.  **Open Claude and find Routines**: Open the Claude desktop app. Look for "Code" on the left side menu, then click "Routines". The author notes to ignore the "Code" label, as this section uses plain English instructions on a timer.
    You should see a section for creating and managing scheduled routines.
2.  **Create a new routine**: Click the button to create a new routine.
    A new routine setup screen will appear, asking for details like a name, schedule, and instructions.
3.  **Set the routine to run in the Cloud**: Look for a setting, usually in the top right corner of the routine setup screen, and change it from "Local" to "Cloud". This is a crucial step.
    The setting should now clearly say "Cloud", meaning your routine will run even if your laptop is off or asleep.
4.  **Design your "Follow-up catcher" routine**: Give the routine a name like "Follow-up Catcher". Set the schedule (for example, "daily 5pm"). In the instruction box, paste the following prompt:
    ```
    Check my sent email for anyone I said I'd follow up with and haven't. Send me a short list of who I owe a reply or a next step, so nothing slips.
    ```
    Claude will process your instruction and show you a summary of what it will do. You might need to connect your email account if you haven't already.
5.  **Design your "Weekly numbers" routine**: Create another new routine. Name it "Weekly Numbers". Set the schedule (for example, "Fridays 4pm"). In the instruction box, paste the following prompt:
    ```
    Pull this week's activity from my connected apps, build a short report of what happened and what's still open, compare it to last week, and email it to me.
    ```
    Claude will show you a summary. You will need to connect relevant apps (such as project management tools or CRM software) within Claude's settings for this routine to work effectively.
6.  **Design your "Sunday planner" routine**: Create a third routine. Name it "Sunday Planner". Set the schedule (for example, "Sundays 6pm"). In the instruction box, paste the following prompt:
    ```
    Look at my calendar and open tasks for the week ahead. Email me a prioritized list of what I need to get done, most important first, with anything time-sensitive flagged.
    ```
    Claude will show you a summary. You will need to connect your calendar and any task management apps if you haven't already.
7.  **Turn on notifications (optional but recommended)**: In Claude's general settings or within each routine's settings, look for an option to turn on notifications for routine completions or results.
    You will receive alerts when your routines run or produce results, helping you stay informed.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier viability**: Claude's free tier should allow you to set up and test a few routines. If you need more complex or frequent routines, or higher usage, you might need a paid plan.
*   **Common mistake**: Forgetting to set the routine to "Cloud" mode. If it's set to "Local", the routine will only run when your computer is awake and the Claude app is open. Always double-check this setting to ensure your routines run reliably.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be very specific in your routine instructions. For example, instead of just "Summarize my week", try "Summarize my week's sales activities from [App Name] and list the top 3 leads to follow up on, then email it to me." The more detail you provide, the better Claude can perform the task.
