## What you'll get
This workflow provides a structured system prompt template designed to help Claude Fable 5 generate detailed stories with multiple characters and follow complex rules without getting confused. This approach works by using specific formatting (XML tags) to clearly separate your instructions from your input, making the AI more reliable for long, intricate creative tasks.

## Tools you need
*   **Claude** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can generate text, stories, and follow instructions.

## Steps
1.  **Open Claude**: Go to the Claude website in your mobile browser or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for your input.

2.  **Understand the prompt architecture**: The author highlights that Claude Fable 5 performs best with prompts that clearly separate different parts of your request. This is done using XML-like tags (like `<system_directive>`) and by defining all your creative settings in one place. This helps the AI understand exactly what each part of your prompt means.
    You should now have a basic understanding of why the prompt is structured this way.

3.  **Construct your optimized prompt**: The author describes a prompt architecture using XML tags for clarity and "single-mount dynamic inputs" (meaning you declare your creative settings once). Since the exact prompt from the source isn't provided, you'll need to create one following these principles. A starting point would involve structuring your request with these tags:

    ```
    <system_directive>
      [Your overall instructions for the AI, e.g., "You are a master storyteller. Generate a detailed narrative."]
    </system_directive>

    <creative_parameters>
      [Key settings for your story, e.g., "Genre: Fantasy, Tone: Epic, Length: 1000 words"]
    </creative_parameters>

    <character_details>
      [Information about your characters, e.g., "Protagonist: Elara, a skilled archer. Antagonist: Kael, a dark sorcerer."]
    </character_details>

    <narrative_context>
      [The starting point or background for your story, e.g., "The ancient prophecy foretells a hero will rise when the twin moons align."]
    </narrative_context>

    <user_input>
      [Your specific request for this turn, e.g., "Begin the story with Elara discovering a strange artifact in the Whispering Woods."]
    </user_input>
    ```
    This structure helps Claude Fable 5 understand which part is an instruction, a parameter, or your actual request. You should have a complete prompt ready to paste, filled with your specific story details.

4.  **Paste and generate**: Copy your structured prompt and paste it into the Claude chat box. Press Enter or click the send button.
    Claude Fable 5 will begin generating a narrative based on your detailed instructions and parameters. You should see a story that closely follows your specified genre, characters, and plot points.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Other AI chatbots like Gemini (gemini.google.com) or ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) also offer free tiers and can generate stories, though the specific XML-tag architecture might need adjustments to work best with them.
*   **Common mistake**: A common pitfall is not clearly separating your instructions from your actual content. If you mix them, the AI can get confused, leading to "style bleed" (where the AI starts writing like an instruction) or skipped instructions. Always use clear tags or distinct paragraphs for different types of information.
*   **Tip for better results**: Always test your structured prompt with short, simple requests first. This helps you confirm that Claude understands your chosen tags and parameters before you ask it to generate a very long or complex story. Adjust your tags or wording if the initial results aren't what you expect.
