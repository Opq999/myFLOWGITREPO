## What you'll get
You'll get a detailed, multi-perspective solution to a complex problem, drawing insights from seven different 'minds' like Albert Einstein, Nikola Tesla, and Steve Jobs. This approach helps you break down challenges, consider diverse viewpoints, and generate innovative, actionable strategies by simulating a 'Quantum Cognitive Council'.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot to process the multi-persona prompt and generate solutions.

1.  **Open ChatGPT**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer.
    You should see the chat interface ready for your input.


    You should see the prompt appear in the chat input area.
3.  **Define your problem**: After pasting the prompt, type your specific problem or challenge directly below it in the chat. For example:

    ```
    My problem is: How can a small business in Lagos effectively use social media to reach more customers without a large marketing budget?
    ```

    You should see the AI start processing your request after you send your message.
4.  **Review the AI's analysis and solution**: The AI will first present independent analyses from each of the seven 'minds,' then integrate them into a collective solution.
    You should get a structured response that breaks down the problem from different angles and offers actionable steps.

## Notes & variations
*   The prompt is optimized for GPT-4, which is part of ChatGPT Plus (a paid subscription). You can still use this prompt with the free version of ChatGPT (which uses GPT-3.5), but the results might be less detailed or insightful.
*   A common mistake is not clearly defining your problem. Be specific and provide enough context for the AI to give relevant advice.
*   To get even better results, you can ask follow-up questions to specific 'minds' after the initial response. For example: 'Elon Musk, what are the key future systems considerations for this solution?'
