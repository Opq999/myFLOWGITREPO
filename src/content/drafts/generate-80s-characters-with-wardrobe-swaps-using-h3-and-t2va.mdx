## What you'll get
You will generate photorealistic images of characters in an 80s setting, complete with specific wardrobe and environmental details. This approach works by using AI models trained on vast amounts of visual data to interpret detailed text descriptions and create corresponding images, mimicking the look and feel of 1980s film.

## Tools you need
*   **H3** (freemium): A web-based AI image generation tool that allows you to create images from text descriptions.
*   **T2VA** (free): A tool that helps in generating detailed prompts for AI image models, often used to achieve specific visual styles.

## Steps
1.  **Access H3**: Go to the H3 website. You may need to create a free account or log in if you already have one.
    You should see an interface for generating images, likely with a text box for your prompt.
2.  **Prepare your prompt**: The original author used a very detailed prompt. You can adapt this by replacing bracketed information with your specific needs. Here is a starting point based on the author's example:

    ```
    integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, PHOTOREALISTIC film footage - this is footage from a camera, not animation - a continuous camera shot with no cuts, shot on 35mm color negative film with period lenses and scanned in high definition from the original camera negative: full film grain and gentle halation, warm highlight rolloff, rich sharp detail beneath the grain. The year is deep in the LATE 1980s, 1985 to 1989, and everything in the frame belongs to that era. THE PLACE: a nightclub in full swing - mirror-ball light sweeping, neon signage, haze, a crowded dance floor. EXACTLY TWO WOMEN stand close to the lens at the edge of the floor, filling the frame together, and no one else is foregrounded. ROXY: her face, her eyes and [describe ROXY's specific clothing and hairstyle here, e.g., 'wearing a bright pink power suit with shoulder pads and a voluminous blonde perm']. [Describe SECOND WOMAN's specific clothing and hairstyle here, e.g., 'wearing a black leather jacket over a band t-shirt and a short, spiky haircut'].
    ```
    You should have a detailed text prompt ready to paste.
3.  **Generate the image**: Paste your prepared prompt into the text input area in H3 and click the generate button (this might be labeled 'Generate', 'Create', or similar).
    You should see the AI processing your request and then an image will appear, matching your description.
4.  **Refine and swap wardrobe (optional)**: To change the wardrobe, you would typically modify the descriptive text in your prompt and generate a new image. For example, change "wearing a bright pink power suit" to "wearing a sequined silver dress".
    You will get a new image with the updated wardrobe.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free tier**: H3 offers a free tier, but usage might be limited. T2VA is open-source and free to use.
*   **Common mistake**: Being too vague in your prompt. AI models need specific details to create accurate images. Instead of "80s clothes," specify "acid-wash jeans and a neon windbreaker."
*   **Tip for better results**: Experiment with different descriptive words for lighting, film grain, and camera effects to achieve a more authentic vintage look. Adding details about the specific era within the 80s (e.g., early 80s vs. late 80s) can also influence the outcome.
