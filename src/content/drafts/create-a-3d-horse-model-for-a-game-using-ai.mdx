## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: While the specific AI models mentioned are likely paid, you could explore free or freemium AI tools that offer 3D model generation capabilities, though they may have limitations. Some AI art generators are starting to include 3D model output options.
*   **Common mistake**: The author experienced poor results despite high credit usage. This could be due to an unclear prompt, limitations in the AI's 3D generation capabilities at the time, or issues with the Blender MCP integration. Ensure your prompt is very specific and detailed.
*   **Tip for better results**: Experiment with different AI models and prompt engineering techniques.
