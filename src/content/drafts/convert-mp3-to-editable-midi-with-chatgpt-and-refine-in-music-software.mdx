## What you'll get
You will get an editable MIDI (Musical Instrument Digital Interface) version of an audio track, ready for further composition, remixing, or learning. This approach works by using AI to analyze the complex sounds in an audio file and translate its musical elements into a structured format that music software can understand. It's useful for extracting melodies, harmonies, or rhythms from existing tracks to create new music.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot used to convert your MP3 audio file into a MIDI file.
*   **GarageBand** (free): Music software (a Digital Audio Workstation, or DAW) for Apple devices (Mac, iPhone, iPad) to open, play, and edit the MIDI file. It comes pre-installed or is free to download.
*   **Logic Pro** (paid): Professional music software (DAW) for Apple devices (Mac) for advanced editing and production of the MIDI file. This is an optional, more powerful alternative to GarageBand.

## Steps
1.  **Prepare your audio file**: Make sure you have the MP3 audio track you want to convert ready on your device. The author used an ambient track, but this method can work for various music styles.

2.  **Upload to ChatGPT**: Open your web browser or the ChatGPT app on your phone or computer. Log in if needed. Look for the attachment icon (often a paperclip) and upload your MP3 file.

3.  **Prompt for MIDI conversion**: Once the file is uploaded, ask ChatGPT to convert it into an editable MIDI track. Be specific about the musical details you want to preserve. The author didn't share their exact prompt; a starting point:

    ```
    Convert this audio file into an editable MIDI track. Please include the details that make it special, such as long, overlapping tones, slow swells, subtle pitch movement, and any vibrating sounds. Ensure the output includes controller data (like for knobs and wheels) for these subtle movements.
    ```

    You should see ChatGPT process the request and, after a short time, provide a response that includes a downloadable MIDI file.

4.  **Download the MIDI file**: ChatGPT will offer a link or button to download the generated MIDI file. Click it to save the file to your device.

5.  **Open in GarageBand**: If you are on an Apple device, open GarageBand. You can usually drag and drop the downloaded MIDI file directly into an empty track area in GarageBand. If not, use the 'File' > 'Import' or 'Open' menu option.

    You should see the MIDI notes appear on a new track in GarageBand, representing the music from your original MP3.

6.  **Explore and edit**: Play the track in GarageBand. You can now see and edit individual notes, adjust their timing, pitch, and duration. The author noted that even subtle movements and controller data (information that controls effects like volume swells or pitch bends) were present, allowing for detailed editing.

7.  **Extend and develop (optional)**: If you want to create a longer or more developed version of the track, you can go back to ChatGPT and ask it to expand on the MIDI. The author asked for a much longer version with new musical ideas:

    ```
    Now, create a 10-hour, 10-minute version of this MIDI track with new melodic development and an actual progression. Keep the original's mixture of melancholy, memory, and hope.
    ```

    ChatGPT will generate a new, longer MIDI file based on your instructions, which you can then download and open in your music software.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives for music editing**: If you don't have an Apple device, you can use other free Digital Audio Workstations (DAWs) to edit MIDI files. Popular options include **Cakewalk by BandLab** (free for Windows) or **LMMS** (free for Windows, macOS, and Linux). There are also various online MIDI editors that can handle basic tasks.
*   **Common mistake**: Don't expect a perfect, note-for-note transcription every time, especially for very complex, noisy, or non-traditional music. AI models are good, but sometimes they interpret sounds differently. Be prepared to make manual adjustments in your music software.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be as specific as possible in your prompt to ChatGPT. Describe the instruments you hear, the tempo, the mood, and any unique musical elements you want to ensure are captured in the MIDI. If the first attempt isn't quite right, describe what's missing or incorrect to ChatGPT and ask it to refine the MIDI output.
