## What you'll get
You will get a detailed, step-by-step camera and scene plan for an AI video model, designed to ensure the video ends on a specific, desired final frame. This approach works by designing the video backward from the desired conclusion, making the AI's task clearer and helping to avoid common issues like sudden changes or flashes at the end of AI-generated videos.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): To act as your AI shot planner, helping you design the camera movements and scene details from the end goal backward.

## Steps
1.  **Define your final frame**: List all the elements that *must* be true in the very last second of your video. Think about the subject's position, gaze, expression, background, camera height, and viewing angle. Paste this into ChatGPT:

    ```
    I am planning a short video using an AI video generator. My goal is to achieve a very specific final frame. I need you to help me plan the camera movements and scene details leading up to this final frame.

    Here are the exact conditions for the final frame:
    - Subject: A young girl, positioned slightly to the left of the frame.
    - Gaze: Looking directly at the camera.
    - Expression: A mischievous smirk, almost a half-smile.
    - Background: A burning house, clearly visible behind her, slightly out of focus but recognizable.
    - Foreground/Background Order: The girl is clearly in front of the burning house.
    - Camera Height: Eye-level with the girl.
    - Viewing Axis: Straight on, no extreme angles.

    Please confirm you understand these constraints for the final frame.
    ```

    You should get a response from ChatGPT acknowledging your constraints and confirming it understands, ready for the next step.

2.  **Ask for a detailed shot plan**: Instruct ChatGPT to turn these final-frame requirements into a detailed shot plan. Emphasize explicit camera movement, foreground events, exposure changes, and stopping behavior. Paste this into ChatGPT:

    ```
    Based on the final frame constraints I provided, please generate a shot plan for a 5-second video. The plan should describe the camera movement, foreground events, exposure changes, and how the video should smoothly arrive at the exact final frame.

    Focus on explicit, actionable descriptions rather than vague terms like "cinematic" or "dynamic." I need a step-by-step description of what should happen in each second, ensuring the video ends precisely on the specified final frame.
    ```

    You should receive a detailed, second-by-second or keyframe-by-keyframe plan describing the camera's path, subject's actions, and scene elements. This plan is designed to lead precisely to your defined final frame and can then be used to guide your prompts for an AI video generator.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Other freemium chat AIs like Google Gemini (gemini.google.com) or Claude (claude.ai) can also be used for this planning task, as the core capability is text generation and logical reasoning.
*   **Common mistake**: Avoid using vague adjectives (like "dynamic" or "cinematic") when defining your final frame or asking for the shot plan. Be as specific as possible about concrete actions and visual states to give the AI clear instructions.
*   **Tip for better results**: If your final frame is very intricate, break down complex scenes into smaller, more manageable constraints. Define the most critical elements first, then add more details in follow-up prompts to refine the plan.
