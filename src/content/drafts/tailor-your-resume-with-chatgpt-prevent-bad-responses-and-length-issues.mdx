## What you'll get
You will get a resume section that is specifically tailored to a job description, without the AI adding unnecessary length, rephrasing good points poorly, or merging unrelated information. This approach helps prevent 'prompt drift,' a common issue where an AI's responses become less accurate or helpful over time in a conversation, by giving it clear boundaries.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot to help you rewrite parts of your resume to match job descriptions.
*   **AIPRM** (freemium): (Optional) A browser extension that provides pre-made prompts for ChatGPT. The original author experienced similar issues with AIPRM's resume prompts and found the same solution.

## Steps
1.  **Open ChatGPT and start a new chat**: Go to the ChatGPT website and click 'New chat' to begin a fresh conversation.
    You should see a blank chat window ready for your input.

2.  **Prepare your resume section and job description**: Have the specific section of your resume you want to tailor (for example, your 'Experience' section or 'Skills' section) and the full job description for the role you are applying for ready to copy and paste.
    You should have both texts easily accessible.

3.  **Tailor a resume section with specific constraints**: Paste your resume section and the job description into the chat. Crucially, add the author's discovered instructions to prevent bad responses and control length. The author did not share their exact initial prompt; a good starting point is below:

    ```
    Rewrite the following resume section to better match the job description provided. Focus on highlighting relevant skills and experiences. Make sure the language is professional and concise. Do not invent new experiences. 

    Resume Section:
    [Paste your resume section here]

    Job Description:
    [Paste the job description here]

    Limit the resume length to 2 pages. Do not unnecessarily reword already good points, merge different points, or delete important points.
    ```

    You should receive a rewritten version of your resume section that is more focused on the job description, without being overly long or containing irrelevant changes.

4.  **Review and refine**: Carefully read the AI's output. Check if it accurately reflects your experience, matches the job description, and adheres to the length and content constraints. You may need to make minor manual edits or ask the AI to refine specific parts if needed.
    You should have a polished resume section ready to be integrated into your full resume.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: If ChatGPT's free tier isn't sufficient or you prefer other options, you can try similar freemium AI chatbots like **Claude.ai** (https://claude.ai) or **Gemini** (https://gemini.google.com). The core principle of adding specific constraints to your prompt should still apply.
*   **Common pitfall**: The most common mistake is not including clear, specific constraints in your prompt. Without instructions like 'Limit the resume length to 2 pages' or 'Do not unnecessarily reword already good points,' AI models can generate overly long, generic, or unhelpful responses, even if you start a new chat. Always add these constraints, especially if you notice the quality of responses declining.
*   **Tip for better results**: Instead of trying to tailor your entire resume at once, break it down into smaller, manageable sections (for example, 'Summary,' 'Experience,' 'Skills'). Tailor each section individually using the prompt above. This helps the AI focus and reduces the chance of errors, making the process more efficient and the results more accurate. The 'Limit the resume length to 2 pages' instruction, while seemingly for the whole resume, helps the AI understand the overall goal for conciseness even when working on individual sections.
