## What you'll get
You will get a dynamic 15-second motion graphics promo video for your website, complete with your logo colors and on-screen text. This approach works by guiding Claude to act like a professional motion designer, using principles like a 'showreel' for varied transitions and a 'star' element to create a cohesive visual flow across different text lines.

## Tools you need
*   **Claude** (paid): An AI chat assistant that, with a Pro plan, can generate motion graphics videos from text prompts.

## Steps
1.  **Access Claude Pro**: Open your web browser or Claude app and log into your Claude account. Ensure you have an active Pro plan subscription, as this feature is not available on the free tier.
    *   You should be logged into your Claude Pro account and ready to start a new chat.
2.  **Prepare your video details**: Before writing your prompt, gather the following information:
    *   Your business name.
    *   Your website URL.
    *   One specific shape or color from your logo that you want to highlight.
    *   Six short, distinct lines of text that will appear on screen, in the order you want them to show.
    *   You should have all these details written down or ready to copy-paste.
3.  **Craft the video prompt**: In a new Claude chat, paste the following prompt. Replace the bracketed placeholders `[BUSINESS NAME]`, `[WEBSITE]`, `[ONE SHAPE OR COLOR FROM YOUR LOGO]`, `[OPENING LINE]`, `[THING YOU DO 1]`, `[THING YOU DO 2]`, `[THING YOU DO 3]`, `[YOUR OFFER]`, and `[TAGLINE AND WEBSITE]` with your specific details.

    ```
    Make a dynamic 15-second motion graphics video that shows what an incredible motion designer you are, like it's your showreel. go all out. make it for [BUSINESS NAME] ([WEBSITE]). The [ONE SHAPE OR COLOR FROM YOUR LOGO] is the star: it leads every shot, and the eye follows it from scene to scene. Use the words exactly as written: "[OPENING LINE]" "[THING YOU DO 1]" "[THING YOU DO 2]" "[THING YOU DO 3]" "[YOUR OFFER]" "[TAGLINE AND WEBSITE]" The six quoted lines are the words that show up on screen, in order.
    ```

    *   Claude will begin processing your request to generate the video. This may take up to 30 minutes.
4.  **Receive and download the video**: Once Claude finishes processing, it will present the generated video in the chat.
    *   You will see a 15-second MP4 video file in your chat, which you can then download to your device.

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: The original author explicitly states this workflow requires the Claude Pro plan. There is no free tier alternative mentioned for generating videos directly within Claude's chat interface.
*   **Common mistake**: Not making the "star" element clear or consistent. If your chosen shape or color isn't distinct or doesn't naturally lead the eye, the video might feel disjointed. Ensure your six lines of text are concise and impactful for a 15-second video.
*   **Tip to get better results**: Experiment with different descriptions for the "star" element. For example, instead of just "blue," try "a vibrant blue circle" or "the swoosh shape from my logo." Also, try varying the tone of "go all out" with phrases like "make it sleek and modern" or "give it a playful, energetic feel" to guide Claude's style.
