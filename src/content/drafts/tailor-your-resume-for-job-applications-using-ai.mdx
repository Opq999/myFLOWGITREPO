## What you'll get
You will get a version of your resume specifically tailored to a job description you provide. This approach helps your resume stand out by highlighting the most relevant skills and experiences for each application, increasing your chances of getting an interview.

## Tools you need
*   **ChatGPT** (freemium): An AI chatbot that can understand and generate human-like text, used here to rewrite and customize your resume.
*   **Google Docs** (free): A free online word processor to store and edit your main resume, and to save the AI-generated tailored versions. Any text editor or word processor will work.

## Steps
1.  **Open your AI chatbot**: Go to the ChatGPT website on your phone or computer. If you don't have an account, you will need to sign up for a free one.
    You should see the chat interface, ready for your input.
2.  **Prepare your resume**: Open your main resume in Google Docs or your preferred word processor. Copy the entire text content of your resume to your clipboard. Make sure it's up-to-date.
    You should have your full resume text ready to paste.
3.  **Find a job description**: Browse job sites like LinkedIn, Indeed, or Glassdoor to find a job you want to apply for. Copy the entire text of the job description to your clipboard.
    You should have the full job description text ready to paste.
4.  **Tailor your resume with AI**: Paste the following prompt into the ChatGPT chat box. Replace `[Paste the full job description here]` with the job description you copied, and `[Paste your entire current resume here]` with your resume text.

    The author didn't share their exact prompt; a starting point:

    ```
        I need help tailoring my resume for a specific job application.
    Please review the job description below and then rewrite my resume to highlight the most relevant skills and experiences.
    Ensure the tailored resume is strictly under 2 pages.

    ---
    **Job Description:**
    [Paste the full job description here]
    ---

    **My Current Resume:**
    [Paste your entire current resume here]
    ```

    You should see the AI processing your request, then generating a new version of your resume.
5.  **Review and save the tailored resume**: Carefully read through the AI-generated resume. Check for accuracy, grammar, and ensure it highlights the most important parts of your experience for that specific job. Make any necessary manual edits.
    Once satisfied, copy the tailored resume and paste it into a new Google Docs document. Save it with a clear name, like "[Job Title] - [Company Name] - Tailored Resume."

## Notes & variations
*   **Free-tier alternatives**: Instead of ChatGPT, you can use other freemium AI chatbots like Claude (claude.ai) or Gemini (gemini.google.com) for similar results. The prompts would work in any of these tools.
*   **Common mistake**: Do not submit the AI-generated resume without reviewing it first. AI can sometimes "hallucinate" (make up information) or misinterpret parts of your resume or the job description. Always double-check for accuracy and ensure it meets the page limit.
*   **Tip for better results**: Be specific in your prompt. If there are particular skills or projects you want to emphasize, add a line like "Please make sure to highlight my experience with [specific skill] and [specific project]." This guides the AI more effectively.
